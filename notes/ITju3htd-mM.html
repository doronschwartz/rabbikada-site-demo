Topic: Haircuts and laundry in the week of Tisha BeAv

Sources: The Gemara; Rambam; Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: The Three Weeks, Tuesday 6 August 2024

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Recap: in the three weeks Sepharadim avoid dangerous activities, Shehiyanu, dancing and music; in the nine days leisure, weddings and non-pressing construction, and meat and wine (Sepharadim eat meat on Rosh Hodesh itself).
  • Tisha BeAv falls only on Shabbat (postponed to Sunday), Sunday, Tuesday or Thursday. When it falls on Sunday there is no week of Tisha BeAv; this year, on Tuesday, the Sunday and Monday carry added restrictions.
  • There are three: haircuts and laundry, from the Gemara, and bathing and showering, a minhag.
  • Haircuts and shaving are forbidden from Saturday night until after Tisha BeAv (one may still cut on Friday afternoon). The accepted minhag extends this to women, and it applies to children too. At a Berit Milah the father, the Mohel and the Sandak may have a haircut.
  • One may not launder, even on Sunday for fresh clothes on Wednesday, and may not wear freshly laundered clothes; this is din of the Gemara, in the Rambam and Shulhan Arukh. One wears the clothes of the previous week or of Shabbat.
  • Underwear is hygiene and is not included; even on Tisha BeAv one may wash away dirt.
  • A later leniency: wear the shirts for these days beforehand (on Shabbat, or Thursday or Friday for an hour or two) so that they are no longer fresh. Strictly, one should wear the same clothes from Shabbat until after Tisha BeAv.

In one line

In the week of Tisha BeAv the Gemara forbids haircuts, shaving, laundering and wearing freshly laundered clothes, except underwear.
