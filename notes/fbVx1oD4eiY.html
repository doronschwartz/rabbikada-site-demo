Topic: Whether sun cream and other lotions may be applied on Shabbat

Sources: The Mishnah; Hakham Ovadiah

Daily Halacha: Shabbat, Wednesday 16 June 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • One of the 39 melakhot is Memareah: smoothing and spreading a pliable substance into shape. The Mishnah's example is spreading wax to seal a hole in a barrel, forbidden by the Torah.
  • Seemingly any lotion spread on Shabbat would be included.
  • But the prohibition applies only when one wants the spread substance to remain, like the wax over the hole, not when one wants it absorbed and gone.
  • So Hakham Ovadiah rules that any cream, lotion or Vaseline rubbed into the skin to be absorbed, not to form a layer, may be used on Shabbat.
  • One must rub it in before adding more, and not spread a thick layer over the hands.
  • Sun cream may therefore be used for protection on Shabbat, provided it is rubbed in and absorbed.

In one line

Sun cream is permitted on Shabbat when rubbed into the skin rather than left as a layer.
