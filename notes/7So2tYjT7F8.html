Topic: All melakhah for food is permitted on Hol HaMoed, and how this differs from Yom Tov

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Wednesday 17 September 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Work is restricted on Hol HaMoed so that one can rejoice in the festival. Food brings happiness, so the Hakhamim permitted all melakhah for food for the moed, even with great tirha.
  • First difference from Yom Tov: on Yom Tov certain melakhot, generally industrial ones, are forbidden even for food. On Hol HaMoed even industrial and professional work (maaseh uman) for food is permitted: shehitah, catching fish or animals, packaging and transporting food.
  • Second difference: on Hol HaMoed one may cook on one day for another, even for the final Yom Tov and for a Shabbat immediately following it (for example Hol HaMoed Monday to Wednesday, Yom Tov Thursday and Friday, then Shabbat). On Yom Tov one may only cook for that day.
  • Leniency: one need not have prepared before the festival what could have been done then (cutting fruit, hunting), even if it would make no difference to the food.
  • One may cook generously, as much as might be needed even if unlikely, without rationing.
  • Caution: no haaramah. One may not cook extra while claiming, to oneself or others, it is for the moed when really intending it for afterwards.

In one line

Any melakhah for food is permitted on Hol HaMoed, for any day of the festival and the Shabbat after it, provided the food is genuinely meant for then.
