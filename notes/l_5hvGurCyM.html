Topic: An egg donated by a single Jewish woman to a married Jewish woman

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer, Thursday 16 July 2026

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Whether the halachic mother is the egg donor or the carrier is a great doubt, and we try to accommodate both opinions as far as feasible.
  • The case: a wife with no viable egg who can carry receives the egg of a single Jewish woman, fertilised with her husband's sperm.
  • There is no question of mamzerut, since the donor is single.
  • There is a concern of siblings marrying: the donor may later have her own children, who could unknowingly marry the child born from her egg, sharing the same mother in terms of the egg.
  • In Israel a 2010 law requires egg donations to be recorded in the marriage registry and checked before a wedding, which solves the issue there. Sperm donations remain anonymous.
  • Outside Israel, at least in England, egg donation is anonymous, so the issue remains; it is worth trying to verify information about the donor. With a non-Jewish donor this concern is unlikely.

In one line

An egg from a single Jewish donor raises no mamzerut, only a sibling concern, which Israel's registry solves.
