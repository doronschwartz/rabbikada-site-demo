Topic: Work for bodily needs (sorkhei haguf) on Hol HaMoed: heating, air conditioning, make-up, showers and phones

Sources: Rav Moshe Feinstein; Rav Shelomoh Zalman Auerbach; Rav Melamed

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Friday 19 September 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Like food, anything relating to the human condition and the body may be done on Hol HaMoed, by oneself or a professional.
  • If the heating or boiler breaks (in England Pesah can be cold), one may call a professional straight after Yom Tov. The same applies to plumbing or a broken window, since one's house is part of one's identity.
  • Rav Moshe Feinstein writes the same of air conditioning in hot countries, where it is essential.
  • A woman may go to a professional make-up artist, paying for it, for an event on Hol HaMoed, as it relates to her body itself. Clothes and shoes are a separate topic.
  • It was once written that a private shower may not be fixed on Hol HaMoed, but times have changed: most people cannot cope without one, so it is part of bodily integrity and may be fixed.
  • A phone: Rav Shelomoh Zalman Auerbach, about 30 to 40 years ago, held it is not sorkhei haguf. Rav Melamed suggests that today perhaps it is, and many would agree.
  • If one has another phone, move the SIM card. Otherwise, if it breaks a few hours before the end of the moed, do not fix it; with the moed still ahead, there is strong ground to have it professionally fixed, and many would argue it is permitted.

Sources quoted

  • Rav Moshe Feinstein: air conditioning may be fixed.
  • Rav Shelomoh Zalman Auerbach: a phone is not sorkhei haguf.
  • Rav Melamed: today a phone may be sorkhei haguf.

In one line

Repairs for bodily needs, from heating and showers to make-up and, with the moed still ahead, a phone, are permitted on Hol HaMoed even by a professional.
