Topic: Who is exempt from the 17th of Tammuz, and what the fast forbids

Sources: The Prophet Zekhariah; Hakham Ovadiah

Daily Halacha: The Three Weeks, Thursday 24 June 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The fast is for all of Israel: men and women are equally obligated; children under Bar or Bat Misvah are exempt.
  • For Sepharadim a pregnant woman is exempt from the start, without waiting to see how she feels (Tisha BeAv is different). A nursing woman is exempt, most likely meaning one actually feeding.
  • Hakham Ovadiah held that a woman within 24 months of giving birth is exempt even if not feeding, as her body is still frail; many disagreed. It would seem a woman who finds fasting difficult within two years of birth could rely on this.
  • An exceptionally old person (zaken mufla) is exempt even without symptoms; no age is given, and an elderly person who finds it difficult is certainly exempt.
  • A holeh, one who is ill to the point of not functioning (bedridden), is exempt even with no danger to life. A minor headache or aches do not qualify.
  • Only eating and drinking are forbidden. Showering is permitted, and Sepharadim, who may cut hair during the Three Weeks, may do so even on the 17th of Tammuz.

Sources quoted

  • Zekhariah: the fast is for the whole house of Israel.
  • Hakham Ovadiah: a woman within two years of birth is exempt even if not nursing.

In one line

Pregnant and nursing women, the very old and the genuinely ill are exempt, and the fast forbids only eating and drinking.
