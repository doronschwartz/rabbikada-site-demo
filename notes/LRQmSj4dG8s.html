Topic: Preparing the table, when the Seder may begin, and the two functions of the first cup

Sources: Shulhan Arukh; the Kabbalists

Daily Halacha: Pesah, Monday 20 March 2023

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Shulhan Arukh opens the Seder laws by saying the table and everyone's place should be ready well in advance, so the Seder can begin as soon as one returns from synagogue.
  • The Seder begins only after nightfall. Eating massah and telling the Haggadah are the two biblical misvot unique to Pesah night and must be at night; massah is compared (hukash) to the Korban Pesah, which was eaten at night.
  • Normally Kiddush may be made early, after Pelag HaMinhah. But since Kiddush is one of the four cups and is essentially for the massah, the Shulhan Arukh rules it is said after nightfall, in London this year about 8:23.
  • If there is no alternative, for health or similar reasons, there is room to start after sunset (about 7:30), provided Maggid begins after nightfall. In the first instance, wait for nightfall.
  • Many Sepharadim recite the simanim (Kaddesh, Urhas, Karpas, Yahas), a custom from the Kabbalists not mentioned in the halacha.
  • As regular Kiddush, hearing it would suffice. As one of the four cups, each person must drink to experience freedom. Even one who dislikes wine or grape juice must drink, according to the Shulhan Arukh, unless medically unable.
  • Drink a reviit, about 86 ml (just under half a regular plastic cup), in the first instance; bediavad a cheekful, about 44 ml.

Sources quoted

  • Shulhan Arukh: prepare the table in advance; Kiddush only after nightfall; one must drink even if one dislikes the taste.
  • The Kabbalists: the custom of reciting the simanim.

In one line

Have everything ready, make Kiddush after nightfall (or after sunset only if necessary), and drink a reviit yourself as one of the four cups.
