The halacha

  • Last week concluded that, times having changed, women should today be encouraged to learn as much Torah as possible.
  • The Gemara in Kiddushin says one should divide one's days into thirds: Mikra, Mishnah and Talmud.
  • Rashi: divide the week (two days each, the seventh split three ways). Rambam: divide each day's study time.
  • Mikra is all 24 books of Tanakh.
  • Rashi seems to read Mishnah as the Mishnah of Rabbi Yehudah HaNasi and Talmud as the Babylonian Talmud.
  • Rambam reads Mishnah as the whole Oral Torah, which his introduction to the Mishneh Torah defines as everything from the Tannaim to Rav Ashi, Mishnah and Gemara together: the facts of the law.
  • His third part is Talmud, not Gemara as some editions misprint (the Oxford manuscript reads Talmud): inferring and comparing concepts until one grasps the principles on which halacha rests, the hardest level.
  • How these approaches shaped Torah study today comes tomorrow.
