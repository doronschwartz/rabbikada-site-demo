Topic: Business, shopping and contracts on Hol HaMoed

Sources: The Gemara; Rambam; Shulhan Arukh; the Rishonim

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Friday 31 October 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Gemara states clearly that business (perakmatia) is forbidden on Hol HaMoed; this is codified in Rambam and the Shulhan Arukh and agreed by all. Employees are a separate topic, for later.
  • So buying and selling one's own stocks and shares is forbidden, unless they are about to plummet, which would be davar haaved.
  • Ordering on Amazon for use after the moed is forbidden, even if only a click, and whether it arrives during or after the moed. Buying something needed on the moed is different.
  • Entering contracts, such as a rental or a house sale, is forbidden by default, unless the deal may fall through if not done now.
  • The Rishonim give reasons: disrespect to the moed, which is for elevated things; upset over a bad deal detracting from simhah; and buying and selling has no end and can take over the day.
  • So a family outing to browse a shopping centre is inappropriate. Buying specific needed items was discussed earlier.
  • Food is the great exception: no limit on buying or selling food for the moed, only not specifically for after. Picking up things for afterwards during the weekly shop is fine, as part of one purchase.

Sources quoted

In one line

Business, browsing and buying for after the moed are forbidden on Hol HaMoed, except food and items needed for the moed or to prevent a loss.
