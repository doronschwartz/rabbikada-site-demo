Topic: The five Amidot and the Viddui as the essential parts of Yom Kippur

Sources: The Ramban

Daily Halacha: Yom Kippur, Thursday 21 September 2023

The halacha

  • The Hakhamim instituted five Amidot on Yom Kippur, four by day and one at night, each with Viddui. These, not the Selihot and Piyyutim, are the focus of the day.
  • In the Temple the Kohen Gadol confessed for all over the goat sent to Azazel; after the destruction the Hakhamim had each person confess himself.
  • Viddui is said 11 times: eight in the silent and repeated Amidot of the day, in Arvit, in the Selihot after Arvit, and at Minhah of Erev Yom Kippur, before the final meal, in case one chokes or does not reach Yom Kippur.
  • The ten key ones are those within an Amidah (excluding the one after Arvit), matching the ten times the Kohen Gadol said the Shem HaMeforash.
  • Women with children, or the unwell, should at least pray the five Amidot with Viddui; failing that, at the very least confess as Yom Kippur begins and at Neilah.
  • The Ramban held one also confesses as Yom Kippur enters, hence Lekha Eli Teshukati before Kol Nidre for Sepharadim and Tefillah Zakkah for Ashkenazim. This is an extra.
  • Viddui is said standing unless ill or very weak; bediavad sitting is valid, though some say not. Sepharadim may sit for much of the repetition but should stand for the Hazan's Viddui.
  • Bowing the head is a nice optional custom. Most strike the heart; the Yemenites and the Spanish and Portuguese do not. Standing and understanding the words are what matter.
  • The core is "Aval anahnu vaavotenu hatanu": Het (unintentional), Avon (intentional), Pesha (rebellious), in rising severity.

In one line

Focus on the five Amidot with their Viddui, said standing, and at the very least confess at the start of the fast and at Neilah.
