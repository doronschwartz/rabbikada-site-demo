Topic: Whether one should bake hallot at home for Shabbat or may buy them

Sources: The Rama (laws of Shabbat), quoting the Mordekhai; the Mishnah Berurah; Hazon Ovadiah; Rav Ben Sion Abba Shaul

Daily Halacha: Hallah, Thursday 30 June 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Hallah is a misvah kiyumit: there is no obligation to bake, as with shehitah; only if one bakes must one separate.
  • Yet the Rama, quoting the Mordekhai, writes it is customary to bake hallot at home on Friday in honour of Shabbat and Yom Tov, so as to do the misvah of separating Hallah.
  • The Mishnah Berurah (Rav Yisrael Meir HaKohen of Radin), about 100 years ago, wrote sharply against buying hallot from a bakery, losing a beautiful custom with a source in the Torah's command to prepare before Shabbat.
  • Hazon Ovadiah agreed: in the first instance one should try to bake one's own.
  • Rav Ben Sion Abba Shaul held that today bakeries make special Shabbat hallot, clearly distinct from ordinary bread, so buying them honours Shabbat just as baking does, even in the first instance.
  • Doing something for Shabbat oneself is valuable. If it is easy, baking at home is a wonderful thing; if it causes stress that affects Shabbat, there is what to rely on in buying them. Either way Shabbat is honoured.

Sources quoted

  • Rama and Mordekhai: the custom to bake hallot at home for Shabbat.
  • Mishnah Berurah and Hazon Ovadiah: preferably bake rather than buy.
  • Rav Ben Sion Abba Shaul: bought Shabbat hallot are equally fine.

In one line

Baking hallot at home is a lovely custom, but where it causes stress one may rely on buying special Shabbat hallot.
