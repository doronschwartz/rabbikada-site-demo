Topic: Oneg Yom Tov: festive meals with bread, and forgetting Yaaleh VeYavo in Birkat HaMazon

Sources: The Torah; Parashat Beshalah; the Rambam; the Rosh; Shulhan Arukh; Tosafot; the Ben Ish Hai and Sepharadi poskim

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Thursday 15 May 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The festival has two aspects; the one shared with Shabbat is oneg, delighting in the day through eating, drinking and fine clothes. The Rambam and Shulhan Arukh rule there is the same misvah of oneg on Yom Tov as on Shabbat. Simhah, rejoicing, is a separate din for next time.
  • Three meals are unique to Shabbat, learnt from Parashat Beshalah. On Yom Tov the Shulhan Arukh rules two meals suffice, but they should be festive and extravagant, unlike a weekday.
  • Most Rishonim, the Rambam, the Rosh and the Shulhan Arukh, require these meals with bread: one washes, eats a full seudah and says Birkat HaMazon. Tosafot hold oneg on Yom Tov does not require bread. The Shulhan Arukh rules clearly that one must have bread, so one should make the effort.
  • The ramification: on Shabbat, one who forgets to mention the day repeats Birkat HaMazon on Friday night and Shabbat morning, since the meals are obligatory. Accordingly the Shulhan Arukh rules that one who forgets Yaaleh VeYavo at a Yom Tov meal repeats it, and so rule the Ashkenazim.
  • But because of safek berakhot lehakel, and since by Tosafot there is no obligation of a meal on Yom Tov, the Ben Ish Hai and many Sepharadi poskim rule not to repeat. This is the Sepharadi practice.
  • The exception is the first night of Pesah and of Sukkot (and outside Israel the second nights too), when there is an obligation to eat massah or eat in the sukkah; then one goes back.

Sources quoted

  • Shulhan Arukh: two festive meals with bread; repeat Birkat HaMazon if Yaaleh VeYavo was forgotten.
  • Tosafot: no obligation of bread on Yom Tov.
  • The Ben Ish Hai: Sepharadim do not repeat, apart from the first nights.

In one line

Delight in Yom Tov with two festive bread meals, but Sepharadim who forget Yaaleh VeYavo repeat Birkat HaMazon only on the first nights of Pesah and Sukkot.
