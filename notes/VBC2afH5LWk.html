Topic: Medical treatment on Hol HaMoed, and when it may be scheduled for then

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Thursday 25 September 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Anything for health (refuat haguf) is permitted on Hol HaMoed without restriction: one may pay, use the best professional and do any melakhah the treatment needs.
  • The distinction from food applies: food may be deliberately left for the moed, but makhshirei okhel nefesh may not. Likewise, treatment for pain or illness may be done even if one put it off, being too busy.
  • But upkeep where there is no pain and no damage should not be deliberately scheduled on Hol HaMoed: a routine dental check-up, a hygienist, ongoing orthodontic treatment that could be the week before or after.
  • If a brace snaps on Yom Tov and hurts, one may go to the orthodontist on Hol HaMoed.
  • If it is very hard to get an appointment, for example an NHS hospital appointment that falls on Hol HaMoed and would take months to rebook, one may attend and do any melakhah involved, even without pain. (On Yom Tov itself it would need rescheduling.)
  • Broken glasses may be fixed professionally and paid for, since the eyes and glasses are an extension of the body; but a routine eye test should not be scheduled for Hol HaMoed.

In one line

Treatment for pain or illness, or an appointment that is hard to get, may be done on Hol HaMoed, but routine check-ups should not be scheduled then.
