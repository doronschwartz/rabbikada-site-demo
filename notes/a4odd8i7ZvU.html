Topic: Rosh Hashanah as the conception of the year, for the individual and the nation

Sources: Talmud Yerushalmi

Daily Halacha: Rosh Hashanah, Monday 22 September 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The points

  • The custom not to sleep by day on Rosh Hashanah goes back to the Talmud Yerushalmi: one who sleeps on Rosh Hashanah will not have a good year.
  • The principle behind it, taught by the Hakhamim, is that everything follows the beginning. A beginning is a conception, laying down the genes of all that follows.
  • Physically, the closer to conception, the more critical any harm: a small injury means little to an adult, but a tiny change to a developing fetus can have far reaching results. So too spiritually, a flaw at the conception of the year's identity and mindset can be critical; the rest of the year reveals what was formed then.
  • So we take on extra care on Rosh Hashanah, and it is not hypocritical, just as a pregnant woman takes care that she may not take at other times. We stay awake to lay down the year's genes in consciousness, not oblivion.
  • Individually, we ask the questions we neglect all year: the purpose of life, and whether we are progressing intellectually, morally and in character.
  • Equally, and this is the stress of the Tefillot, we consider our identity as a nation under HaKadosh Barukh Hu: that His unity and sovereignty be recognized, Am Yisrael lead the world forward, and the world reach its final perfection (Tikkun HaKelali), living together in peace.
  • We also ask what our personal identity can contribute to that national picture.

In one line

Rosh Hashanah is the conception of the year, so we stay awake to shape who we are, as individuals and as a nation under Hashem.
