Topic: How the melakhah of borer (selecting) differs between Shabbat and Yom Tov

Sources: The Rema

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Thursday 19 June 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • On Shabbat selecting from a mixture is forbidden (for food and, by the halacha, non-food) unless three conditions are met: the wanted from the unwanted (okhel mitokh pesolet), for immediate use (about the next half hour), and by hand or ordinary cutlery, not a designated utensil.
  • On Yom Tov the first two conditions do not apply; the third does. This concerns household selecting; industrial-scale borer, as in the Mishkan after threshing, remains forbidden.
  • One may take the unwanted from the wanted when easier: removing the few almonds from a bowl that is mostly pistachios, picking lemon pips straight out of a salad, removing knives from a pile of forks.
  • This is certainly permitted for Sepharadim and generally for Ashkenazim too; the Rema cites those who are strict specifically with flour.
  • Removing bones from fish or chicken is certainly permitted on Yom Tov (some poskim permit it even on Shabbat, a separate discussion).
  • One may select for later in the day on Yom Tov, not only for immediate use.
  • Still forbidden: a designated instrument such as a strainer or sieve, which the Hakhamim forbade as looking industrial. Hand or ordinary cutlery only.

In one line

On Yom Tov one may select the unwanted from the wanted and for later in the day, but only by hand or ordinary cutlery, never with a sieve or strainer.
