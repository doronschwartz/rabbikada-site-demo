Topic: Is the misvah of having children fulfilled through IVF?

Sources: The Gemara (a woman who conceived in a bathhouse); Likkutei Maharil (Ben Sira); a commentary on the Shulhan Arukh from Lithuania; Rav Moshe Feinstein; the Sis Eliezer (Rav Eliezer Waldenberg)

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer, Thursday 22 January 2026

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Gemara knows of a woman conceiving without relations ("in the bathhouse"); the science does not matter here, but the concept exists. It does not say whether the father fulfils the misvah.
  • Likkutei Maharil (about 600 years ago, of uncertain authority) claims Ben Sira was conceived this way from Yirmiyahu. A Lithuanian commentary on the Shulhan Arukh about 400 years ago inferred that the father fulfils the misvah; others questioned this.
  • It depends on the definition: if the misvah is the result, he fulfils it; if it is the act (Rav Moshe Feinstein's view), seemingly not.
  • Yet Rav Moshe Feinstein, the Sis Eliezer and other contemporary poskim all agree that with IVF he fulfils the misvah: there is a deliberate action by the husband aimed at bringing a child, and a child results.
  • This is accepted by poskim worldwide.

In one line

A man who has a son and a daughter through IVF has fulfilled "Peru urevu" according to all opinions.
