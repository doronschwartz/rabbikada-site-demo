Topic: Oseh Maaseh Bereshit on mountains and deserts

Sources: The Mishnah; Rambam; the Rishonim; Shulhan Arukh; the poskim

Daily Halacha: Peculiar Berachot, Wednesday 5 February 2025

The halacha

  • The Mishnah gives Oseh Maaseh Bereshit on mountains and deserts, said when one has not seen them for 30 days. The Rambam writes "mountains" without distinction.
  • Some Rishonim say the Hakhamim meant breathtaking mountains, very tall or beautiful, that reveal the might of creation. The Shulhan Arukh rules so, despite the Rambam.
  • In practice the poskim say it only on such mountains: the Alps when going skiing, Everest and the Himalayas, Mount Hermon in the north of Israel, the Andes. It is a full berakhah with Hashem's name, an opportunity to thank Him for His world.
  • Deserts likewise: the Mishnah and Rambam just say "deserts", but in practice the poskim say it only on large ones such as the Sahara or Sinai, on travelling in and seeing their vastness, once every 30 days.
  • The 30 days apply to each phenomenon separately. One who sees a breathtaking mountain and the next day a desert says the berakhah again, since each is a different wonder.

In one line

Oseh Maaseh Bereshit is said only on truly breathtaking mountains and vast deserts, each after 30 days.
