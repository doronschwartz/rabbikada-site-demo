Topic: Who is the halachic mother: the egg donor or the woman who carries the child?

Sources: The Gemara and Rashi (Leah and Rachel); Rav Ovadiah Yoseph

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer, Wednesday 15 July 2026

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • If the wife's egg is viable but she cannot carry, the couple's egg and sperm are carried by another woman; if her egg is not viable, a donated egg fertilised with her husband's sperm may be carried by the wife.
  • The question, which Batei Din across the world must deal with: is the mother the woman whose egg it is, or the one who carries it for nine months?
  • Logic cuts both ways: the egg is the original matter; or the egg is mere matter until a woman develops it over nine months.
  • Halachic proofs are not usually brought from midrashim, but there is one from the Gemara that Rashi brings on the Torah: Leah, pregnant with her seventh child, prayed it would be a girl so that Rachel would not have fewer sons. The fetuses were swapped: Leah gave birth to Dinah, and Rachel to Binyamin, who is called Rachel's son. This suggests the carrying decides.
  • Some poskim hold the egg decides; Rav Ovadiah Yoseph held this, the main view. Others hold the carrier decides.
  • In practice it is treated as a doubt. Where one woman is not Jewish, a conversion is often done as a stringency, more relaxed than a regular conversion. In his understanding it is most lenient where the egg is Jewish and the carrier is not, since many poskim follow the egg.
  • Scenarios follow in the coming days.

Sources quoted

In one line

Poskim divide on whether the egg or the pregnancy defines the mother, so a precautionary conversion is often done where one woman is not Jewish.
