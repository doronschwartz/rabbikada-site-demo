Topic: Why no berakhah is said on honouring parents

Sources: The Rashba; Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Honouring Parents, Wednesday 6 January 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The points

  • The Rishonim ask why there is no berakhah when honouring parents, though many misvot have one. The same question applies to giving sedakah.
  • The Rashba explains that no berakhah is said on a misvah that depends on another person, who may refuse it. If a poor man declines the coins, the berakhah would be in vain. Likewise a parent may forgo the honour, and when a parent forgoes it, the child need not honour.
  • A simpler explanation: misvot between people (bein adam lahavero) must be done for the sake of the other person, not for our own spiritual gain. Visiting the sick only to "get a misvah" would mean not caring about the patient.
  • Misvot between us and HaShem are done for His service with a pure heart; misvot between us and others are done out of real care for them. A berakhah would turn the act into something done for oneself.

In one line

We make no berakhah on honouring parents because it depends on the parent's acceptance (the Rashba) and because interpersonal misvot must be done out of genuine care for the other person.
