Topic: When professional work is permitted on Hol HaMoed: food, comfort, health, clothes and writing

Sources: Rav Moshe Feinstein

Daily Halacha: Sukkot, Wednesday 4 October 2023

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Forbidden work on Hol HaMoed is work with tirhah: professional (maaseh uman) or lengthy. But the Hakhamim made exceptions.
  • Food: all work, even professional, is permitted, including field work (unlike Yom Tov) and preparing for other days of the festival. Harvesting, deliveries, bakeries and restaurants are all sorekh hamoed.
  • One may also repair an oven, dishwasher or mixer, even hiring and paying a Jew, if it is needed for the festival and was not deliberately left until then.
  • Bodily comfort: fixing a broken heater, air conditioning or window is permitted if needed during the festival; if it is unlikely to be used, it must wait.
  • Medical needs are permitted; a routine check-up or an operation that could be scheduled at another time is not. Rav Moshe Feinstein discusses sunglasses: not an important need, so not repaired, while regular glasses may be.
  • Clothes: professional repair, such as at a tailor, is not allowed unless one has nothing else to wear. Laundry is for next time.
  • Household items: professional carpet cleaning is not allowed, ordinary cleaning is. Taping a broken chair is maaseh hedyot and permitted; a proper repair is not.
  • Writing: only professional writing is forbidden, such as even one letter of a Sefer Torah, mezuzah or tefillin, or a precise business spreadsheet. A shopping list is maaseh hedyot and permitted.

Sources quoted

  • Rav Moshe Feinstein: sunglasses are not an important need on Hol HaMoed.

In one line

Professional work is permitted on Hol HaMoed only for food, bodily comfort or health needed during the festival; casual work and writing are fine.
