Topic: The better mazal of Adar, "ein mazal leYisrael", and why the joy belongs to Adar

Sources: The Talmud; the Ibn Ezra; the Meiri

Daily Halacha: Purim, Monday 15 February 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The points

  • The Talmud says when Adar begins we increase our joy, so one who can choose should have a court case heard in Adar, when Israel's mazal (fate, destiny, lot) is better. Likewise, appeal a parking ticket in Adar rather than Shevat.
  • This seems to contradict another Talmudic statement, "ein mazal leYisrael": Israel is not under the stars but directly in God's hands.
  • The Ibn Ezra (12th-century Spain): one who lives recognising that only the Almighty controls his life is above mazal, and Adar makes no difference to him. One who lives under the idea of being ruled by this star or that is subject to such influences, and for him Adar helps.
  • So the advice to prefer Adar, and to avoid court cases with non-Jews in Av, the saddest month, applies only to those who live within the concept of mazal.
  • Why Adar and not Nisan or Kislev? The Meiri (Perpignan, 13th century): the Purim miracle came about hidden; the Megillah can be read as coincidences, yet God directed everything for the good of the Jewish people.
  • When we train ourselves to see HaKadosh Barukh Hu in everything, good and bad, even when things look terrible as at the start of the Megillah, we are filled with joy and gratitude. Adar is the month for that training.

Sources quoted

In one line

Adar's joy comes from learning to see God's hidden hand in everything, which also lifts us above mazal.
