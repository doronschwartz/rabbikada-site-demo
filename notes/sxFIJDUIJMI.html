Topic: Bringing in the second night of Shavuot early so the meal may be prepared on the first day

Sources: The Ben Ish Hai; Hakham Ovadiah

Daily Halacha: Shavuot and Talmud Torah, Wednesday 1 June 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Waiting for nightfall this year applies only to the first night of Shavuot. Going into the second night it may even be advisable to pray Arvit and make Kiddush early.
  • Outside Israel the first day of Hag is biblical and the second rabbinic, so one may not prepare on the first day for the second.
  • From Pelag HaMinhah (about an hour or more before sunset in summer) one may bring in the next day early, as with Shabbat.
  • One who makes Kiddush and starts the meal before sunset (for example at 8.30, with sunset after 9.15) is eating on the first day, even though it is the second night's meal. So it is not preparation for the next day, and one may set the table and prepare the food any time on Sunday, morning or afternoon.
  • So, as the Ben Ish Hai and Hakham Ovadiah write, Sepharadim pray Arvit early (from about 7.30), light the Yom Tov candles after Pelag HaMinhah and start the meal before sunset.
  • If the meal will start after sunset, one may not set the table or prepare food until close to nightfall.
  • Some Ashkenazim objected that this detracts from the first day, so some Ashkenazi communities wait for nightfall even on the second night, but many Ashkenazim follow the early practice.
