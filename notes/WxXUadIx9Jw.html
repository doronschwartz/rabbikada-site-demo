Topic: Which places need searching for Hames, and the timing and blessing of the search

Sources: Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Pesah

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • One searches for a Kezayit, but where food will be prepared or eaten on Pesah, such as the kitchen and dining table, one must clean even crumbs.
  • The Shulhan Arukh says any place Hames is brought during the year, even occasionally, must be searched. Bedrooms where fruit is sometimes taken must be checked; where no food is ever brought, they need not be.
  • Places Hames never enters, like a shed, a storehouse (the Shulhan Arukh's example), a bathroom or toilet, need no search, unless there are young children, who take food everywhere; then the whole house is checked. High bedroom shelves children cannot reach, where no Hames is put, need not be checked.
  • A car, an office, and any place one owns food and brings it during the year must be checked.
  • Pockets and handbags where food is put must be checked; if they will be used for food on Pesah, even for crumbs, hence the custom to check pockets for crumbs.
  • The search is at night, since people are busy by day and a candle reaches corners better. If missed, it is done on Erev Pesah by day, and if still missed, during Pesah.
  • If not done before or during Pesah, one must search after Pesah, since Hames owned over Pesah is forbidden in benefit forever and must be disposed of.
  • The blessing is "Al Biur Hames", since the search begins the disposal completed the next day.

Sources quoted

  • Shulhan Arukh: places where Hames is brought must be searched; storehouses where food never goes need not be.

In one line

Search wherever Hames is brought, everywhere if there are young children, at night before Pesah, or later if missed.
