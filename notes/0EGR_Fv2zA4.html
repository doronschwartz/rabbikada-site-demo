Topic: The week of Tisha BeAv when the 9th of Av itself falls on Sunday

Sources: The Gemara; Shulhan Arukh; the Ben Ish Hai

Daily Halacha: The Three Weeks, Monday 12 July 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • In our fixed calendar Tisha BeAv falls only on Shabbat, Sunday, Tuesday or Thursday; on Shabbat it is postponed, so about half the time it is kept on Sunday. It always falls on the same weekday as the first day of Pesah.
  • The Gemara forbids, in the week of Tisha BeAv, haircuts, washing clothes even for after Tisha BeAv, wearing freshly laundered clothes, showering and more.
  • When the 9th of Av is postponed from Shabbat, the Shulhan Arukh brings two opinions on whether the previous week counts. This year the 9th of Av itself falls on Sunday, so all Sepharadi poskim agree there is no week of Tisha BeAv.
  • So this week one may shower, do laundry, wear freshly laundered clothes through Shabbat, and cut hair and shave even until Friday afternoon.
  • On Tisha BeAv itself, changing after Shabbat, one may not wear freshly laundered clothes; wear the Shabbat shirt or clothes already worn.
  • The Ben Ish Hai and others write it is praiseworthy not to cut hair or shave on Friday, so as not to enter Tisha BeAv freshly groomed, but that is a stringency.

In one line

With the 9th of Av on Sunday there is no week of Tisha BeAv, so laundry, showers and haircuts are permitted until Shabbat.
