Topic: When dough divided into smaller portions still requires Hallah with a berakhah

Sources: Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Hallah, Monday 11 July 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The minimum for Hallah with a berakhah is 43.2 eggs, about 1.5 to 1.6 kg.
  • Shulhan Arukh: one who makes a large dough in order to divide it among other people, with no portion reaching the minimum, is not obligated, and no berakhah may be said. For example, 3 kg split among six people at half a kilo each.
  • A large dough divided into small hallot or rolls all for one's own use is still obligated, with a berakhah.
  • Even for oneself, if it is split into portions under the minimum for different purposes (say 1 kg for bread and 1 kg for cakes), there is no obligation or berakhah.
  • At a Hallah bake, whoever says the berakhah for everyone, or each participant, needs the minimum amount. If each woman kneads less than about 1.5 kg, no berakhah or separation is possible.
  • As long as there is a core of 1.6 kg used for one purpose by oneself, one says the berakhah even if the rest is used otherwise.
  • Saying the berakhah when the dough is divided among people or purposes would be wrong.

Sources quoted

  • Shulhan Arukh: dough made to be divided among others into portions below the minimum is exempt.

In one line

Hallah with a berakhah needs at least 1.6 kg kept for one person and one purpose; dough divided among people or uses below that is exempt.
