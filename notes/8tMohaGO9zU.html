Topic: Shemini Aseret and Simhat Torah for visitors, and flying abroad on the second day

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Monday 21 July 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • In Israel Shemini Aseret and Simhat Torah are one day, with Hatan Torah and Hatan Bereshit. A visitor keeping two days may be honoured then: it is Yom Tov for him, and the honours are a minhag of the last thousand years or so.
  • So, as a riddle, a visitor can be Hatan Torah or Hatan Bereshit two days running: on the Israeli Simhat Torah, and again the next day with a minyan of visitors.
  • Outside Israel we sit in the sukkah on Shemini Aseret without the berakhah. A visitor eating with Israelis should not sit in the sukkah alone, which would be poresh min hasibbur; he may rely on their view and eat with them. Alone, with family or in a hotel, he should eat in the sukkah on the eighth day.
  • An Israeli keeping one day may fly abroad on the second day: melakhah is permitted outside a Jewish community, and an airport is not one. He must stay at the airport and not enter the Jewish community until the second day ends. If staying where there is no community, he may travel there, even by taxi.
  • The Three Weeks and Tisha BeAv come next.

In one line

A visitor may be Hatan Torah on both days, skips the sukkah on Shemini Aseret when eating with Israelis, and an Israeli may fly in on the second day if he keeps away from the Jewish community.
