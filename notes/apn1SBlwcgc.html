Topic: Students from abroad studying in Israel: one day or two?

Sources: The Bet Yoseph; Hakham Ovadiah Yoseph; the Hakham Svi

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Monday 14 July 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Bet Yoseph rules clearly that those coming from outside Israel keep two days. The default for the vast majority of poskim: a short visitor who intends to return keeps two.
  • Hakham Ovadiah was asked about people who travel to Israel to study, in yeshivah, seminary or otherwise, for a fixed period, intending to return. In a long teshuvah he holds strongly that they keep one day.
  • His reasons combine: in the background is the view of the Hakham Svi that a visitor keeps one day, which, though not the halacha, can support leniency where there is another doubt; and a single, independent student may well find a spouse and settle in Israel.
  • He caveats this: if there is no openness at all to marrying someone in Israel, or the student depends on parents for flights and support and they would not want this, two days must be kept.
  • A student who is relatively self-sufficient and open (not necessarily wanting, but open) to settling in Israel if the right spouse comes along keeps one day. This is the practice of many Sepharadim.

Sources quoted

  • The Bet Yoseph: visitors from abroad keep two days.
  • Hakham Ovadiah Yoseph: a long teshuvah allowing students in Israel to keep one day, with caveats.
  • The Hakham Svi: the minority view in the background that supports leniency.

In one line

A self-sufficient student in Israel who is open to settling there keeps one day, but one with no such openness or dependent on parents who oppose it keeps two.
