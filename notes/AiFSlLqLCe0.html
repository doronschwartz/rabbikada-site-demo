Topic: Combining small doughs or baked loaves to reach the measure for Hallah

Daily Halacha: Hallah, Tuesday 12 July 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Hallah should be taken at the dough stage, but if a dough of the full measure was forgotten, one may take a small piece after plaiting or baking, with the berakhah.
  • If the small doughs never together reached the measure (about 1.6 kg), there was never an obligation. One can still create one by neshikhah or sseruf kli.
  • Neshikhah: touching the pieces together so they form one larger dough.
  • Sseruf kli: placing them, even not touching, in one basket or utensil, intending them to become one; if the total is 1.6 kg or more, one takes Hallah with a berakhah. Covering them all with one towel also combines them.
  • Combination does not work if one wants them kept apart, such as different flavours; it works for the same grain, taste and kind of bread, even if baked separately.
  • This is common with Massot before Pesah: small doughs avoid Hames, and after baking the Massot, thin or the thicker kind some Sepharadim use, are put together in a basket and Hallah is taken.
  • If there was once a full-measure dough and one forgot, no combining is needed; one simply takes Hallah from the bread.

In one line

Small doughs or loaves that never reached the measure can be combined by touching, one container or one cover, and Hallah then taken with a berakhah.
