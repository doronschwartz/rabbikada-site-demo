Topic: The berakhah on seeing places of miracles for the Jewish people or for famous saddikim

Sources: Mishnah Berakhot; Rambam; Shulhan Arukh; the Midrash; the poskim

Daily Halacha: Peculiar Berachot, Monday 17 February 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Mishnah, codified by the Rambam and Shulhan Arukh, says that one who sees a place where a miracle happened for all Israel blesses "sheasah nissim laavotenu bamakom hazeh".
  • Examples: the crossing of the Red Sea; the crossing of the Jordan in the book of Yehoshua; the stone Moshe sat on in the war with Amalek; the stone Og king of Bashan wanted to throw at Moshe, according to the Midrash.
  • For a miracle done for saddikim known to all Israel, one blesses "sheasah nissim lassaddikim bamakom hazeh", as at the furnace of Hananyah, Mishael and Azaryah or the place Daniel was thrown in.
  • On the pillar of salt of Lot's wife the Hakhamim give two berakhot: Dayan HaEmet, and "Zokher HaSaddikim".
  • It must be the exact place, and today we do not know where these were, such as the exact crossing point of the Red Sea or Jordan, so the berakhah with Hashem's name can no longer be said.
  • The poskim write that near such places (the Red Sea, the Jordan, the walls of Jericho) one may say "Barukh sheasah nissim laavotenu bamakom hazeh" without Hashem's name.
  • This differs from the berakhah on a personal miracle, which still applies to the person and his descendants at that place, to be discussed next.

In one line

The berakhah on public miracle sites is said today only without Hashem's name, since the exact places are unknown.
