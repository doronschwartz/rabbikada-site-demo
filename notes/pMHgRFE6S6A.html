Topic: A woman who remarried on testimony that her husband died, and he returns

Sources: Mishnah Yevamot

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer, Monday 25 May 2026

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • So far: a mamzer from the Torah (from a union where kiddushin cannot take effect, such as adultery or incest) and a safek mamzer (such as a birth more than 12 months after the husband left). Now a third: a rabbinic mamzer, seen in a case that sadly occurred several times after the Holocaust.
  • The Mishnah in Yevamot: a husband goes abroad and is missing for years. She cannot remarry unless he has died.
  • Here we are very lenient about evidence: instead of two witnesses, even one witness, hearsay from a witness (ed mipi ed), or a casual remark (mesiah lefi tumo) that he died. Otherwise she would be chained for the rest of her life.
  • The difficult case: on such testimony she marries man two, and years later man one returns. She was always married to man one, so the second marriage never took effect.
  • She is forbidden to man two, even if man one divorces her. Because we were so lenient in letting her remarry, she must bear the consequences if the testimony was wrong; she had to be certain.
  • She is also forbidden to man one, since she lived with man two when it was in fact prohibited, even though she had a ruling from the Beit Din.
  • This is the Mishnah's "she leaves this one and that one" (tese mizeh umizeh).
  • Next time: the status of the children from both men.

Sources quoted

A woman who remarried on testimony of her husband's death and then saw him return is forbidden to both men.
