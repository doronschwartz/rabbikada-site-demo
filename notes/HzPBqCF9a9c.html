Topic: Honouring grandparents and great-grandparents

Sources: Rashi; the Midrash on Bereshit (Yaakov's offerings to "the God of his father Yishak"); Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Honouring Parents, Tuesday 19 January 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Rashi is cited as holding that the misvah covers only parents, but most poskim and the Shulhan Arukh rule that grandparents must be honoured too.
  • The proof is the Midrash: Yaakov brought offerings to "the God of his father Yishak", not of Avraham, though Avraham was greater, because one owes more honour to a father than to a grandfather. So grandparents are owed honour, but less: in a conflict, parents come first.
  • Why grandparents? Either an independent obligation (they brought your parents into the world), which would extend to great-grandparents and apply even when the parents have died; or it is part of honouring your parents, who must honour their own parents.
  • The final halacha, though disputed, follows the second view: honour grandparents while the connecting parent is alive. If your father has died, the obligation to his parents falls away; your mother's parents remain while she lives.
  • Great-grandparents: no special obligation, since your parents are not obliged in their own grandparents. Respect is still derekh erets.

In one line

Honour your grandparents, after your parents, as part of honouring your parents, so the duty to a grandparent ends with the connecting parent's death and does not reach great-grandparents.
