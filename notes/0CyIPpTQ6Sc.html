Topic: Work on Friday, Mukseh Seder massah, praying Arvit early and setting the Seder table when Erev Pesah is on Shabbat

Daily Halacha: Pesah, Thursday 10 April 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Work is permitted all day Friday, the 13th of Nisan; the usual Erev Pesah restrictions do not apply, only the normal care near Shabbat.
  • Regular massah may be moved on Shabbat, and children below the age of hinukh may be fed massah during the day. But Shemurah massah set aside specifically for the Seder is Mukseh on Shabbat, since it cannot be eaten that day. If one uses the same massah all seven days, this does not arise.
  • Arvit is best prayed at nightfall (Seit HaKokhavim), going straight from Shabbat into Pesah, with Vatodienu as the Havdalah in the Amidah.
  • In London nightfall is after 8:40, which would push the Seder to 9:30 or 10. So there is a leniency, when needed, to pray Arvit from sunset (about 7:50), still saying Vatodienu.
  • One may not prepare on Shabbat for Yom Tov, so the Seder table is set on arriving home at nightfall.
  • Where there is a need, such as many guests or very young children, one may begin setting the table from sunset, during Bein HaShemashot: a rabbinic prohibition is permitted then for a misvah. This allows only setting out plates and cutlery, nothing Mukseh and no melakhah, so the candles may not be prepared or touched.
  • One who must start the Seder before nightfall should consult, as that is beyond this recording.

In one line

Work on Friday is allowed, Seder massah is Mukseh on Shabbat, and when needed one may pray Arvit and set the table from sunset, but no melakhah until Shabbat ends.
