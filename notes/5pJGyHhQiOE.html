Topic: Driving, driving lessons and car washing on Hol HaMoed

Sources: Rav Melamed

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Wednesday 29 October 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Driving, or taking a bus, train or taxi, is melekhet hedyot lesorekh hamoed: to synagogue, shopping or a trip, it is permitted on Hol HaMoed.
  • Driving lessons seemingly are not for the moed, being preparation for a future test. Rav Melamed rules they are not allowed.
  • If a person genuinely enjoys the lessons and it is part of his enjoyment of Hol HaMoed, there is room to permit it even though it also prepares for a test after the moed. He must be honest with himself.
  • Davar haaved permits a lesson: a lesson booked in advance where cancelling would lose the money (but one should not deliberately schedule it for the moed), or a test just after the festival that one would otherwise fail.
  • A full car wash and polish is not a weekly activity, so, like routine dental appointments, it should not be scheduled for Hol HaMoed. Clearing dirt from the windscreen is fine, and a car that became very dirty on a trip during the moed may be cleaned.
  • Fixing cars follows next.

Sources quoted

  • Rav Melamed: driving lessons are not permitted on Hol HaMoed.

In one line

Driving for the moed is permitted, but driving lessons and full car washes are not, unless there is real enjoyment now or a loss to prevent.
