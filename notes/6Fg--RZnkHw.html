Topic: The berakhah on earthquakes and on very strong winds

Sources: The Mishnah; the Gemara and Rishonim; Rambam; the poskim

Daily Halacha: Peculiar Berachot, Monday 10 February 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Mishnah's zevaot are identified by the Gemara and Rishonim as earthquakes; the Rambam describes a great sound like huge millstones grinding.
  • One may choose either Oseh Maaseh Bereshit or "shekoho ugevurato malei olam".
  • The Hakhamim set no minimum, so the poskim write that any earthquake one hears, even low on the Richter scale, without danger or fear, takes the berakhah.
  • It must be said within a few seconds of hearing it.
  • It is not limited to once in 30 days: after hesech hadaat, having taken one's mind off it, one blesses again, so a tremor in the morning and another in the afternoon each take a berakhah.
  • On winds the Gemara requires extremely strong ones. A hurricane or tornado certainly qualifies, with the same choice of berakhot, said within seconds.
  • The poskim add that even wind short of a hurricane, strong enough to shake doors and windows and move things, such as gusts of 100 miles per hour in the UK, warrants Oseh Maaseh Bereshit.

In one line

On any audible earthquake, a hurricane or wind strong enough to shake the house, say the berakhah within seconds, each time anew.
