Topic: Introduction to a new series on lesser-known berakhot, and the three categories of blessings

Daily Halacha: Peculiar Berachot, Monday 20 January 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • After two years on topics from the parashah, the Daily Halacha now takes core topics systematically, opening with lesser-known berakhot the Hakhamim instituted on nature: not only thunder, lightning and rainbows, but creatures, animals, kinds of people and other aspects of nature.
  • All berakhot are rabbinic. Birkat HaMazon after bread is the only one that all opinions hold is from the Torah.
  • Birkot hanehenin: on pleasure from this world (food, drink, smell), always with a berakhah before and sometimes after.
  • Birkot hamisvot: almost always before the misvah, and normally not said afterwards, as on eating massah, sitting in the sukkah, shehitah and immersing utensils in the mikveh.
  • Birkot hashevah, of praise and thanksgiving, on nature around us, making us conscious of God in daily life. The classic examples are the berakhot before Shema in Shaharit and Arvit, on God creating light and darkness and changing day to night.
  • The coming weeks focus on less common berakhot of this third kind, which build awareness of Hashem in all of nature. When saying any berakhah, try to recognise its category.

In one line

Every berakhah is one of benefit, of misvah, or of praise, and this series explores the lesser-known berakhot of praise on nature.
