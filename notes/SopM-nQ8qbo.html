Topic: The berakhot of Shehehiyanu and Mehayeh HaMetim on seeing a dear friend or relative after a long time

Sources: Gemara Berakhot (Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi); Rambam; Tosafot; Shulhan Arukh; the Maharsha; Hesed LaAlafim (Rabbi Eliezer Papo); the Ben Ish Hai; Hakham Ovadiah Yoseph

Daily Halacha: Peculiar Berachot, Tuesday 21 January 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi says in the Gemara that on seeing a friend not seen for 30 days one says Shehehiyanu; the Rambam codifies it. Tosafot limit it to a friend who is especially dear, and the Shulhan Arukh adds this condition.
  • After more than 12 months one says "Barukh Atah Hashem... Mehayeh HaMetim". Simply, after 12 months a person is forgotten, so he has come alive in one's life again. The Maharsha, in Poland, explains that Yom Kippur has passed and he was judged for life. The Shulhan Arukh codifies this, again only for one who is dear.
  • Why is it rarely heard? The Hesed LaAlafim, by Rabbi Eliezer Papo of Bulgaria (author of Pele Yoes), says flattery is rampant and people say it without meaning it, so it is better said without Hashem's name. The Ben Ish Hai records the same.
  • But a berakhah without Hashem's name and kingship is no berakhah, so Hakham Ovadiah and many poskim reject this: when the conditions are met, say the full berakhah, as Hakhamim are recorded doing, in line with the Gemara, Rambam and Shulhan Arukh.
  • If one corresponded during the 12 months (emails, phone calls), the poskim write one says Shehehiyanu instead of Mehayeh HaMetim.
  • It applies to friends, parents, children, siblings and relatives, regardless of gender, whenever one is very happy to see the person.

In one line

On seeing someone dear after 30 days say Shehehiyanu, and after 12 months without contact Mehayeh HaMetim, with Hashem's name.
