Topic: Sitting in the sukkah without a berakhah on Shemini Aseret outside Israel, and its message

Sources: Zekharyah

Daily Halacha: Sukkot, Sunday 26 September 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Shemini Aseret is a new festival, which is why Shehiyanu is said in its Kiddush, unlike the seventh night of Pesah.
  • Outside Israel it is also possibly the seventh day of Sukkot, since the second day of Yom Tov exists only in case it was really the first.
  • Shemini Aseret itself has nothing to do with the sukkah; the Torah commands sukkah for seven days only. We sit in the sukkah for the night and day meals only because it may be the seventh day.
  • Though we make berakhot on other second days of Yom Tov, no Leshev BaSukkah is said: it would imply it is not Shemini Aseret and detract from its sanctity.
  • So we celebrate it as the main festival, with Shehiyanu and in the Tefillah, and sit in the sukkah without a berakhah.
  • The message: Sukkot is universal. Zekharyah foresees the nations celebrating it, it concerns the world's need for rain, and 70 bulls are offered for the 70 nations.
  • Shemini Aseret is yihud, the Hakhamim's one more day of HaShem alone with Israel. With a strong core connection to Torah and HaShem we need not fear engaging the world.
  • In Israel Sukkot ends before Shemini Aseret; in the diaspora, living among the nations, both elements meet on one day.

In one line

Outside Israel we sit in the sukkah on Shemini Aseret without a berakhah, joining our private bond with HaShem to our place in the wider world.
