Topic: Children of an extremely promiscuous woman, and marrying the woman herself

Sources: The Gemara; the Rambam; the Bahag (Baal Halachot Gedolot); Shulhan Arukh; the Aharonim

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer, Wednesday 20 May 2026

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • A child of a woman who committed adultery once or twice is assumed to be her husband's and is completely kasher, even to marry a Kohen.
  • The Gemara asks about a woman who is extremely promiscuous (perusah beyoter), acting brazenly and without shame as a regular occurrence, and leaves the question unresolved.
  • The Rambam rules that since it is unresolved, we must be concerned about the children.
  • The Bahag holds the children are kasher: a safek mamzer is kasher from the Torah and forbidden only rabbinically, so in a doubt of rabbinic law one may be lenient.
  • The Shulhan Arukh rules like the Rambam: we are concerned about the children.
  • The majority of the Aharonim understand the concern as possible mamzerut, so a regular Jew should not marry into that family. A minority read it only as advice for those who want good lineage, not a prohibition. The main view, and seemingly the Shulhan Arukh, is that there is a prohibition.
  • The woman herself: adultery does not forbid her to marry another man, but a Kohen may not marry a woman who committed adultery even once. The Gemara advises even a regular Jew not to marry her because of her conduct; this is good advice rather than necessarily a prohibition.
