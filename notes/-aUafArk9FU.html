Topic: Moving Shabbat candles, candlesticks and their tray, while lit and after they go out

Sources: Shulhan Arukh; the Rama; the Rishonim

Daily Halacha: Shabbat, Wednesday 9 June 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • While the candles are lit they are mukseh by rabbinic law, lest moving them extinguish them. The candlesticks and even the tray are forbidden too, since a base for something mukseh is also mukseh.
  • Normally what is mukseh at the start of Shabbat stays mukseh all Shabbat, even once the reason has gone.
  • The Shulhan Arukh, following many Rishonim, rules that since candles commonly go out during Shabbat, one who stipulates (a tenai) before Shabbat that he intends to move them once they go out may do so. The stipulation can be made once a year.
  • The Rama, for Ashkenazim, says the stipulation does not help: what was mukseh when Shabbat began stays mukseh.
  • A second option: a base is mukseh only when it holds something forbidden. If one places something permitted and of greater value on the tray, such as jewellery, the tray may be moved once the candles go out. This is less universally accepted, but it is a solution.

In one line

Lit candles and their tray may not be moved; once out, Sepharadim who stipulated, or who placed something valuable on the tray, may move them.
