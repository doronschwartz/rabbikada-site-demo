Topic: The decree against laundry on Hol HaMoed and its exceptions

Sources: The Gemara; the Mishnah; the Rishonim

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Monday 27 October 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Laundry is melekhet hedyot and should be permitted for the needs of the moed, but the Gemara records a decree, as with haircuts, so that people do not postpone their laundry to Hol HaMoed when they have time.
  • The Rishonim imply another reason, the tirha of laundry in those days. Some suggested washing machines make it permitted today, but that is not the halacha: the main reason is postponement, and the decree is comprehensive.
  • Exception: the Mishnah permits washing things that get dirty daily, such as hand towels, when needed for the rest of the moed.
  • By extension, children's clothes, with two caveats: they must be needed on the moed, and they must have been washed before the moed and got dirty during it, not left over.
  • The same applies to underwear, even of adults, if needed on the moed and not left over from before.
  • A child here means one who gets dirty regularly: some poskim say up to nine, some up to bar or bat misvah; it depends on the child.

In one line

Laundry is forbidden on Hol HaMoed, except for things that get dirty regularly, like towels, underwear and children's clothes, washed before and needed on the moed.
