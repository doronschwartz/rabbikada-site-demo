Topic: The berakhah on mashed fruit, purees, crisps and juice with pulp

Sources: The Rambam; the Rishonim

Daily Halacha: Berachot, Tuesday 23 January 2024

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • A fruit stays a fruit however finely it is cut or shaped, and even mashed into a pulp it remains HaEs.
  • A puree, strained until it has become liquid, is debated: the Rambam holds it is still HaEs, other Rishonim hold that once it loses its solid form it is Shehakol. Because of safek berakhot lehakel, the berakhah on a true puree is Shehakol.
  • Crisps made from finely sliced potato never lost their form and are HaAdamah. Pringles, made from potato flour reconstituted into something else, are Shehakol.
  • Apple sauce with pieces of apple in it, such as homemade, is HaEs. If it is ground into a liquid or strained so no pieces remain, it is Shehakol.
  • Fruit juice, apart from olives and grapes, is not considered fruit in halacha, so juice is Shehakol, even with pulp, since the pulp is secondary.
  • One who drinks Tropicana primarily for the pulp would seemingly say HaEs, the juice being secondary; but people generally drink it for the juice.

In one line

Mashed fruit with pieces is HaEs, while purees and juices, even with pulp, are Shehakol unless the pulp is the main reason for drinking.
