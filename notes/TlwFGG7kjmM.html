Topic: The versions of Birkat HaShanim in winter and summer, and what to do if one forgets the request for rain

Sources: The Gemara; Rambam; the Tur

Daily Halacha: Hanukkah

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Sepharadi practice today is Barekh Alenu in winter and Barekhenu in summer.
  • Rambam said Barekhenu all year, adding the request for rain in winter. The Gemara only says that the request is made in the blessing of the years, without fixing a text, and the Tur brings the different customs.
  • The Temanim, he thinks, follow the Rambam with Barekhenu all year; Ashkenazim say Barekh Alenu all year and change only the words of the request.
  • Saying only morid hatal in winter is fine, since that is just a statement. But the Hakhamim required asking for rain, so one who forgot to ask and finished the Amidah repeats it, throughout the winter.
  • One unsure whether he said it presumes he did not until he has said it about a hundred times; after that he may presume he said it correctly. So pay attention.
  • One who remembers after Barekh Alenu but by Shema Kolenu inserts "veten tal umatar livrakhah" there and continues. One who has passed Shema Kolenu but not finished the Amidah goes back to Barekh Alenu. After finishing the Amidah he repeats it.

In one line

Forgetting to ask for rain requires repeating the Amidah, unless one remembers in time to go back or to add it in Shema Kolenu.
