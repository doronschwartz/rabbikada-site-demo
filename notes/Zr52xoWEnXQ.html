Topic: What is the right age for women to marry today?

Sources: The Gemara (Sanhedrin); Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer, Wednesday 28 January 2026

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Hazal give no age for women, since women have no independent misvah of procreation. There is still some misvah for women in marrying, explained later.
  • The Gemara in Sanhedrin says it is right for girls to marry as early as possible. The halacha even provides that a father should give up to a tenth of his assets, even after his death, to help his daughter marry.
  • The Torah allows a father to betroth his daughter under 12, but Hazal forbid a man to do so until she is grown and says whom she wants to marry. The Torah's permission was for dire circumstances, when a father could not otherwise provide for her; the proper way is to marry her only to someone she wants, at the right time.
  • Today women are often expected to earn and are more educated, including in Torah, which should be taken into account as with men.
  • So the appropriate age for women today seems similar to men, roughly 20 to 24; earlier, from 18, is best where she is ready.

In one line

There is no set age for women, but marrying early when ready is encouraged, and today a range similar to men (about 20 to 24) seems right.
