Topic: Crushing ice, letting it melt, and freezing water into ice cubes on Shabbat

Sources: The Gemara; the Rambam; Shulhan Arukh; Hakham Ovadiah

Daily Halacha: Shabbat, Tuesday 15 June 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Gemara forbids crushing snow, hail or ice on Shabbat without giving a reason.
  • The Rambam explains it resembles Dash (threshing), extracting liquid from a solid as with olives or grapes. It is not truly Dash, since nothing is extracted, so the prohibition is rabbinic. Others say it looks like creating something new.
  • One may take out ice and let it melt by itself, according to the Shulhan Arukh and Sepharadim; only actively crushing it is forbidden.
  • Freezing liquid is not mentioned as a problem anywhere. Some compare it to building, but Hakham Ovadiah dismisses these weak arguments and rules that making ice cubes on Shabbat is mutar.
  • The caveat is preparation: one may not freeze water on Shabbat solely for after Shabbat, such as a Sunday party. If one intended to use it on Shabbat and in the end did not need it, that is permitted.
  • One may fill and freeze a whole ice tray even if only part is needed on Shabbat, since that is the normal single act.

In one line

Ice may not be crushed but may melt by itself, and one may make ice cubes on Shabbat when they are meant for Shabbat.
