Topic: The status of the misvah of Hallah today, in and outside Israel, and its reasons

Sources: Parashat Shelah Lekha ("Baakholkhem milehem haares"); the Gemara; the Rambam; Sefer HaHinukh

Daily Halacha: Hallah, Wednesday 29 June 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Parashat Shelah Lekha commands separating part of the dough, given in Torah times to the Kohanim: "when you eat of the bread of the land", a misvah for those in Israel.
  • The Gemara learns from "when you come to the land" that it is biblical only when all or most of the Jewish people live in Israel; so today it is rabbinic even there.
  • The Hakhamim also instituted Hafrashat Hallah outside Israel, the Rambam explains, so that the misvah is not forgotten.
  • So it is rabbinic everywhere today, but outside Israel it is wholly rabbinic while in Israel it has a biblical source and could again be biblical.
  • One difference: outside Israel one may eat the bread first and separate Hallah afterwards, so if it was not separated before Shabbat (when separating is forbidden, to be explained), one leaves a piece and separates it after Shabbat. In Israel one may not eat before Hallah is taken.
  • Sefer HaHinukh gives two reasons: bread was the main sustenance, and HaShem wanted to elevate the mundane act of eating it; and it is another gift supporting the Kohanim in their service.

In one line
