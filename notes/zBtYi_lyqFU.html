Topic: The two Misvot of studying Torah and teaching Torah

Sources: Shulhan Arukh; Rambam; Yehoshua (Vehagita bo yomam valaylah); the Rema; the Torah (Veshinantam levanekha)

The halacha

  • There are two separate Misvot: to study Torah and to teach Torah.
  • The Shulhan Arukh opens with the Rambam: every Jewish male, rich or poor, healthy or not, young or old, even with a wife, children and family duties, must set aside time to study Torah by day and by night, from "vehagita bo yomam valaylah" in Yehoshua. How long is discussed later.
  • The Rema: in pressing circumstances, when study was impossible, reciting Shema at Shahrit and Arvit fulfils the obligation, since its paragraphs are Torah.
  • The second Misvah, "veshinantam levanekha", is to teach one's children Torah, or hire a teacher if one cannot. The obligation is to a son, not a daughter (women are discussed later), and also to a grandson.
  • The Hakhamim read "your sons" as your Talmidim, so a teacher must teach his students, though sons and grandsons take precedence. It is up to the Rav to decide who his Talmidim are.
  • The Rambam praises study at night: though the duty is day and night, the main time is at night, when it is cold and dark and one wants to sleep, and that is how one merits the crown of Torah.

Sources quoted

  • Rambam, quoted at the opening of the Shulhan Arukh: every Jewish male must study day and night.
  • Yehoshua: "vehagita bo yomam valaylah".
  • The Rema: Shema morning and evening suffices in pressing circumstances.
  • The Torah: "veshinantam levanekha", read also as students.

In one line

Every man must fix Torah study by day and by night, and teach his sons, grandsons and students.
