Topic: Why Sepharadim stand for Kiddush on Sukkot mornings

Sources: The Rambam

Daily Halacha: Sukkot, Friday 24 September 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • When one person performs a misvah on behalf of others, all should be seated. So Sepharadim sit for Kiddush on Shabbat morning and for Havdalah on Mosei Shabbat. On Friday night we stand, for various reasons given, such as the sanctity of Shabbat arriving.
  • On Sukkot even the morning Kiddush, on Shabbat and Yom Tov, is said standing; after Leshev BaSukkah one sits down.
  • The reason: for the Rambam "Basukkot teshevu" means actually sitting, and a berakhah is said before the misvah, as with the lulav and etrog. So Leshev BaSukkah, said with Kiddush, must come before sitting.

In one line

On Sukkot, Kiddush is said standing even on Shabbat morning, and one sits only after Leshev BaSukkah.
