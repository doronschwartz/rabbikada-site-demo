Topic: Meat and wine in the Nine Days, and haircuts, laundry and showers in a year with no "week of Tisha BeAv"

Sources: The Gemara; Shulhan Arukh; the Rishonim

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Monday 28 July 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • By the Gemara, meat and wine are forbidden only at the seudah hamafseket, the final meal after midday on Erev Tisha BeAv. This year Erev Tisha BeAv is Shabbat, so that does not apply.
  • The Shulhan Arukh brings three customs from the Rishonim: refraining all three weeks, all nine days, or the week of Tisha BeAv. Ashkenazim generally refrain all nine days. Sepharadi communities varied: nine days, the week of Tisha BeAv, only Erev Tisha BeAv, or nine days excluding Rosh Hodesh.
  • One should keep one's minhag; the Shulhan Arukh writes very sharply against breaching the community's minhag. One without a minhag should follow the minhag of many Sepharadim, refraining all nine days.
  • Haircuts, shaving, laundry and showers are forbidden by the Gemara in the week of Tisha BeAv.
  • The Shulhan Arukh brings two opinions on whether there is a week of Tisha BeAv when the fast is on Sunday, but these apply only when Tisha BeAv falls on Shabbat and is postponed to Sunday.
  • In 2025 Tisha BeAv actually falls on Sunday, not postponed. So according to all opinions there is no week of Tisha BeAv, and Sepharadim may have haircuts, shave and do laundry up to Tisha BeAv, and shower until the Friday before.
  • Ashkenazim have different minhagim (some refrain from haircuts all three weeks, laundry and showers all nine days) and should continue them.
