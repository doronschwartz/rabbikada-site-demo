Topic: Car repairs, MOTs, services and taxi driving on Hol HaMoed

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Thursday 30 October 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • A car problem from before the moed may not be deliberately left to fix on the moed (shelo yekhaven melakhto bamoed).
  • A car that breaks down on the moed may be fixed only if it is needed for the moed: not, for example, on the afternoon before Shemini Aseret or the seventh day of Pesah, if it will not be used again until after.
  • It must also be melekhet hedyot. A broken engine needing a professional mechanic seems to be melekhet uman and must wait. Changing a tyre or fixing a puncture may be melekhet hedyot, though for some it needs a mechanic; he finds it hard to define exactly, so one must judge the circumstances: minor, simple, little time and effort.
  • An MOT (the UK's annual road-safety check) should not be scheduled for the moed. But if one discovers on the festival that it expires on the first day of Hol HaMoed, so the car cannot legally be driven, it is davar haaved and seemingly melekhet hedyot (only a check), so it is permitted.
  • A car service should not be scheduled on the moed; do it after.
  • Working as a paid taxi driver seems forbidden, since paid work becomes forbidden even if melekhet hedyot. The poskim permit it where it is a public need, such as the only driver in a village or moshav, but not where there are plenty of taxis.

In one line

A car may be repaired on Hol HaMoed only if needed for the moed and the repair is simple, while MOTs, services and taxi work are generally not permitted.
