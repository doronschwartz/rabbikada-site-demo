Topic: Why Al HaNissim has no counterpart in Al HaMihyah

Sources: The Brisker Rav

Daily Halacha: Hanukkah

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • On Hanukkah we add Al HaNissim in the Amidah and in Birkat HaMazon; one who forgets it does not go back.
  • Other insertions are echoed in Al HaMihyah (Berakhah Mein Shalosh): for Rosh Hodesh "vezokhrenu letovah beyom Rosh Hodesh hazeh", and so on for the festivals. Yet Hanukkah and Purim are not mentioned there.
  • The Brisker Rav explains: Al HaMihyah summarises the three berakhot of Birkat HaMazon that are from the Torah, ending with Boneh Yerushalayim (hence the "Amen" there); HaTov VeHametiv and what follows are additions.
  • One who forgot Resseh or Yaaleh VeYavo and remembers after Boneh Yerushalayim, before beginning HaTov VeHametiv, inserts a special berakhah there. Unlikely as it is, these insertions can warrant a berakhah of their own, so they are mentioned in Al HaMihyah.
  • Al HaNissim never warrants its own berakhah, since one never goes back for it, so it is not summarised in Al HaMihyah.

In one line

Al HaMihyah mentions only insertions that can become a berakhah of their own, which Al HaNissim never does.
