Topic: Bringing in the second night of Shavuot early so that the meal may be prepared on the first day

Daily Halacha: Shavuot and Talmud Torah, Thursday 13 May 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Even for Ashkenazim, waiting for nightfall applies only to the first night of Shavuot, to keep the 49th day complete.
  • On the second night one may pray Arvit and make Kiddush before sunset, any time after Pelag HaMinhah (about 7.30 in London), and it can even be preferable.
  • One may not prepare from a day of greater sanctity for one of lesser sanctity. The first day of Hag is biblical and the second rabbinic, so one may not set the table, lay cutlery or heat food on the first day for the second.
  • If one will make Kiddush and eat before sunset (about 8.45 in London), the meal is still eaten on the first day. One may then set the table, heat and even cook food all afternoon for that meal.
  • If one will make Kiddush only after sunset, no preparations may be made until nightfall, which can be very late. It is therefore advisable to make Kiddush and eat before sunset.

In one line

Make Kiddush before sunset on the second night of Shavuot, and the meal may be prepared during the first day's afternoon.
