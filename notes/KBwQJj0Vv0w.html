Topic: Taking medication, cooking, slaughtering, trapping and measuring on Yom Tov

Sources: Hakham Ovadiah Yoseph; the Hakhamim

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Wednesday 25 June 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Refuah on Shabbat, taking medication without a strong need, is a rabbinic prohibition based on tohen: medicine was made from herbs, and the Hakhamim feared one would come to crush them. So on Shabbat one generally may not take a tablet for a minor headache.
  • Since grinding is permitted on Yom Tov by the letter of the law, Hakham Ovadiah holds all medication is permitted on Yom Tov, even for a minor ailment. This is much debated and not all agree, but there is plenty to rely on.
  • The eleventh melakhah, cooking and baking, is permitted on Yom Tov in all the ways discussed.
  • Other melakhot, such as writing, building and tearing, are not for okhel nefesh and are forbidden as on Shabbat.
  • In principle one may slaughter an animal or a chicken on Yom Tov, with many details. But the Hakhamim forbade trapping (sedah), even to slaughter, so all trapping is forbidden.
  • Measuring (medidah) is a rabbinic prohibition derived from writing, forbidden on Shabbat unless for a misvah. On Yom Tov one may measure ingredients for a recipe, as part of okhel nefesh.

On Yom Tov one may, per Hakham Ovadiah, take medication even for minor ailments and measure for cooking, but not trap or do melakhot unrelated to food.
