Topic: Why the Thirteen Attributes work only when we emulate them

Sources: Gemara Rosh Hashanah; Tomer Devorah (Rabbi Moshe Cordovero of Sefat); Tehillim 121

Daily Halacha: Yom Kippur, Thursday 10 October 2024

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The points

  • We say the Thirteen Attributes of Mercy 26 times on Yom Kippur, and many times in the 40 days of Selihot.
  • The Gemara in Rosh Hashanah says that after the golden calf Hashem taught Moshe this prayer, and Rabbi Yehudah says a covenant was made that the Thirteen Attributes never return empty-handed. Yet our prayers do not always seem answered.
  • Tomer Devorah, by Rabbi Moshe Cordovero of Sefat some 500 years ago, notes the wording: "let them do before Me this order", not say it. Hashem need not be reminded more than once an hour that He is merciful.
  • Each time we say Hashem is merciful, compassionate, truthful and slow to anger, we should ask whether we are so with colleagues and acquaintances. The guarantee holds when we say them and emulate them, not when we pay lip service.
  • "Hashem is your shadow": our relationship with Hashem is an ongoing tutorial in our relationships with others.
  • The Gemara teaches elsewhere that one of the greatest ways to have our sins forgiven is to forgive others. Letting go is hard, but it is the real test.

In one line

The Thirteen Attributes are guaranteed to work when we live them, above all by forgiving others.
