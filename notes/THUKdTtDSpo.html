Topic: When a berakhah is said on water, and how soda water is treated

Sources: The Gemara; the Rambam

Daily Halacha: Berachot, Wednesday 17 January 2024

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Any food one benefits from takes a berakhah, even a small amount, unless it is harmful. Water is an exception: the Hakhamim say one blesses on it only when drinking to quench thirst.
  • So no berakhah is said on water drunk to clear the throat, to swallow a pill or help swallow food (as an elderly person may need), or for medical reasons such as aiding urination or bowel movements. Water is unflavoured, and its benefit is quenching thirst.
  • The Rambam wrote that one should drink only when thirsty, but today we know one should stay hydrated. Drinking a lot in advance of a long summer outing, when not thirsty, takes no berakhah.
  • Flavoured drinks always take a berakhah, since it is on the flavour.
  • Soda water is water with a distinctive taste from the carbon dioxide. He has not seen a teshuvah on it and would appreciate hearing of one.
  • In his opinion it depends on the person, as berakhot often follow how one relates to a food: one who drinks soda essentially to quench thirst says no berakhah when not thirsty, while one who drinks it for the bubbles and texture, like Coca-Cola, even when not thirsty, says a berakhah.

In one line

Plain water takes a berakhah only when quenching thirst, and soda water, in his view, depends on whether one drinks it for pleasure.
