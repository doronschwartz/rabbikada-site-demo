Topic: Haircuts and shaving on the Friday before Lag BaOmer when it falls on Sunday

Sources: The Rama; the Radbaz

Daily Halacha: Sefirat HaOmer, Friday 24 May 2024

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Today is the 31st of the Omer, so Lag BaOmer, the 33rd, is on Sunday. Ashkenazim generally shave and cut hair on the 33rd day; Sepharadim wait until the 34th, this year Monday morning.
  • The Rama writes that when Lag BaOmer falls on Sunday, Ashkenazim may cut hair and shave from Friday, since it is Kavod Shabbat to enter Shabbat groomed when one could cut on Sunday anyway.
  • The poskim discuss whether this is from Friday morning or after midday; the general consensus permits all of Friday.
  • The permission is only on Friday. One who missed it may not cut on Mosei Shabbat, since the whole reason is Kavod Shabbat.
  • For Sepharadim, permitted only from the 34th day, it is not allowed today. In extenuating circumstances, such as needing to be ready on Monday, one should ask a rabbi: the Radbaz allows a haircut every Friday, and though we do not necessarily follow him, together with the Rama one could perhaps be lenient where there is a need.

Sources quoted

  • The Rama: when Lag BaOmer falls on Sunday, haircuts are permitted on Friday for Kavod Shabbat.
  • The Radbaz: one may cut hair every Friday of the Omer.

In one line

Ashkenazim may cut hair today for Kavod Shabbat but not on Mosei Shabbat, while Sepharadim wait until Monday morning unless a rabbi permits it for a real need.
