Topic: Praying a late Minhah on Friday after the community has accepted Shabbat

Sources: Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Shabbat, Tuesday 8 June 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Shulhan Arukh: the community accepts Shabbat at "Boi khallah" at the end of Lekha Dodi, or at Mizmor Shir LeYom HaShabbat.
  • Once they have, one who arrives late may not pray the weekday Minhah in the same room. He must pray it in a separate place and then join them. We act together as a sibbur and not against the spirit of the community's acceptance.
  • If he arrives before they have accepted, during Mizmor LeDavid or Lekha Dodi, he may begin Minhah in the same hall, even though they will accept Shabbat before he finishes.
  • If he joins in Mizmor Shir or answers Barukh Hashem HaMevorakh to the hazan's Barkhu, even unwittingly, he has accepted Shabbat with them, since Barkhu begins Arvit. He can no longer pray Minhah even in another room.
  • He then prays Arvit twice: once for Arvit with the community, and again as tashlumin for the missed Minhah, though the sun is still out.
  • Best of all is to come on time and pray Minhah and Arvit with the community.

In one line

Once the community accepts Shabbat, a latecomer prays Minhah elsewhere, and if he has answered Barkhu he makes it up with a second Arvit.
