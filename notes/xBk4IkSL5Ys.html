Topic: The berakhah "Hakham HaRazim" on seeing 600,000 Jews together

Sources: The Gemara; the Rishonim; Rambam; Shulhan Arukh; the later poskim

Daily Halacha: Peculiar Berachot, Thursday 13 February 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • One who sees "shishim ribbo" Jews says "Barukh... Hakham HaRazim". A ribbo is 10,000, the highest Hebrew unit, so this is 600,000.
  • The Gemara explains: as each person looks different, so each mind and opinion differs, and among so many we bless Hashem who knows the inner secrets of every one.
  • Why 600,000? Perhaps it is the number of men who left Egypt, a whole nation. Contemporary halachists suggest it then requires 600,000 males, though no Rishon or Aharon mentions this.
  • Or perhaps it is simply a vast number, since for the Hakhamim sixty marks independent standing (more than a sixtieth is not nullified), in which case males are not required.
  • The Gemara and most Rishonim set no place, but the Rambam adds it applies only in Eres Yisrael. The Shulhan Arukh omits this, yet the later poskim follow the Rambam, so outside Israel one says only "Barukh Hakham HaRazim" without Hashem's name.
  • The Shulhan Arukh adds that one must see them together at once, not merely know they are there.
  • It is still said: a rosh yeshiva recited it at a vast gathering in Israel in 2014.

In one line

On seeing 600,000 Jews at once in Israel, perhaps only males, one says Hakham HaRazim with Hashem's name.
