Topic: How quickly to drink the four cups, red wine, and eating or drinking before the massah

Sources: The Rambam; the Raavad; the Midrash; Biur Halacha citing the Ramban; Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Pesah, Wednesday 22 March 2023

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Food eaten for a misvah must be eaten within Kedei Akhilat Peras, roughly five to seven minutes, such as the kazayit of massah.
  • For liquids, the Rambam held the time is much shorter, drinking in the normal way in a few gulps; the Raavad held it is the same as for food.
  • So each cup (a reviit of about 86 ml, or at least 44 ml) should be drunk in two to four gulps, not sipped, in the first instance. Bediavad, drinking it within about five minutes suffices.
  • Red wine is preferred, being generally better and a sign of aristocracy, and recalling the blood of the children, which the Midrash says Pharaoh bathed in. If white wine is better or more enjoyable, it is permitted.
  • From the start of Maggid, the Biur Halacha cites the Ramban and other Rishonim that one should not interrupt by eating or drinking until the massah, unless so hungry one cannot concentrate.
  • Between Kiddush and Maggid eating is strictly allowed, but eat little, so as to eat the massah with appetite (leteavon), as the Shulhan Arukh rules even before Pesah.
  • Also, karpas is less than a kazayit so that no Borei Nefashot is said and its Borei Peri HaAdamah covers the maror. If one eats enough before Maggid to require Borei Nefashot, say it, and then do not repeat Borei Peri HaAdamah on the maror. Better not to eat, or to eat less than a kazayit.

Sources quoted

  • The Rambam and the Raavad: the time limit for drinking.
  • The Midrash: Pharaoh bathing in the children's blood.
  • Biur Halacha, citing the Ramban: no eating or drinking once Maggid begins.
  • Shulhan Arukh: do not eat too much before the massah.

In one line

Drink each cup in a few gulps, prefer red wine, and avoid eating before the massah, or keep it under a kazayit.
