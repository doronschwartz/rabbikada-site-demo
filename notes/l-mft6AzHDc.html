Topic: Why Rosh Hashanah is about Hashem's sovereignty, not personal requests

Sources: The Zohar

Daily Halacha: Rosh Hashanah, Thursday 14 September 2023

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The points

  • Rosh Hashanah is not about praying for oneself. The Zohar says that those who pray on Rosh Hashanah for children, health and livelihood are like dogs barking at their master.
  • Rosh Hashanah is about recognizing the Malkhut and Yihud of HaKadosh Barukh Hu: that everything comes from Him and nothing happens without His will. When a person crowns Hashem this way, everything falls into place.
  • Introspection and requests belong to Yom Kippur, which is why there is no Viddui on Rosh Hashanah.
  • The Tefillah the Hakhamim instituted for Rosh Hashanah contains no personal requests; it is all about Hashem's sovereignty being recognized universally.
  • In the times of the Geonim, phrases such as "remember us for life" and "write us in the book of life" were added, since people found the pure theme hard to relate to. They are later additions, not the essence.
  • It is the Day of Judgment, but coming with personal requests is asking for judgment on whether one deserves them. The best way to succeed in judgment is to recognize that our Father is in control.
  • The shofar is the coronation: if Rosh Hashanah were only two minutes long, the most important thing would be the shofar.

In one line

On Rosh Hashanah the focus is "not me but You, Hashem": crowning Him through the Tefillah and the shofar, leaving personal requests for Yom Kippur.
