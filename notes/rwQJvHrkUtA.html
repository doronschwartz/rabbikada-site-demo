Topic: Whether popcorn is HaAdamah or Shehakol

Sources: Shulhan Arukh; the Kaf HaHayim; Hakham Ovadiah Yoseph; Rav Moshe Feinstein

Daily Halacha: Berachot, Thursday 25 January 2024

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Shulhan Arukh rules that ground spices mixed with sugar are HaAdamah, since the spices are primary and the sugar is secondary (tafel).
  • The Kaf HaHayim wrote that this applies only when the spices are not completely ground; once they are unrecognisable they lose their status and become Shehakol. By that reasoning one could argue popcorn, whose form has changed, is Shehakol.
  • Hakham Ovadiah disagreed: the primary ingredient keeps its berakhah even when unrecognisable, as the Shulhan Arukh writes of finely ground spices, so popcorn is HaAdamah.
  • Rav Moshe Feinstein also held popcorn is HaAdamah. Even granting that a food can lose its form, that applies to mashing, grinding or crushing until it is unrecognisable; popcorn is only puffed, and everyone knows it is corn, as its very name shows.
  • So most poskim hold one says HaAdamah on popcorn, at least Hakham Ovadiah Yoseph and Rav Moshe Feinstein.

In one line

Popcorn is Boreh Peri HaAdamah, since puffing corn does not change its form.
