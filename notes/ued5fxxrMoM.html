Topic: Reducing simhah in the nine days: entertainment, building, business, weddings and buying a car

Sources: Mishnah; Tosafot; Mishnah Berurah; Magen Avraham; Rambam; Shulhan Arukh; Rav Moshe Feinstein

Daily Halacha: The Three Weeks, Thursday 1 August 2024

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • "Mishenikhnas Av memaatin besimhah": from Rosh Hodesh Av we reduce our simhah.
  • Entertainment that is not a necessity, though permitted all year, should be avoided; each person must work out what is necessity and what is luxury.
  • Binyan shel simhah: building that is not a necessity is forbidden. An extension needed because the house is too small may continue; a back garden swimming pool, unless for medical purposes, may not.
  • Masa umattan shel simhah: business purely for simhah, such as buying things for a mundane (non-misvah) party after Shabbat, is not allowed.
  • No weddings from Rosh Hodesh. Engagements are permitted even on Tisha BeAv itself, so as not to miss the opportunity.
  • Rav Moshe Feinstein: an upgrade car should wait until after Tisha BeAv. If one has no car, or it has broken down, or the price will rise or the car will not be available, one may buy it.
  • Haircuts are still permitted at this stage. The time should be used for introspection about the galut.

Sources quoted

  • Tosafot: since no measure is given, one must not rejoice at all; codified by the Mishnah Berurah, Magen Avraham and others.
  • Rambam quotes the Mishnah verbatim and seems to take it literally: one is in a less happy spirit than usual.

In one line

In the nine days one curtails all simhah that is not a necessity: entertainment, building, purchases for celebrations and weddings.
