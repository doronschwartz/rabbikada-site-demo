The points

  • On Hoshana Rabbah 5786, the berakhah "Matir asurim", who releases the imprisoned, has taken on new meaning: those held in terror tunnels for two years are now free.
  • When we say it on Shemini Aseret and Simhat Torah, we should thank HaShem for what will be the end of this war, and for what we read on Simhat Torah: Israel dwelling securely.
  • The Vilna Gaon called "vesamahta behagekha", to be joyful all eight (outside Israel nine) days, the hardest misvah. This year we can enter Shemini Aseret and Simhat Torah with joy, renewal and hope.
  • Our greatest victory is "Nesah Yisrael lo yeshakker": the eternity of Israel never fails.
  • There is a time to mourn and a time to dance; now is the time to rejoice and thank HaShem.
  • The tragedy began on Simhat Torah, and just under two years later by the Jewish calendar, the day before Simhat Torah, an end seems in sight.
  • There are a few thousand fewer Jews since 7 October; we have a duty to fill their places in synagogue and dance with the Torah that has kept us through every tragedy.

In one line

This Simhat Torah we dance with thanks for the captives' release and fill the places of those we lost.
