Topic: The third heter: public needs (sorkhei rabbim) on Hol HaMoed

Sources: The Gemara; Shulhan Arukh; the Bet Yoseph

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Wednesday 12 November 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Gemara gives examples such as repairing roads and wells: people need to travel and to get water. The Shulhan Arukh permits these on the moed even as melekhet uman and even for pay.
  • Today this extends to anything for public use that is needed for the moed: a mechanic fixing a bus taking people on a Hol HaMoed trip, lorries transporting food, rubbish collection in Israel, a communal staircase or communal garden in a block of flats.
  • If it is for public use but not needed for the moed, only melekhet hedyot is allowed.
  • The Bet Yoseph discusses building a synagogue on Hol HaMoed: it is melekhet uman and will not be finished for the festival, so seemingly not allowed. He notes that in his time, with troubles from the surrounding population, not building might mean being stopped later, which is davar haaved and permitted.
  • Today a week's break generally does not prevent building later, so one may not build or renovate even communal buildings such as a Jewish school. If not doing so would force a school to close during term, that would probably be davar haaved.

Sources quoted

  • The Gemara and Shulhan Arukh: repairing roads and wells on the moed.
  • The Bet Yoseph: building a synagogue, permitted in his time only as davar haaved.

In one line

Any melakhah for public needs of the moed is permitted on Hol HaMoed, but public work not needed for the moed is limited to non-professional work.
