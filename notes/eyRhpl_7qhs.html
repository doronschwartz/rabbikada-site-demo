Topic: Repairing and buying items used to prepare food (makhshirei okhel nefesh) on Hol HaMoed

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Thursday 18 September 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Beyond food itself, melakhah for things that prepare food is also permitted. The poskim write that a fridge that breaks on Hol HaMoed may be fixed, by oneself or a paid technician, even though that is maaseh uman, because it is makhshirei okhel nefesh: without it one cannot store food for the moed.
  • The same applies to an oven, a kitchen sink and any utensil used to prepare food.
  • Caveat: one may not intentionally leave the repair for Hol HaMoed. One who could fix it before the festival but plans to call the technician when off work on Hol HaMoed should not do so; this is debated, but that is the halacha.
  • If one simply did not get round to it, being busy, it may be done on Hol HaMoed.
  • Likewise one may buy cutlery on Hol HaMoed if one did not manage before, but should not plan a kitchen shopping day (a new fridge, new cutlery) for Hol HaMoed.
  • The difference: food itself may be deliberately left for the moed; preparations for food may be done on the moed only if not deliberately left until then.

In one line

Kitchen appliances and utensils may be fixed or bought on Hol HaMoed, even professionally, as long as this was not deliberately left until then.
