Topic: What may be done with oil and wicks left over after Hanukkah

Daily Halacha: Hanukkah

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Oil set aside for the Menorah may not be used for any personal purpose, not for cooking and not even for Shabbat candles, since Hanukkah lights may not be used for benefit while Shabbat candles are for our enjoyment.
  • This applies only to oil meant to burn during the required half hour that was not used, for example when the lights had to be put out, or went out by accident, within the first half hour.
  • Oil poured in beyond the half hour may be used freely, since one needs to light for half an hour at most.
  • A bottle of oil set aside for the Menorah, but not poured in, may be used.
  • The wicks are tashmishei misvah, not objects of kedushah like Tefillin, a Sefer Torah or a Mezuzah, which need genizah. They may be thrown away; some like to keep and burn them, but that is not halachically necessary.

In one line

Leftover Hanukkah oil and wicks may be used or thrown away, except oil poured in for the first half hour that did not burn.
