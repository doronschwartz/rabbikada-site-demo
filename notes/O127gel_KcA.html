Topic: The status of the 17th of Tammuz and the five events it commemorates

Sources: The Prophet Zekhariah; the Mishnah; the Gemara

Daily Halacha: The Three Weeks, Wednesday 23 June 2021

The points

  • Zekhariah speaks of a fast in four months: Tishrei (Som Gedaliah), Tevet (the 10th), Av (Tisha BeAv) and Tammuz (the 17th, this coming Sunday).
  • Of the six fasts of the year, with Yom Kippur and Taanit Esther, Yom Kippur is the most important and Taanit Esther the least, far less of an obligation.
  • These four are from the words of the Prophets: neither biblical nor rabbinic but in between, and close in importance to a biblical misvah, like Purim.
  • The Mishnah gives five reasons: Moshe broke the Luhot after the golden calf; in the First Temple the daily Tamid ceased because the siege stopped sheep entering Yerushalayim; in the Second Temple (70 CE) the walls were breached, leading to the destruction three weeks later on Tisha BeAv.
  • Also: before Bar Kokhba's revolt Apostomus publicly burnt the Torah; and an idol was set up in the Beit HaMikdash in First Temple times, most likely by King Menasheh.
  • The Gemara teaches the fast is not an end in itself but a means to introspection: HaKadosh Barukh Hu looks at the Teshuvah, not the fast. One who fasts without reflecting on why we are in exile has missed the point.
