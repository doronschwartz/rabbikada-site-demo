Topic: Whether tefillin are worn on Hol HaMoed, and how the custom developed

Sources: The Gemara; the Baal Halachot Gedolot; the Rashba; the Rosh; Tosafot; Rambam; the Rif; the Bet Yoseph; Shulhan Arukh; the Zohar; the Rema; the Meiri

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Tuesday 9 September 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Gemara debates tefillin on Shabbat and Yom Tov. The accepted ruling is that they are not worn, either because the verse "miyamim yamimah" excludes some days, or because Shabbat and Yom Tov are themselves an ot (sign) and need not the ot of tefillin. A berakhah on them would be in vain.
  • Hol HaMoed is part of the festival, yet some melakhah is permitted. The Baal Halachot Gedolot (a Gaon of about the 8th century) and some Rishonim, including the Rashba, held that tefillin are not worn.
  • Many Rishonim, including the Rosh and Tosafot, held they are worn: some melakhah is permitted, and on the view that the prohibition is only rabbinic all melakhah is permitted from the Torah; and the Gemara allows writing tefillin and mezuzot on Hol HaMoed.
  • Rambam excludes only Shabbat and Yom Tov. The Bet Yoseph infers from his limiting the writing of tefillin to one who needs it for his livelihood that Rambam held they are not worn. The Rif also mentions only Shabbat and Yom Tov.
  • The Shulhan Arukh rules clearly not to wear them, because the Zohar speaks very sharply against it. The Bet Yoseph: since our Gemara is not explicit, who can go against Rabbi Shimon bar Yohai?
  • The Rema writes that the Ashkenazi minhag is to wear them, with the berakhah said quietly. Later poskim say to wear them without a berakhah, so as not to enter a dispute.
  • The Bet Yoseph heard that Sepharadim once wore them like the Rosh and stopped on finding the Zohar; the Meiri testifies that the Sepharadi minhag was not to wear them. One follows minhag: Sepharadim do not wear them; Ashkenazim today have different minhagim.

Sources quoted

  • The Rosh and Tosafot: tefillin are worn; the Baal Halachot Gedolot and the Rashba: they are not.
  • The Bet Yoseph and Shulhan Arukh: not worn, in deference to the Zohar.
  • The Rema: the Ashkenazi minhag to wear them with a quiet berakhah.

In one line

Sepharadim do not wear tefillin on Hol HaMoed, following the Shulhan Arukh and the Zohar, while Ashkenazim who wear them do so without a berakhah.
