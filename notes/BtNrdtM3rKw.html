Topic: The procedure and mechanism of Prozbol

Sources: Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Shemitah & Prozbol, Monday 16 May 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Loans made in the cycle may not be claimed after Rosh Hashanah 5783. Since this is rabbinic today, a rabbinic enactment, Prozbol, can address it, just as an eruv works only where carrying is forbidden rabbinically.
  • In a Prozbol the lender hands over his loans to a Bet Din of three dayanim, so that he may claim them whenever he wishes after the seventh year, in the presence of two witnesses who sign.
  • The halacha is that the lender need not appear before the Bet Din; he states to which Bet Din he hands his loans, before two kosher witnesses. A rabbi of Damascus is recorded advising people to hand their loans to the Bet Din of Safed in this way.
  • The mechanism: the Bet Din has the power to treat the borrower's assets, to the value of the loan, as transferred to the lender, so afterwards the lender collects what is already his.
  • So the Bet Din need not collect the debts, and after the seventh year the lender may claim directly from the borrower.
  • The borrowers need not be named; handing over all one's loans suffices.
  • The Shulhan Arukh rules it must be a Bet Din hashuv, three rabbanim who understand Prozbol, unlike annulling vows, where three laymen may serve.

In one line

A Prozbol is a declaration before two witnesses handing one's loans to a prominent Bet Din, which lets the lender collect them himself after Shemitah.
