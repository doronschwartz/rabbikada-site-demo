Topic: Whether to give one large gift or many smaller ones

Sources: Rambam on the Mishnah, Pirkei Avot; the Torah ("what he lacks")

Daily Halacha: Tsedakah & Maaser, Thursday 3 March 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Is it better to give a large sum to one poor person or to divide it among many?
  • The Mishnah in Avot says "all is according to the majority of deeds". The Rambam explains: not according to the size of the deed, but the number of deeds.
  • So, he writes, 100 coins each to 10 people is better than 1,000 to one person, because sedakah is meant to ingrain care for others, and each act of giving refines one's character further.
  • The caveat: the Rambam speaks of giving each person a substantial amount that will genuinely benefit him. No one holds that many almost insignificant gifts are better.
  • Faced with 100 pounds to one person or one pound to 100 people, he believes the Rambam would say to give the 100 pounds to one, since the Torah commands giving "what he lacks", and a tiny sum covers nothing.

In one line

Many gifts are better than one, provided each is large enough to make a real difference to the poor person.
