Topic: The week of Tisha BeAv when the fast is postponed from Shabbat to Sunday

Sources: The Gemara; Shulhan Arukh; the Ben Ish Hai

Daily Halacha: The Three Weeks, Monday 1 August 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Tisha BeAv can fall only on Shabbat, Sunday, Tuesday or Thursday, and on Shabbat it is postponed, so half the time it is kept on Sunday.
  • The week of Tisha BeAv is the most severe period after the day itself: the Gemara forbids laundry and wearing freshly laundered clothes, and haircuts, and bathing is customarily forbidden. On a Tuesday these apply Sunday and Monday; on a Thursday, Sunday to Wednesday.
  • When it falls on Sunday itself, as last year, all Sepharadim agree there is no week of Tisha BeAv.
  • This year the 9th of Av is Shabbat, postponed to Sunday. The Shulhan Arukh's primary opinion treats it as falling on Sunday, so there is no week of Tisha BeAv; a second opinion counts the whole previous week except Thursday and Friday, to prepare for Shabbat.
  • The poskim and the custom follow the first opinion: this year one may bathe normally, do laundry and wear freshly laundered clothes, cut hair and shave, right up to Tisha BeAv.
  • The Ben Ish Hai writes it is not in good taste to come to Tisha BeAv just shaved, so one should refrain on Friday; strictly it is permitted until Shabbat.
  • For Ashkenazim these are customs, not the Gemara's law: haircuts the whole Three Weeks, laundry and bathing (with some leniencies) the Nine Days.

When Tisha BeAv is postponed to Sunday, Sepharadim have no week of Tisha BeAv and may bathe, launder and cut hair until Shabbat.
