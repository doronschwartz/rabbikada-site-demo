Topic: The message of Pesah Sheni and the custom of eating Massah

Sources: The Torah; the Gemara; the Hazon Ish

Daily Halacha: Sefirat HaOmer, Monday 26 April 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The points

  • The 14th of Iyar is Pesah Sheni. People who were tamei (from a dead body, or from carrying the coffin of Yoseph; the Gemara gives two reasons) could not bring the Korban Pesah and asked Moshe why they should lose out.
  • They did not accept "no", because the Korban Pesah is one of only two misvot, with Brit Milah, whose omission carries karet.
  • HaKadosh Barukh Hu gave a second Pesah on the 14th of Iyar, the only misvah with a second chance. It is the 14th because the Korban was offered the day before Pesah.
  • The lesson: even when unable to do a misvah through no fault of one's own (ones), one should still want it. Serving Hashem needs tenacity.
  • Tahanun is not said. Some, more among Ashkenazim and Hasidim, eat Massah: last night, today, or tonight (the night of the 15th, when the Korban was eaten).
  • Sepharadim never really had this custom, and the Hazon Ish opposed it as having no source. He says there is nothing wrong with eating Massah, but it was not the Sepharadi custom.

Sources quoted

  • The Torah: the impure men who approached Moshe, and the second Pesah.
  • The Gemara: two reasons for their impurity.
  • The Hazon Ish: against eating Massah on Pesah Sheni, as having no source and adding misvot.

In one line

Pesah Sheni marks the will to do a misvah even when one cannot, and eating Massah on it is a custom Sepharadim did not have.
