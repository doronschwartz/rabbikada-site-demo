Topic: Why the lulav, hadas and aravah are mukseh on Shabbat of Sukkot but the etrog is not

Daily Halacha: Sukkot

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Hakhamim decreed that the Arba Minim are not taken on Shabbat, as with the shofar, lest one carry them. So the lulav, hadas and aravah become mukseh.
  • All seven days the Arba Minim are set aside for the misvah (huksah lemisvato). One may not smell the hadassim of one's lulav until after Sukkot.
  • The etrog's primary use is eating, so what is set aside is eating it. Even an etrog replaced during Hol HaMoed by a nicer one may not be eaten all seven days.
  • Smelling it is strictly permitted throughout Sukkot (what berakhah to say is discussed). Because it has this use, the etrog is not mukseh on Shabbat; one who finds his etrog box at his seat may move it.
  • Some held it is mukseh mehamat hesron kis, because it is so valuable nowadays, but the halacha does not follow this.
  • People do not smell it today simply because they do not know; that does not change the halacha.

In one line

On Shabbat of Sukkot the lulav, hadas and aravah may not be moved, but the etrog may.
