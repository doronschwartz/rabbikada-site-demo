Topic: Grinding, mashing, grating and fine chopping on Yom Tov

Sources: The Hakhamim; the Rama; the Ashkenazi poskim

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Monday 23 June 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Tohen on Shabbat forbids grinding, crushing, mashing and cutting very small.
  • On Yom Tov the Hakhamim forbade grinding flour, normally done in bulk and for the long term. All other grinding in the kitchen is permitted.
  • So on Yom Tov one may in the regular way mash bananas, potatoes or avocado, crush spices, grate vegetables, and cut salad very fine (dak dak), all of which are restricted on Shabbat.
  • First point: use a simple grater or crusher, not an industrial machine, since the Hakhamim were concerned one might prepare beyond Yom Tov itself. Simple implements are permitted and not muksah. Anything in regular kitchen use for crushing, grating, cutting or mashing is permitted; only industrial machines are excluded.
  • Second point: Ashkenazi poskim recommend a small shinui to recall it is Yom Tov and not prepare for after it, as the Rama rules, for example grating onto a tray rather than a plate. Sepharadim need not.
  • Even Sepharadim should use a small shinui for a food that could have been prepared before Yom Tov with no difference in taste, such as salt; not for avocado or banana, which are not comparable when mashed in advance.

Sources quoted

  • The Rama: Ashkenazim grind with a small shinui on Yom Tov.

In one line

On Yom Tov all household mashing, grating, crushing and fine chopping is permitted with simple tools, though not grinding flour, with a shinui for Ashkenazim.
