Topic: Taking Hallah after baking, and what to do when the separated Hallah gets mixed back in

Sources: Shulhan Arukh; the Rama; Hazon Ovadiah

Daily Halacha: Hallah, Thursday 7 July 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Hallah should be taken while it is still dough, but the Shulhan Arukh rules that one may still take it after baking, as long as it has not been eaten: separate a piece, say the berakhah and "harei zo Hallah", and discard it.
  • Outside Israel one may even take it from what is left at the end, such as the last piece of ten hallot eaten over Shabbat, with a berakhah, since it works retroactively. In Israel one should not rely on this.
  • If the separated Hallah got mixed back into the food, it is nullified only in 101 times its amount. If there is not 101 times as much, or one is unsure, the food cannot be eaten.
  • The Rama gives the solution, which is the halacha: separating Hallah is like a vow, which can be annulled. Before three people one says that had one known it would get mixed in, one would not have separated it, and they say "it is permitted to you".
  • The first piece retroactively stops being Hallah, and one takes a new piece. Hazon Ovadiah says no new berakhah, relying on the first.
  • This works even after baking.

Sources quoted

  • Shulhan Arukh: Hallah may be taken after baking if not yet eaten.
  • Rama: annulling the separation before three.
  • Hazon Ovadiah: no second berakhah.

In one line

If the separated Hallah gets mixed back in, one annuls the separation before three people and takes Hallah again without a berakhah.
