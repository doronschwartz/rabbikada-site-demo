Recap from Parts 1–3

The Definition

Bitachon = complete reliance on Hakadosh Baruch Hu, the Creator of all, to whose will everything is subservient. Living this brings security and serenity (betach). We use the intermediaries Hashem created — but place no reliance on them.

The Turning Point — From Theory to Practice

ולאחר שהבינות ותפסת את כל הדברים האלה, דע כי הביטחון נחלק לכמה וכמה חלקים Now that you've grasped all this [theory], know that bitachon divides into many parts — regarding what one trusts Hashem for, and the outcome sought.

Until now: theoretical bitachon. Now: practical scenarios — when and how bitachon is actually employed.

A Caveat on the Difficulty

אך הדברים הללו קשים לביאור, וידיעת סודם בשלמות... נשגבת מבינתנו

Understanding precisely how to apply bitachon in every scenario is beyond us — even the prophets couldn't fully grasp God's ways. Asaf said ואחשבה לדעת זאת, עמל הוא בעיני ("when I sought to understand this, it was wearying"); Hashem told Moshe וחנותי את אשר אחון ("I show favor to whom I show favor"). So Rabbi Avraham offers general categories and direction, not an exhaustive system.

The Structure — Five (× Two = Ten) Categories

The Two Master Axes

Axis 1 — Purpose: Bitachon is used either

  • להרחקת הנזקים — to avoid harm/danger, OR
  • להשגת התועלת — to attain a benefit/goal

Axis 2 — Degree of necessity of the goal/harm:

  • הכרחית — essential/critical
  • בלתי הכרחית אך כמעט הכרחית — non-essential but nearly so (important)
  • בלתי הכרחית לחלוטין — completely non-essential (a luxury)

The Five Categories (הביטחון יפיק כוח עשרה דברים)

Two Prerequisites Before the Categories

Prerequisite 1 — You Cannot Use Bitachon Without Doing Hashem's Will

המבוא לביטחון הוא השמיעה בקול השם יתעלה ודבקות בתורתו והישמר מפני מרידה The gateway to bitachon is obeying Hashem, cleaving to His Torah, and guarding against rebellion.

There is no point claiming bitachon — however sincere — while not observing the mitzvot. Doing Hashem's will is the alef-bet prerequisite for trusting in Him and gaining serenity of mind.

Yirmiyahu's Castigation

אל תבטחו לכם על דברי השקר לאמר: היכל ה' היכל ה' היכל ה' המה

Don't trust the falsehood of "the Temple, the Temple, the Temple" — i.e., I'll do the three pilgrimages (Pesach, Shavuot, Sukkot) and bring sacrifices, then do as I please all year. It doesn't work that way:

הגנוב, רצוח, ונאוף... ובאתם ועמדתם לפני? Can you steal, murder, commit adultery, swear falsely, serve idols — then come stand before Me [in the Temple]?

Only כי אם היטיב תיטיבו את דרכיכם — if you genuinely improve your ways — will Hashem dwell with you.

This is the obvious-but-essential reminder: there's no practical bitachon without fulfilling Hashem's will.

Prerequisite 2 — Spiritual Bitachon Is Always More "Employable" Than Physical

כל ביטחון שתכליתו דתית הרי הוא נכון ושלם מן הביטחון שתכליתו גשמית Bitachon for a spiritual purpose is more valid and complete than for a physical purpose — even bitachon for a non-essential spiritual matter outranks bitachon for an essential physical matter.

The Logic

It makes more sense to employ bitachon to fulfill Hashem's will than to employ it for Hashem to fulfill your will. Physical bitachon is for me — my body, myself. Spiritual bitachon is for the will of Hashem — so even a non-critical spiritual aim trumps a critical physical one.

The Analogy — Two People of Bitachon

Person

What They Trust For

Purpose

Verdict

Person 1

Basic survival — bread, clothing, shelter

Purely mundane — not to die of hunger/cold, not to beg charity. Not in order to serve Hashem or learn Torah

Valid, but lower

Person 2

Wealth and authority (non-essential)

Spiritual — to give tzedakah, sustain the needy and Torah-scholars (like Ovadiah, who hid and fed 100 prophets from Izevel); to use power to restrain the wicked, protect the oppressed, establish justice

(We don't judge Person 1 — someone who can't make ends meet, simply trusting Hashem for food, is in a hard place. But it's not the same level.) (Person 2's intent must be genuine — "you can fool yourself, but you can't fool Hashem.")

The Highest Form — Essential AND Spiritual: Yaakov's Bitachon

אם יהיה אלהים עמדי ושמרני בדרך הזה... ונתן לי לחם לאכל ובגד ללבש... ושבתי בשלום אל בית אבי

Yaakov asked for food, clothing, and safe return — all essential — and his purpose was והיה ה' לי לאלהים ("that Hashem be my God"): so he could be free to serve Hashem undistracted by material struggle. Essential + spiritual = the most praiseworthy bitachon of all.

The Practical Takeaway — Reframing Changes Everything

A sick person praying "Hashem, heal me so I won't suffer pain" → bitachon for a mundane matter. The same person praying "Hashem, heal me so I can keep going to shul, helping the poor, serving You" → transformed into spiritual bitachon.

The shift in focus changes the entire quality of the bitachon. This is why focus matters when employing it.

CATEGORY ONE — Bitachon in Serious (Life-Threatening) Danger

הראשון בהם הוא הרחקת נזק חמור The first is averting serious harm.

What Counts as "Serious Harm"

  • A life-threatening illness (חולאים הקשים)
  • Sudden dangers (סכנות מקריות) — accident, attack
  • War / civil war; falling into quicksand (טיט היון); storms, thunder (סופות רעמים); earthquakes (רעידת אדמה) — natural forces beyond one's control

These are critical, death-involved, uncontrollable situations, for the sake of survival (a mundane purpose).

The Method — How to Employ Bitachon Here

Step 1 — Turn Your Heart to Hashem

שיתלה האדם את לבו בשם יתעלה, ויפתח בו שירחיק ממנו את הנזק ויצילו מן הפגעים — כי כל הסיבות כפופות לחפצו ורצונו Cast your heart on Hashem, trusting He will remove the danger and save you — for all causes are subject to His will.

Step 2 — Recall Hashem's Promises Through Tehillim

Tehillim 91 (יושב בסתר עליון) — the "song against perils":

  • (Said Shabbat morning, Motzaei Shabbat, and at a levaya — to protect against the spiritual dangers of the soul's ascent.)
  • Opening (the structure of trust): אמר לה' מחסי ומצודתי, אלהי אבטח בו — "I say to Hashem: my refuge and fortress, my God in whom I trust"
  • Middle: כי אתה ה' מחסי, עליון שמת מעונך — "You, Hashem, are my refuge"
  • End (the reason): כי בי חשק ואפלטהו, אשגבהו כי ידע שמי — "Because he desired Me I will rescue him... because he knows My name" — i.e., a relationship, a brit with Hashem

Tehillim 18 (David's song when saved from Shaul):

  • Opening (his love and trust): ארחמך ה' חזקי, ה' סלעי ומצודתי ומפלטי, צורי אחסה בו
  • Middle (why he was saved — his righteousness): כי שמרתי דרכי ה'... כל משפטיו לנגדי... ואהי תמים עמו — Hashem saved him because he kept Hashem's ways

How Tehillim actually works: NOT a mystical/magical effect of reciting the words — but to remind ourselves who truly governs the world and to reset our priorities. When anxious, open 91, read it (even in English), understand it. (Anxiety often stems from a lack of bitachon; bitachon can help overcome it — though this is not to say every anxiety sufferer lacks bitachon, nor a substitute for treating genuine medical conditions.)

Step 4 — STILL Employ Every Natural Means

מכל מקום ברי כי לשם הרחקת הפגעים הרעים, יש להשתמש באמצעים שתיקן השם יתעלה להרחקתם Nonetheless — to avert harm, one must use the means Hashem established for averting it.

A sick person must find the best doctor (within reason), follow the best diet (within reason — not at the expense of Shabbat meals, etc.) — together with the trust and prayer above.

The Torah's Own Proofs — Trust AND Act

Even though Hashem is the ultimate cause, the Torah itself commands natural action:

Hashem's Role (verse)

Yet the Torah Commands

אני אמית ואחיה — "I bring death and life"

ועשית מעקה לגגך — build a parapet so no one falls (Ki Teitzei)

מחצתי ואני ארפא — "I wound and I heal"

ורפא ירפא — a doctor shall heal (the license to practice medicine)

The Kohen anointed for war says אל ירך לבבכם... אל תיראו — trust Hashem, say Shema

Yet ופקדו שרי צבאות בראש העם — appoint generals; ובנית מצור — build siege-works (employ war tactics)

כי לא בחרבם ירשו ארץ — "not by their sword did they inherit the land" (Bnei Korach)

Yet ויכהו ישראל לפי חרב — Israel struck Sichon by the sword

The resolution: "Not by their sword" means they didn't rely on the sword alone — they relied on Hashem's providence (כי ימינך וזרועך ואור פניך, כי רציתם) while wielding it. Build balconies, use doctors, raise armies, wield swords — but know the outcome is from Hashem.

The Snakebite / Cancer Paradigm

אם קצצך עקרב או הכישך נחש — if a scorpion stings or a snake bites you (or, bar minan, a sudden cancer diagnosis):

  • לא רק תסרוט את מקום הנשיכה ותגום תריאק — don't only lance the wound and take the antidote (do that, certainly!)
  • אלא עליך לבטוח במי שבידו נפש כל חי — but also trust in the One in whose hand is all life

ובתוך כדי שתיית הצרי ולקיחת התרופה — תבטח בה' While taking the medicine / undergoing the procedure — trust in Hashem that healing comes from Him.

This does not diminish appreciation for the doctors and medicines — on the contrary, it should increase our recognition of how wondrously Hashem created these means.

The Yehi Ratzon Over Medicine (Gemara Berachot, codified in Shulchan Aruch)

יהי רצון מלפניך ה' אלהי שיהא עסק זה לי לרפואה "May it be Your will, Hashem, that this engagement be for my healing." (Rabbi Kada: rarely seen practiced today — seemingly forgotten.) It connects the physical act to the recognition that healing comes from Hashem.

When There Are No Means Left — Pure Bitachon

אם תקרה למקום שלא תמצא בו דבר מרפא — if you reach a place with no remedy (bitten in the desert with no antidote; ill with no hospital): אז לא נשאר עליך אלא ביטחון בלבד — then only bitachon remains.

The Sailor in the Storm

מי שהפליג ללב ים, וזעף עליו הים, ונתקו פרשיו, ואבדה עצת מנהיגי הספינה וקברניטיה — לא נשאר אלא ביטחון בלבד

The sails are torn, the sailors and captain are at a loss (a "Titanic" scenario) — only bitachon remains. Hashem knows the magnitude of the danger and, in His mercy, can save where all hope is lost — as He saved Chananyah, Mishael, and Azaryah from the furnace, and Daniel from the lions' den, biskhut godel tzidkatam (in the merit of their great righteousness): אלהי שלח מלאכה וסגר פום אריותא.

A sobering note: Rabbi Avraham's own uncle, Rabbi David (the Rambam's brother), died at sea — and the Rambam grieved deeply for years. So bitachon does not always "work" as hoped. Why? — the next paragraph.

Why the Righteous Sometimes Are Not Saved

ויש עוונות מסתירים ומעכבים חסדו של אלהים — there are sins that conceal and block God's kindness; or, טעם כמוס — a hidden reason beyond human grasp.

So ויבא הדבר שהוא ירא ממנו — the very thing one feared (and trusted Hashem to prevent) sometimes happens.

The Examples

  • The Ten Harugei Malchut (the ten martyrs, read on Tisha B'Av) — killed by wicked Romans
  • Uriah killed by Yehoyakim; Zechariah killed by Yoash (whose own father had saved Yoash); other prophets killed by wicked Israelites

אין סיבת הדבר קוצר יד ההשגחה — it is not because Hashem is unable to save — אלא סיבות נסתרות בחכמתו — but hidden reasons in His wisdom, judgments beyond human comprehension.

The Gemara Story — Lulianus and Pappos (Turianus/Trajan)

The wicked Roman ruler Turianus seized two righteous Jews (Lulianus and Pappos) and taunted:

"If you are the people of Chananyah, Mishael, and Azaryah — let your God save you from my hand as He saved them from Nebuchadnezzar!"

They answered:

חנניה מישאל ועזריה צדיקים גמורים היו, ונבוכדנצר מלך הגון היה וראוי לעשות נס על ידו — ואנו נתחייבנו הריגה למקום "They were perfectly righteous and Nebuchadnezzar was a worthy king for a miracle. But WE have been found liable for death by Heaven. If you don't kill us, Heaven has many other agents. Hashem has merely handed us to YOUR hand — to exact our blood from you."

Two Versions of the Ending

  • Our Gemara: He killed them — but then officials came from Rome and killed Turianus (his brain was crushed).
  • The Be'er Avraham's text: reads that they were saved in their merit (שבזכותן ניצולו) — evidently he had a different girsa.

The depth here: their knowledge of providence, their acceptance of Heaven's judgment, and a kiddush Hashem they were not even commanded to make.

A Note on Enemies (Quick Halachic Aside)

When enemies seek your life:

Enemy

When Not Imminently Pursuing

Reasoning

Non-Jewish enemy sworn to kill you

Strike first if you have the ability (כמו בימי דוד) — combine bitachon with military action

(Applied: Israel's neighbors sworn to its destruction — 100% permitted to act first)

Jewish enemy not actively pursuing

Do NOT attack — trust Hashem, escape elsewhere

Like David, who twice caught Shaul and spared him

(When someone breaks in actively threatening your life — בא במחתרת — you may kill even a Jewish pursuer to save yourself. The above is about non-imminent enemies.)

The Sage's Answer (Tehillim 37)

Asked whether to get informers/slanderers killed:

דום לה' והתחולל לו — read homiletically: be silent before Hashem, and He will make them into חללים (corpses); השכם והערב עליהם בבית המדרש והם כלין מאליהם — pray morning and evening, and they will perish on their own.

Spiritual Enemies — Different Rules

For those who endanger the faith — machtiei harabim (those who cause the masses to sin), mosrim (informers), apikorsim (heretics) publicly desecrating Hashem's name — דינם כדין הגויים; whoever can eliminate them is praiseworthy (in eras when this applied).

  • If you lack the ability (e.g., in exile, under hostile government): only bitachon remains — pray, and tolerate the situation (יסבול את מצב הדחוק).
  • The Chazon Ish: nowadays we don't act against heretics — that was for eras when Hashem's providence was more revealed.
  • Rav Kook: today the only way to draw them back is with love.

The Beautiful Gemara — Bruria and Rabbi Meir

Rabbi Meir was tormented by biryonei (lawless bandits) in his neighborhood and prayed for them to die. His wife Bruria challenged him:

מי כתיב "יתמו חוטאים"? "חטאים" כתיב! — Does it say "may sinners be consumed"? It says "sins"! Look at the verse's end: ורשעים עוד אינם — "and the wicked are no more" — because they will have repented. אלא בעי רחמי עלייהו דלהדרי בתיובתא — pray for them to do teshuvah (so the wicked are "no more").

Rabbi Meir prayed for their repentance — and they repented. A model of true yirat shamayim.

Summary

The Framework

  • Bitachon now moves from theory to practice — but precise application in all scenarios is beyond full human grasp; we work in general categories
  • Five categories (avoid serious harm / avoid lesser harm / attain essential / attain important / attain luxury) × physical or spiritual = TEN

Two Prerequisites

  1. No bitachon without doing Hashem's will (against Yirmiyahu's "Heichal Hashem" falsehood)
  2. Spiritual bitachon always outranks physical — even non-essential spiritual over essential physical; Yaakov's (essential + spiritual) is the highest; reframing a request from mundane to spiritual transforms its quality

Category One — Serious Danger

  1. Turn your heart to Hashem (all causes are subject to His will)
  2. Recall His promises through Tehillim 91 and 18 — to reset priorities, not as magic
  3. Pray (tefillah, tachanun)
  4. Use every natural means — best doctor, diet, tactics — the Torah itself commands parapets, doctors, armies, swords, while trusting Hashem
  5. The Yehi Ratzon over medicine connects the act to Hashem
  6. When no means remain — pure bitachon (Chananyah/Mishael/Azaryah; Daniel) — though it doesn't always "work" (the Rambam's brother at sea; the martyrs), for hidden reasons beyond human grasp (Lulianus & Pappos)
  7. Enemies: strike first against a non-Jewish enemy if able; spare a non-pursuing Jewish enemy; for spiritual enemies, act only where able and where they can't be returned — otherwise pray (Bruria: pray for the sins to end, not the sinners)
