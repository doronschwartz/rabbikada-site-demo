Topic: The second and third reasons: the death of Ezra and the siege of Yerushalayim

Sources: Megillat Taanit; the book of Ezra; the Selihot of Asarah BeTevet

Daily Halacha: Asarah BeTevet

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The points

  • Second reason: on 9 Tevet Ezra the Scribe died. In many ways he repeated the work of Moshe: he brought the people back from Bavel after the destruction of the First Temple, ended the intermarriage of the exile, and taught them what had been forgotten (the verse says they did not know about Sukkot). Without him the Mesorah would have been lost.
  • Third and main reason: on 10 Tevet, about 2,400 years ago, Nevukhadnessar of Bavel laid siege to Yerushalayim. The prophet Yirmiyahu had long prophesied destruction and called for teshuvah.
  • The siege lasted two and a half years until the walls were breached on 9 Tammuz, and about a month later, on 9 Av, the First Temple was destroyed. So 10 Tevet begins the process of the destruction.
  • The Selihot of Asarah BeTevet mention all three reasons. The whole stretch from the 8th to the 10th of Tevet is a dark time; the fast primarily marks the siege, and also the death of Ezra and the translation of the Torah.

In one line

Asarah BeTevet mainly mourns the siege that began the Temple's destruction, together with the death of Ezra on the 9th and the Greek translation on the 8th.
