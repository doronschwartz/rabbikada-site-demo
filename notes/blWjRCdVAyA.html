Topic: Haircuts and laundry in the week of Tisha BeAv

Sources: The Mishnah and Gemara

Daily Halacha: The Three Weeks, Monday 17 July 2023

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Restrictions grow stricter as Tisha BeAv approaches. When it falls on or is postponed to Sunday there is no week of Tisha BeAv; this year it falls on Thursday, so the week is Sunday to Wednesday, the longest possible.
  • Three restrictions apply that week, two from the Mishnah and Talmud and one more a custom.
  • Haircuts: Ashkenazim refrain from the 17th of Tammuz, but by the Gemara it is only this week. Neither hair nor beard may be cut or shaved, and by most opinions and custom this applies to women too.
  • The custom extends to not giving children haircuts, even under Bar or Bat Misvah. Some say that at a Brit Milah that day the father, sandak and mohel may cut their hair.
  • Laundry: one may not launder, even for after Tisha BeAv, nor wear freshly laundered clothes, which give a comfort that distracts from mourning. Underwear was never included; it is basic hygiene.
  • Since today we change shirts daily, the practice arose of wearing clothes before the week for half an hour or an hour so they are no longer fresh and may be worn during it.
  • Ashkenazim are strict from Rosh Hodesh, but by the Gemara, and in Sepharadi custom, it is only this week. Washing is next.

Sources quoted

  • The Mishnah and Gemara: haircuts and laundry forbidden in the week of Tisha BeAv.

In one line

In the week of Tisha BeAv no one, including women and children, cuts hair, and one neither launders nor wears freshly laundered clothes, underwear apart.
