Topic: When one may speak between the berakhah on the shofar and the end of the 100 blasts

Sources: The Arizal; the Hakhamim (Musaf blasts)

Daily Halacha: Rosh Hashanah, Tuesday 20 September 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • No speaking during the first 30 blasts. The Arizal's custom of confessing between the sets is not recommended unless it is one's established custom.
  • The misvah is not complete until the blasts in the repetition of the Musaf Amidah, which the Hakhamim instituted, and the berakhah must cover them too. So one should refrain from all idle talk from the berakhah until the end of the repetition, including while the Sefer Torah is returned.
  • A needed berakhah, such as Asher Yasar, or a drink with its berakhot when feeling faint, is not idle talk and is allowed.
  • After those 60, one has fulfilled both the Torah and the rabbinic obligation, so strictly one may talk before the last 40, our custom. The correct practice is still to refrain until all 100.
  • If needed, for instance when greeted or asked how one is, one may answer after the repetition, though it is good to refrain.
  • Even prayers such as the Yehi Rason printed after the first 30 in some old prayer books should be avoided until after the blasts.

In one line

From the berakhah on the shofar until the Musaf repetition one must not speak, and ideally not until all 100 blasts are done.
