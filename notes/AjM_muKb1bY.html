Topic: When a Prozbol may be made, which loans it covers, and doing it at twilight on Erev Rosh Hashanah

Sources: The Gemara ("Stam halvaah shloshim yom"); Hakham Ovadiah Yoseph

Daily Halacha: Shemitah & Prozbol, Wednesday 18 May 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • A Prozbol may be made at any time, even today; it need not be in the last month or on the day before Rosh Hashanah.
  • But it covers only loans made up to the time it is written. One who will lend more in the coming months should wait until the month before Rosh Hashanah.
  • A default loan is payable only after 30 days ("stam halvaah shloshim yom"). So a loan made, say, on 15 Elul is payable on 15 Tishrei, in the eighth year, and is not released; no new Prozbol is needed for loans made within 30 days of Rosh Hashanah.
  • If the lender stipulated repayment sooner, for example within seven days, so that it falls due before Rosh Hashanah, it does need a Prozbol.
  • Once made, a Prozbol continues to cover those loans even through the next Shemitah, seven years later.
  • The question of time zones (a lender in America when it is already Rosh Hashanah in Israel) is left for the next class.
  • One who realises before sunset on Erev Rosh Hashanah, even in synagogue, may make it verbally before two kosher witnesses, handing his loans to a Bet Din of three. After nightfall it is too late.
  • Between sunset and nightfall, a time of doubt, Hakham Ovadiah rules that it still works, since the whole matter is rabbinic today.

In one line

A Prozbol may be made any time before nightfall on Erev Rosh Hashanah, but covers only loans made up to that point, apart from loans due after Rosh Hashanah.
