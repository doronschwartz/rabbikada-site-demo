Recap: Chapters 26 and 27

The end of Chapter 26: ten verbs of motion said of HaShem (stand, sit, walk, go and the rest), and a tenth, סבב, to go around, which Rambam left for us to explain.

Chapter 27: Onkelos, a thousand years before Rambam, translated these expressions with words like שכינה and “created light”, insisting that HaKadosh Barukh Hu has no physical form. The exception is a prophetic vision, as in Parashat Vayiggash: there he leaves the language as it is, because it only happened in a vision.

Last week we also began Chapter 28. Why does Rambam turn to a body part, the foot, in the middle of the verbs? The simplest answer: this chapter is about Onkelos's reading of a verse, and Rambam's own, so it flows straight on from Chapter 27.

“Tonight is a deep chapter, although very understandable. It just needs concentration.”

The Three Meanings of רגל

רגל הוא מונח רב משמעי: the word has three meanings.

  1. A foot or leg: שמה של רגל הרגל התחתונה.
  2. A following: צא אתה וכל העם אשר ברגליך, the people who walk in your footsteps. Not needed tonight, but worth knowing.
  3. A cause, a basis, a footing: ויברך ה׳ אותך לרגלי, Yaakov tells Lavan: HaShem blessed you because of me. כי דבר שהוא בגלל דבר אחר, הרי הוא בא בסיבת הדבר: if X happens because of Y, then Y is the cause of X.

Why should “foot” mean “cause”? Because the foot is the base of the body. It carries everything above it. In English too we speak of the footing of a thing.

In this week's parashah: after Yaakov and Esav embrace, Esav wants to travel together. Yaakov answers ואני אתנהלה לאטי לרגל המלאכה אשר לפני ולרגל הילדים: I will go slowly because of the flocks and because of the children. לרגל here means on account of, on the footing of. So they go two different ways.

(Last week we also saw the difference between בגלל and לרגל.)

“His Feet Will Stand on the Mount of Olives” (Zechariah)

Before the main verse, Rambam gives an example of meaning three.

ועמדו רגליו ביום ההוא על הר הזיתים: in the days of Mashiah, HaShem's feet will stand on the Mount of Olives. “How anthropomorphic is that?”

Rambam: הוא קיום סיבותיו. It means His causes will be established: הנפלאות שיופיעו אז באותו מקום, שהוא הסיבה להם, כלומר, העושה אותם. The miracles that appear there will show that He is their cause, the One doing them.

Two words, both already learned

  • עמד, said of HaKadosh Barukh Hu, means lasting, permanent. We revised it last week.
  • רגליו here is meaning three: His causes, His footing.

So: in the messianic era there will be miracles with long-lasting effects, and it will be plain that HaKadosh Barukh Hu is their cause and basis. Not a physical leg, not a following, but a cause.

Yonatan ben Uziel: the same idea by another road

ופירוש יונתן בן עוזיאל עליו השלום קרוב לזה. Yonatan ben Uziel translated the Prophets into Aramaic, and renders the verse ויתגלי בגבורתיה ביומא ההוא על טור זיתיא: His might will be revealed on that day.

וכך הוא מתרגם כל אבר שביצוע מעשהו בא ממנו: גבורתא. Whenever a limb of HaShem is mentioned, a hand or a foot, he translates it as “strength”, כי הכוונה בכולם היא מעשים הנרצים לו.

The Main Verse: Mishpatim

Around the time of Matan Torah (exactly which day is a discussion among the Rishonim):

ויעלו משה ואהרן נדב ואביהוא ושבעים מזקני ישראל. ויראו את אלהי ישראל ותחת רגליו כמעשה לבנת הספיר וכעצם השמים לטהר.

They saw God, and under His feet was the work of a sapphire brick, like the very heavens for purity. “One of the most obscure verses in the Torah, and perhaps one of the most anthropomorphic.”

Where have we met this verse before?

The Rabbi asked the class: where in the first 28 chapters does Rambam already discuss this verse? A member of the class answered: ויאכלו וישתו, והבטה. Chapter 5, on not penetrating too deeply, with Aristotle and the burning bush. (Asked whether it was connected with Nadav and Avihu's אש זרה: no, not that.) We come back to Chapter 5 at the end.

Onkelos: Removing the Body Twice

פירוש אונקלוס בהם הוא כידוע לך. Known to you how? Rambam expects anyone learning the Moreh to read the parashah every week שנים מקרא ואחד תרגום, as is נפסק. So you already know the Targum.

Onkelos relates רגליו to the throne: ותחות כורסי יקריה, “under the throne of His glory”.

הבן והתפעל כיצד הרחיק אונקלוס מן ההגשמה. See how far he goes.

  1. First removal: “His feet” becomes the feet of a throne. HaShem has no feet.
  2. Second removal: he does not say ותחות כורסיה, “under His throne”. שאלו היה מיחס לו את הכסא במשמעות הראשונה, היה הדבר מחייב שהוא נח על הגוף: God sitting on a throne is still a body. So he writes the throne of His glory, לשכינה שהיא אור נברא, the created light.

What is the אור הנברא? The Rabbi: it can mean an actual, manifested light (“that's how Saadiah understood it”) or a light seen in a vision. Either way, it is created light, the Shekhinah, not God Himself.

Which meaning of רגל does Onkelos use?

The Rabbi asked the class. Not three, and not the “following” of ההולכים ברגליך. It is meaning one: a literal leg, the leg of the throne. “A chair always has legs.” Under His feet means under the feet of the throne of the Shekhinah.

Another example: Beshallah

כי יד על כס יה מלחמה לה׳ בעמלק מדר דר. A hand placed on something is an oath. Onkelos: מן קדם אלהא דשכנתיה על כורסי יקרא. Again the throne is the throne of the Shekhinah, not of God.

וכן אתה מוצא שגור בפי כל האומה: we all speak of the כסא הכבוד, the throne set apart to display the glory of the Shekhinah. Rambam stops himself: יצאנו מטרת הפרק לדבר שיתבאר במקומו, ונחזור אל הפרק. The meaning of כבוד comes in Chapter 64.

What Onkelos Did Not Do

אבל כל מה שעשה הוא שלילת הגשמיות, ולא הבהיר לנו איזה דבר השיגו, ולא מה הכוונה במשל זה.

Onkelos removed the crude reading. He did not tell us what they saw, or what the sapphire means. And that is fine:

  • ואין לו עסק כי אם לבאר פשוטו של פסוק בשלילת הגשמות בלבד. His only task is to translate, and he cannot translate anthropomorphic words literally.
  • משום ששלילת הגשמיות הוא דבר ממוכרחי האמונה. Removing corporeality is a foundation of faith, so he does it consistently.
  • ואילו ביאור משמעות המשל, הרי יש בו סברות. The meaning of the image is open: it could be this, it could be that.
  • הם דברים נסתרים מאוד, והבנתם אינה ממוסדי הדת, וגם אסור ללמדם לכל המון. Deep matters, not foundations, not for the masses.

A rule for learning Onkelos: he gives the simple meaning and keeps us away from heresy. That is all.

ואולם אנחנו, בהתאם למטרת החיבור, אין לנו מנוס מלפרש דבר מה. But the purpose of this book, as we learned in the introductions this summer, is to explain. So Rambam will.

Rambam: HaShem Is the Cause of First Matter

הכוונה בדבריו תחת רגליו בסבתו בגללו כמו שביארנו. Here רגליו is meaning three: He is the cause.

ומה שהשיגו הוא אמיתת החומר הראשון, שהוא מאתו לו וסבת מציאותו. In this vision, Nadav, Avihu and the seventy elders grasped the truth of first matter: that it comes from Him and He is the cause of its existence.

Building block 1: what is first matter?

In the science of Rambam's day, everything below the heavens is made of four elements, אש, רוח, מים, עפר, and all four come from חומר ראשון, first matter (in Greek, hyle). “Basically stuff.” The heavens were thought to be made of a fifth material.

The Rabbi: we now know there are far more than four elements, and Rambam was working with the science of his day. The idea holds whatever the elements are: the primordial stuff from which all of them were made.

Building block 2: Plato and Rambam

Plato: HaKadosh Barukh Hu is eternal, and He made the universe from pre-existing matter that has existed alongside Him for ever. Infinitely backwards, like HaShem.

Rambam: wrong, but not heresy. Some Rishonim held it, notably Ibn Ezra, who reads בראשית ברא this way. (This is for Section 2 of the Moreh, and was covered in the Rabbi's shiurim on the fourth of the thirteen principles in the introduction to Helek, now on YouTube.)

The correct view: HaShem created first matter. He is not concurrent with it. He is its רגל: its cause, its foot, its basis.

Building block 3: matter and form

A table is the two together. We discussed this at length in Chapter 6 (man and woman): חומר is the passive, צורה the worker.

כמעשה לבנת הספיר: Two Words, Two Properties

Now Rambam reads the words. Why do they mean first matter?

מעשה: the work done upon it

אילו הייתה הכוונה לצבע, היה אומר כלבנת הספיר. If the verse only meant the colour, it would say כלבנת. Why add מעשה?

הוסיף מעשה משום שכידוע לך, החומר הוא מקבל תמיד ונפעל מצד עצמותו, ואין לו פעולה אלא במקרה, כשם שהצורה פועלת תמיד בעצמותה ונפעלת במקרה.

Matter is always receptive, always acted upon, by its very nature. When it does act, it is incidental. Form is the opposite. ולכן אמר עליו כמעשה: “the work of”, because matter is what is worked upon.

לבנת: not white, clear

The Rabbi had been translating “brick” following Rashi, who explains לבנת as the bricks Benei Yisrael made in Egypt, a vision of the pain HaShem felt for them. Rambam reads it differently: לבנת is whiteness. And not the colour white either.

ואילו לבנת הספיר הוא ביטוי לשקיפות ולצבע הלבן... לובן הגביש אינו צבע לבן אלא שקיפות בלבד. והשקיפות אינה צבע.

“This is so beautiful. Just pay attention to this. Transparency is not a colour.”

משום שאילו היתה צבע, לא היתה מראה את כל הצבעים מאחוריה ופניה. Put something black behind a clear crystal and it looks black; something red, red; something green, green. If transparency were a colour, it could not do that.

ומכיוון שהגוף השקוף הוא משולל כל צבעים, הוא יכול לקבל את כל הצבעים. Because it has no colour of its own, it can receive any colour.

The image

דבר זה דומה לחומר הראשון, שמצד אמיתותו חסר כל הצורות, ולכן הוא מקבל את כל הצורות.

“You can make four elements, you can make a hundred elements, you can do whatever you want. That's what כמעשה לבנת הספיר means.”

השגתם היתה לפיכך את החומר הראשון מיוחס להשם, כיוון שהוא ראשית נבראיו מחוייבי ההויה והכליון, ויוצר שלהם. They attributed first matter to HaShem: He is the first cause of all that comes to be and passes away, and its Creator.

Read the verse again

They saw the God of Israel, and ותחת רגליו, under His feet (meaning three: He is the cause and footing of) כמעשה לבנת הספיר: first matter, as passive and as formless as a clear sapphire. Not like Plato. HaShem is the cause of it all.

A Question from the Class

Someone was not sure how לבנת הספיר could mean first matter. The Rabbi: leave Onkelos out of it; Onkelos only translates the words.

Robert: “Whilst I can see the jump he's made, you could make a jump in all sorts of different ways. I'm not very convinced that this is all that it can mean.”

The Rabbi: “Of course it can mean many other things. I do believe he means this. This is certainly one very good interpretation. I don't think it's a jump.” He then explained it again the other way round: the sapphire is clear; clarity is complete passivity, no identity of its own, open to be worked and to reflect anything; so too first matter. And the elders saw that this was תחת רגליו: HaShem is its cause.

Three Further Points

1. Why were they punished? (Chapter 5 again)

In Chapter 5 Rambam told us that Nadav, Avihu and the elders went too far in this vision and were punished with death (at different times). Chapter 28 tells us what they saw: first matter attributed to HaShem. So what was the mistake? Perhaps they thought like Plato. But Chapter 5 says their perception was too corporeal. “The Rambam doesn't reveal to us. Perhaps they went too deep, perhaps they went too corporeal. I don't know exactly.”

2. Why this vision at Sinai?

On a simple level: whenever we speak of the giving of the law, we speak of the Creator: יציאת מצרים and זכר למעשה בראשית. The One giving the Torah is the One who made heaven and earth, their elements, and the first matter itself.

3. The Sforno: a new creation at Sinai

Quoted by Rabbi Korobkin in his shiurim. The Sforno first reads the verse like Rambam: עצם לבנת העדר כל הצורות אשר היא מוכנה לקבלן כמו הספיר הלבן נעדר מכל המראות.

Then: והוא עצם הנפש האנושית השכלית הנעדרת מכל מדע ומוכנת לקבלם בעיון בחירי. The sapphire is also the human intellect, empty of knowledge and ready to receive it.

At בראשית HaShem gave form to formless matter. At Matan Torah, on a smaller scale, He made a new creation: He gives the Torah, and with it makes human minds clear like the sapphire, open to receive it.

The Maharal: Mashiah's Donkey and the Horse of a Hundred Colours

The Gemara: Zechariah says Mashiah comes עני ורוכב על חמור. King Shavor mocked one of the Hakhamim: I'll send you a horse for him. The answer: do you have a horse of a hundred colours?

The Maharal: מפני שהחמור הוא פשוט יותר מכל בעלי חיים, שהוא בריה פשוטה שאין לה דעת וחכמה. The donkey is the simplest, most formless of animals (that is why we call a fool a חמור).

וכאשר החומר דבר פשוט... לכך מוכן לקבל כל הגוונים, כי דבר שאינו פשוט אינו מוכן לקבל. Because it is simple, it can receive every “colour”. Anything complex cannot.

So Mashiah, who brings new wisdom and a remaking of the world (as Rambam explains in the Mishneh Torah), comes on the simple, transparent חמור. You want to give a horse instead? Then it must be one of a hundred colours: already able to be everything.

חמור, חומר, the clear sapphire: the simpler a thing is, the more it can receive.

A life lesson: the older we get, the more set in stone we become, and the harder it is to take someone else's ways into our life. The younger and simpler we are, “the more חומר you are”, and two people can blend their חומר and צורה and live together. (The Rabbi also mentioned a Maharal on why Torah is compared to water, which is formless, and to ענוה: perhaps next week.)
