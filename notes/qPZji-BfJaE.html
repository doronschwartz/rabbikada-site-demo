Topic: Smelling the hadassim and etrog on Sukkot, and whether the Arba Minim are mukseh on Shabbat

Sources: Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Sukkot, Wednesday 12 October 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The lulav and aravah have no use beyond the misvah; the hadas has a fragrance, and the etrog has both fragrance and taste.
  • The Arba Minim are set aside for the misvah for all seven days, so one may not use them for anything else. One may not smell the hadassim, even while holding them in prayer.
  • The etrog's primary purpose, the Hakhamim understood, is eating, so only that is set aside. Strictly, one may smell it all seven days, as Shulhan Arukh rules.
  • Smelling an edible fruit takes the berakhah "Hanoten reah tov bapeirot", as with smelling a guava one will eat tomorrow. Some say no berakhah is said on the etrog, while taking it or all seven days, since it is set aside. Because of this question we do not smell it.
  • But one could say the berakhah on another fruit and then smell the etrog too.
  • So on Shabbat, when the Arba Minim are not taken, the lulav, hadassim and aravot are mukseh, but the etrog is not and may be moved freely, on Shabbat and on Hol HaMoed.

Sources quoted
