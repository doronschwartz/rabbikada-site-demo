Topic: Is the misvah of shofar to blow it or to hear it?

Sources: The Torah ("Yom teruah yihyeh lakhem"); Rabbenu Tam; the Rambam; Mishnah Rosh Hashanah; Hakham Ovadiah

Daily Halacha: Rosh Hashanah, Thursday 26 September 2024

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Shofar is the only Torah misvah unique to Rosh Hashanah, the centre of the day.
  • The berakhah is "lishmoa kol shofar", to hear the sound of the shofar, even for one blowing for himself. So the misvah is to hear; one blows in order to hear.
  • Some Rishonim, such as Rabbenu Tam, held the berakhah is "litkoa shofar" or "al tekiat shofar", the misvah being to blow. The Rambam disagreed, with two proofs.
  • First, the Mishnah in Rosh Hashanah: one who blows into a pit fulfils the misvah only if he hears the shofar itself, not just its echo, though he has blown.
  • Second, a misvah done with the body (misvah begufo), like sitting in the sukkah, taking the lulav or putting on tefillin, cannot be done through an agent. Things one hears, like Kiddush or prayer, can. Since one person blows for the whole congregation, the misvah must be hearing.
  • Hakham Ovadiah rules that one who mistakenly said "to blow the shofar" in the berakhah has fulfilled it and does not repeat it, since some held that is the berakhah.
  • Even the one blowing fulfils the misvah by hearing himself blow. There is a deep message in hearing the shofar, to be developed.

In one line

The misvah of shofar is to hear it, as the berakhah and the Rambam's proofs show.
