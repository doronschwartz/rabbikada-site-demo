Topic: The main reason for the fast (the siege of Jerusalem) and the death of Ezra

Sources: Divrei Nevi'im; the Selihot of Asarah BeTevet; the book of Ezra

Daily Halacha: Asarah BeTevet

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The points

  • The four fasts are Divrei Nevi'im, a middle category between Torah and rabbinic misvot, like reading the Megillah on Purim (which could not be from the Torah, since the story came later). There is a dispute whether a doubt in Divrei Nevi'im is treated strictly or leniently.
  • The main reason: around 420 BCE Nevukhadnessar of Bavel laid siege to Yerushalayim, the beginning of the destruction of the First Temple. HaShem, in His mercy, had given the people time through the prophets to do teshuvah.
  • The siege lasted two and a half years before the walls were breached (the 9th of Tammuz), and a few weeks later the Temple was burnt. Asarah BeTevet marks the start of that process.
  • A further reason: Ezra the Scribe died on the 9th of Tevet. In many ways he did the work of Moshe: he brought the people back from Bavel, stopped intermarriage, taught them basic halachot they had forgotten (they did not even know about Sukkot) and secured the Mesorah.
  • All three reasons are mentioned in the Selihot of the day.

In one line

We fast mainly for the siege that began the destruction of the First Temple, and also remember the death of Ezra and the translation of the Torah into Greek.
