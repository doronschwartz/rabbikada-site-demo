Topic: Prozbol across time zones, through an agent, and on behalf of others

Sources: Hakham Ovadiah Yoseph

Daily Halacha: Shemitah & Prozbol, Monday 23 May 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Recap: bediavad one may make a Prozbol between sunset and nightfall, by declaring before two people that he hands his debts to the Bet Din, since it is rabbinic today.
  • Hakham Ovadiah discusses time zones: a lender in America or England writing a Prozbol when it is already night, the new year, in Israel where the borrower lives.
  • A parallel is selling Hames on Erev Pesah in England when it is already forbidden to own Hames in Israel, where the Hames is. The poskim say the prohibition is on the owner, so his location decides.
  • So too, Hakham Ovadiah writes, the prohibition "lo yigos" (do not press for payment) falls on the lender, so if it is still day for him he may make the Prozbol. One should avoid relying on this, as some are stricter, but since it is rabbinic one may be lenient if necessary.
  • A Prozbol may be made through a shaliah, authorised to hand over one's loans before two witnesses.
  • Hakham Ovadiah also holds that one may make a Prozbol for relatives who will not keep Shemitah and will claim their loans anyway, even without their knowledge, through "zakhin leadam shelo befanav", unless they explicitly say they do not want it.
  • The Sephardi Bet Din has Prozbol documents available; one need not appear before it, only make the declaration before two witnesses nominating that Bet Din.

In one line

The lender's own time decides, a Prozbol may be done through an agent, and even for non-observant relatives without their knowledge.
