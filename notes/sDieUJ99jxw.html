Topic: When children have died, and fulfilling the misvah through grandchildren

Sources: The Gemara (Rabbi Yohanan); the Rambam; Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer, Wednesday 21 January 2026

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • If a man had a son and a daughter who then died, the Gemara (following Rabbi Yohanan) rules he has not fulfilled the misvah. On either understanding (result or effort), the obligation returns.
  • A grandson and granddaughter from one child do not fulfil it: the misvah is a son and a daughter of one's own.
  • The Rambam and Shulhan Arukh rule that if one had a son and a daughter, and each of them had a child, then even if the son and daughter died, two grandchildren (one male, one female) coming from two different children fulfil the misvah.
  • But two grandchildren of the same sex, even from two children, do not.

In one line

The misvah requires a living son and daughter, or a grandson and granddaughter descended from two different children.
