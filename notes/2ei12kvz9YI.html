Topic: Why the etrog, unlike the other three species, may be moved on Shabbat of Sukkot

Daily Halacha: Sukkot, Friday 29 September 2023

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • When Sukkot falls on Shabbat the Arba Minim are not taken, a decree like that of the shofar lest one carry. So the lulav, hadas and aravah are mukseh.
  • For all seven days the Arba Minim are huksah lemisvato, set aside for the misvah, so one may not smell the hadassim until Sukkot is over.
  • The etrog's main use is eating, and that is what is set aside; even an old etrog replaced on Hol HaMoed may not be eaten during Sukkot.
  • Smelling it is strictly permitted all Sukkot (the berakhah is discussed). Since it has this use, the etrog is not mukseh, and one may move an etrog box found at one's seat.
  • The view that it is mukseh mehamat hesron kis, being so valuable, is not the halacha.
  • People do not smell it nowadays only because they do not know, which does not make it mukseh.

In one line

On Shabbat of Sukkot the etrog may be moved, though the lulav, hadas and aravah may not.
