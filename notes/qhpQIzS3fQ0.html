Topic: Mishloah Manot, Matanot LaEvyonim and the Purim seudah

Sources: The Rambam; Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Purim, Thursday 2 March 2023

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • All the misvot of Purim apply to men and women. Mishloah Manot means sending two kinds of food to one person: "manot" is plural.
  • Two reasons are given: to increase friendship and unity, against Haman's claim of "one nation scattered"; and to provide food for the Purim seudah. Hence it must be food that is immediately edible. The Rambam, recorded in the Shulhan Arukh, says gifts of meat ready for the seudah are good, which is why biltong is a wonderful gift; raw food or fancy items are lovely but not part of the misvah.
  • It must be given during the day of Purim, not the night. Some understood "mishloah" to require an agent; this is not the main halacha, which is giving directly, though some send one through an agent to satisfy that view.
  • Matanot LaEvyonim: "evyonim" is plural, so the Hakhamim learn that each man and woman gives two gifts to two poor people. The Rambam writes, and later authorities quote, that one should spend more on gifts to the poor than on Mishloah Manot, the opposite of common practice.
  • Each gift should be about the cost of a basic meal, now perhaps 10 to 15 pounds, so about 20 pounds or so in all. It may be collected in advance but must reach the poor on Purim day and be readily usable, so avoid cheques or vouchers unless the recipient will exchange them and distribute to the poor on Purim itself.
  • The seudah is a misvah to enjoy a physical meal, because the threat was physical, unlike Hanukkah, where the threat was spiritual. It is fulfilled any time during the day, not at night.

Sources quoted

  • The Rambam and Shulhan Arukh: ready-to-eat meat is a fitting gift; spend more on the poor than on Mishloah Manot.

In one line

Give two ready foods to one person, two meal-sized gifts to two poor people that reach them on Purim day, and eat a festive meal by day.
