Topic: A brit milah on Taanit Esther when the fast is brought forward to Thursday

Sources: The Rambam; the Rama

Daily Halacha: Purim, Monday 18 March 2024

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • This year Purim falls on Sunday. We cannot fast on Shabbat and do not want to fast on Friday, so Taanit Esther is brought forward to Thursday, the 11th of Adar.
  • The fast is not in the Talmud, and the Rambam records it as a minhag fast. Unlike the other fasts, which commemorate tragedies, it commemorates something celebratory: the 13th of Adar, when they went out to battle and fasted, and were victorious. So when other fasts fall on Shabbat they are postponed, since we do not bring forward something sad, but Taanit Esther is brought forward.
  • On the other rabbinic fasts, when the fast is postponed from Shabbat to Sunday, the father, the sandak and the mohel need not complete the fast after the brit, though the other guests may not eat.
  • The Rama rules that when Taanit Esther is brought forward to Thursday, not only the father, sandak and mohel but all those invited may break their fast after the brit. The caveat: guests who took part and ate at the seudah must fast the next day, Friday.
  • In practice there are two options: hold the brit late in the afternoon and break the fast together after it ends; or hold it in the morning, the father, sandak and mohel eat, and the guests are invited to a seudah in the evening. A seudah for all the guests after the brit is possible in theory, but they would then fast on Friday.
  • Taanit Esther has other leniencies: for the other fasts an ill person is exempt only when bedridden (hole kol gufo), but here even a slight pain exempts.

Sources quoted

  • The Rambam: Taanit Esther is a minhag fast.
  • The Rama: when it is brought forward, guests at a brit may eat after it but must fast the following day.

In one line

On a brought-forward Taanit Esther the father, sandak and mohel may eat after a brit, and guests may too if they fast on Friday instead.
