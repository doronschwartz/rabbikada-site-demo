Topic: Whether one may eat and drink during the Haggadah, and when to eat the egg

Sources: Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Pesah, Tuesday 16 April 2024

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • With Pesah starting late and Erev Pesah busy, people reach the Seder hungry and the question of eating during the Haggadah arises.
  • The egg recalls the Korban Hagigah, brought every festival, alongside the zeroa for the Korban Pesah; it is round, a sign of mourning for the Beit HaMikdash.
  • The Shulhan Arukh permits drinking during the Seder, except intoxicating drinks that take away focus. Tea, coffee and water after Kiddush are fine, with no Berakhah, as the wine of Kiddush covers all drinks.
  • Eating is strictly permitted, with two cautions. One should not fill up, so the Massah is eaten with appetite.
  • Karpas is less than a kezayit so that no Boreh Nefashot is needed and its Boreh Peri HaAdamah carries over to the Maror, on which a Berakhah is doubtful. Eating a kezayit would require Boreh Nefashot and close that Berakhah.
  • So one eats more than a kezayit only if needed to concentrate (for example a firstborn who fasted). Then one says Boreh Nefashot and does not say another Berakhah on the Maror.
  • For the same reason the egg is better eaten during the meal than at the start of the Haggadah, where a whole egg would require Boreh Nefashot.

Sources quoted

  • Shulhan Arukh: one may drink during the Seder, but not intoxicating drinks.

In one line

Drink freely after Kiddush, eat less than a kezayit unless needed to focus, and eat the egg during the meal.
