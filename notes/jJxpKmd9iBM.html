Topic: How much massah must be eaten on Seder night

Sources: Torah, Parashat Bo; Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Pesah, Tuesday 16 March 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • "Ba-erev tokhelu massot": there is a Torah misvah to eat massah on Pesah night, and Torah eating almost always means a kazayit, about 27 grams. This is eaten at Mosi Massah.
  • The Hakhamim added a kazayit at Korekh with the maror, and one at Afikoman in place of the meat of the Korban Pesah. So the Torah requires one kazayit and the rabbis three.
  • Someone very old or ill who cannot eat much should eat at least one kazayit if possible; everyone else should eat three.
  • At Mosi Massah it is unclear whether the kazayit should come from the whole top massah or the broken middle one (from Yahas). Because of the doubt, the Shulhan Arukh rules one eats a kazayit from each.
  • This applies only to the Baal HaBayit leading the Seder, who has the three massot. Others at the table are given massah from the box, so one kazayit at Mosi Massah suffices for them.
  • There is a humrah to eat two kezeitim at Afikoman, one for the Korban Pesah and one for the massah eaten with it in Temple times. This is not strictly required.
  • In sum: the Baal HaBayit eats four kezeitim and everyone else three, or five and four with the humrah. The massah should be Massah Shemurah, explained tomorrow.

Sources quoted

  • Parashat Bo: "Ba-erev tokhelu massot", the Torah misvah of eating massah.
  • Shulhan Arukh: the leader eats a kazayit from both the top and middle massah.

In one line

The Torah requires one kazayit of massah and the rabbis three, with the Seder leader eating an extra kazayit at Mosi Massah.
