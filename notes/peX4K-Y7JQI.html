Topic: Brushing one's teeth and rinsing the mouth on the minor fasts

Sources: Shulhan Arukh; the Arukh HaShulhan

Daily Halacha: Asarah BeTevet

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • There are six fasts: Yom Kippur from the Torah, and five rabbinic ones. Tisha BeAv is the most severe; Taanit Esther is a lesser category; the 17th of Tammuz, Som Gedaliah and Asarah BeTevet form the middle group.
  • Shulhan Arukh writes that one who rinses his mouth every morning should not do so on a fast day.
  • Many poskim permit brushing teeth and rinsing anyway, for one of two reasons: the Shulhan Arukh refers to using more than a reviit (about 86 ml), where one may swallow; or he refers to Tisha BeAv, which is more severe (brushing on Tisha BeAv is a separate discussion).
  • Sefaradi poskim, and among Ashkenazim the Arukh HaShulhan, permit it on these three fasts with two conditions: less than 86 ml of water, and the head bent downwards so that nothing is swallowed.
  • Swallowing water is of course forbidden.

In one line

On Asarah BeTevet, the 17th of Tammuz and Som Gedaliah one may brush and rinse with under 86 ml of water, head bent down.
