Topic: How to have the three Shabbat meals when Erev Pesah falls on Shabbat

Sources: The Gemara; the Rambam; Hakham Ovadiah Yoseph

Daily Halacha: Pesah, Wednesday 24 March 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • There is a misvah of three Shabbat meals, ideally with bread (washing and Hamosi).
  • Eating massah on Erev Pesah is forbidden, and the Gemara speaks sharply of one who cannot wait. Of three opinions (from the night before, from the morning, from midday), the halacha follows the Rambam: from the morning.
  • So on Friday night one may make Hamosi on the massah to be used the next night.
  • Shabbat morning, option one: pray Shaharit early and eat a small, measured amount of hallot set aside before Shabbat, washing and eating the bread before 10 o'clock (in England); the meal may continue after 10. For Seudah Shelishit, rely on the opinions that it can be fulfilled with other food, such as fruit.
  • Option two: Massah Ashirah, made with pure fruit juice, which the Gemara says cannot become hames (though a drop of water makes it hames faster). It cannot be used for the Seder, so it may be eaten on Erev Pesah. Its berakhah is Mezonot unless one eats a large amount, about four to five square massot. Ashkenazim do not eat it at all on Pesah, so it is not an option for them.
  • Option three, which Hakham Ovadiah preferred and did himself: before Shabbat, put the massah in hot chicken soup for a couple of minutes. Cooked massah (Massah Mevushelet) is unfit for the Seder and may be eaten on Erev Pesah. If intact, one says Hamosi and Birkat HaMazon on a regular amount, about a kebeisah (some 56 grams), even after 10.
  • In all cases, eat Seudah Shelishit early in the afternoon so as to come into Pesah with an appetite.

Sources quoted

  • The Gemara: one who eats massah on Erev Pesah; fruit-juice dough cannot become hames.
  • The Rambam: massah is forbidden from the morning of Erev Pesah.
  • Hakham Ovadiah Yoseph: prefers cooked massah, and did so himself.

In one line

On Shabbat Erev Pesah eat hallot before 10, or Massah Ashirah, or massah cooked before Shabbat, and have Seudah Shelishit early.
