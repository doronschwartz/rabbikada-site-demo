Topic: Keeping two days of Purim, skipping Purim altogether by travel, and places that keep two days out of doubt

Sources: The Ben Ish Hai; the Gemara (Tiberias)

Daily Halacha: Purim, Wednesday 1 March 2023

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The 14th of Adar is the standard Purim; the 15th, Shushan Purim, is for cities walled at the time of Yehoshua's conquest, such as Yerushalayim. Where a person is at dawn is the decisive factor.
  • This year the 14th is Monday night and Tuesday, the 15th Tuesday night and Wednesday. One who kept Purim outside Yerushalayim, then enters it on Tuesday evening planning to be there at dawn, must hear the Megillah again that night and do all the misvot on Wednesday, even if he then returns home. He would read the Megillah four times.
  • Conversely, though he does not think it is recommended, one could skip Purim: spend the 14th in Yerushalayim, where it is a regular day, then leave at the start of the 15th and be at dawn in a place such as Ashdod that keeps the 14th. He is then obligated in nothing, neither the Megillah nor the misvot.
  • When one is obligated on both days, the berakhah on the Megillah is said both times.
  • Some places keep two days out of doubt. The Ben Ish Hai writes that in Baghdad they kept the 14th with the berakhah, and repeated all the misvot on the 15th without a berakhah on the Megillah, as it was doubtful whether it was walled from Yehoshua's time.
  • The Gemara says it is doubtful whether Tiberias counts as walled, since the sea surrounds it, so two days are kept there.
  • Yafo is walled but it is unclear since when, so two days are kept there, and also in Bnei Brak, which is samukh venireh (close and visible) to Yafo. Where two days are kept out of doubt, the 14th is the main day with the berakhah and the 15th is repeated without it.

In one line

Purim may be one day, two days by obligation with a berakhah each day, two days out of doubt with a berakhah only on the 14th, or theoretically none at all.
