Topic: Drinking the Havdalah wine on Mosei Shabbat in the Nine Days

Sources: Shulhan Arukh; the Rama; the Mishnah Berurah; Hakham Ovadiah; Hakham Ben Sion Abba Shaul

Daily Halacha: The Three Weeks, Thursday 28 July 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Many Sepharadim eat meat and drink wine on Rosh Hodesh Av (tonight and tomorrow) and on Shabbat, so the restriction begins on Mosei Shabbat. Havdalah should ideally be on wine or grape juice.
  • The Shulhan Arukh rules one may drink the wine of Havdalah, since it is associated with Shabbat and is for a misvah, and likewise the cup of Birkat HaMazon; we never accepted the custom in such cases. Sepharadim make Havdalah and drink as usual.
  • The Rama says the Ashkenazi custom is not to drink it but to give it to a child who heard the Havdalah.
  • The child must be old enough for hinukh and understand Havdalah; a four or five year old does not help, and it would be a Berakhah in vain. The Mishnah Berurah adds he must not yet be old enough to mourn for Jerusalem, perhaps 9 or 10, so roughly seven or eight.
  • Hakham Ovadiah and Hakham Ben Sion Abba Shaul hold the custom does not apply to children under 12 (11 for girls), Hakham Ben Sion perhaps until 13, since we do not train children in mourning.
  • An Ashkenazi with no suitable child may drink it himself, better than using another drink, since it is a custom and for a misvah.

Sources quoted

  • Shulhan Arukh: the wine of Havdalah and Birkat HaMazon may be drunk.
  • The Rama: give it to a child.
  • The Mishnah Berurah: the child must not yet be of age to mourn.
  • Hakham Ovadiah and Hakham Ben Sion Abba Shaul: children under 12 are not bound.

In one line

Sepharadim make Havdalah and drink the wine as usual, while Ashkenazim give it to a suitable child or, if there is none, drink it themselves.
