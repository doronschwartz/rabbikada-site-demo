Topic: The fourth category: a half mamzer

Sources: The Gemara; the Rambam; Rashi; Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer, Friday 29 May 2026

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Three categories so far: a mamzer from the Torah, a safek mamzer and a rabbinic mamzer. The fourth, almost impractical today, is a half mamzer.
  • The Gemara speaks of one who is half a servant and half free (hasi eved hasi ben horin): for example, a slave owned equally by two Jewish owners, one of whom freed him.
  • If such a person commits adultery with a married Jewish woman, the Rambam holds the child is a half mamzer. A servant's child with a married woman is not a mamzer, but the free Jewish half creates a mamzer.
  • Rashi disagrees: since part of him is free, the child is a complete mamzer.
  • The Shulhan Arukh rules like the Rambam.
  • A half mamzer cannot marry a regular Jew, nor a mamzer from the Torah or a rabbinic mamzer; only a convert or another half mamzer.
  • Next: whom a mamzer may marry, and whether there is any way out for a mamzer to have regular Jewish children.

In one line

The child of a half slave half free man and a married woman is a half mamzer according to the Rambam and the Shulhan Arukh.
