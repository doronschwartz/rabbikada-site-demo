Topic: Reducing simhah in the Nine Days: entertainment, building, purchases, weddings and a new car

Sources: The Mishnah; Tosafot; the Mishnah Berurah; the Magen Avraham; the Rambam; Shulhan Arukh; the Gemara; Rav Moshe Feinstein

Daily Halacha: The Three Weeks, Wednesday 7 July 2021

The halacha

  • The Nine Days run from Rosh Hodesh Av (this Shabbat) to Tisha BeAv. The Mishnah says, "When Av begins we reduce simhah".
  • Tosafot infer from the lack of a measure that one should not be happy at all, codified by the Mishnah Berurah, the Magen Avraham and others. The Rambam quotes the words as they are, and he understands him literally: a less happy spirit than usual.
  • Entertainment that is not a necessity should be avoided; each person must work out what is necessity and what is luxury.
  • Binyan shel simhah: a needed extension for a family that has outgrown the house may continue, but a swimming pool, unless for medical purposes, may not. Likewise buying for a mundane party after Tisha BeAv (not a misvah party) is not allowed.
  • No weddings from Rosh Hodesh. Engagements are permitted even on Tisha BeAv itself, as the Gemara says not to miss such an opportunity.
  • A new car (Rav Moshe Feinstein): an upgrade when one already has a car is not allowed. If one has none, or it broke down, it is permitted. Without pressing need wait until after Tisha BeAv, but if the price will rise or it will not be available one may buy it.
  • Haircuts are still permitted until the week of Tisha BeAv. The time should be used for introspection about the exile.

Sources quoted

  • The Mishnah: "When Av begins we reduce simhah".
  • Tosafot, the Mishnah Berurah and the Magen Avraham: no simhah at all.
  • The Rambam: reduce simhah, read literally.
  • The Gemara: engagements permitted even on Tisha BeAv.
  • Rav Moshe Feinstein: buying a new car in the Nine Days.

In one line

In the Nine Days one avoids weddings, unnecessary building, entertainment and luxury purchases, while engagements and needed purchases are permitted.
