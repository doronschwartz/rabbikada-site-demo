What is Muktzeh?

Definition

Certain objects that we are forbidden to move on Shabbat

Sources and Reasons (According to Rambam - Hilchot Shabbat)

Primary Source: אסרו חכמים לטלטל מקצת דברים בשבת כדי שזה בחול

  • Chachamim forbade moving certain things on Shabbat so it shouldn't be like weekdays

Three Reasons Given by Rambam:

  1. Creating Shabbat Atmosphere
  • Based on Nevi'im: Don't walk on Shabbat as you walk on weekdays, don't speak on Shabbat as you speak on weekdays
  • קל וחומר: Don't move objects on Shabbat as you move on weekdays
  • כדי שלא יהא חול בעיניו - so it doesn't become mundane in your eyes
  • Prevents spending entire day organizing house instead of resting
  • Ensures fulfillment of למען ינוח (so you shall rest)
  1. Prevention of Melachah
  • Some utensils have primary purpose for prohibited activities
  • Example: Don't touch/move a pen so you won't come to write
  1. General Preventive Measure
  • Less movement = less chance of violating other Shabbat laws

Additional Reason (Ra'avad):

  • Less carrying on Shabbat = less chance to violate carrying prohibitions outside

Halachic Status

  • Consensus: Muktzeh is Issur D'Rabbanan (Rabbinic prohibition)
  • Minority Opinion: Some view it as D'Nevi'im (from the Prophets)
  • Practical Implication: When in doubt about the halachah (ספק בהלכה), one can be lenient
  • Important Caveat: When in doubt about facts (ספק במציאות), we are strict
  • Example: Fruit found under tree - if uncertain when it fell, we treat it as muktzeh

Three Main Categories of Muktzeh

1. כלי שמלאכתו לאיסור (Utensil Whose Primary Purpose is Prohibited)

Definition: A utensil (כלי) whose primary function is for something forbidden on Shabbat

Examples:

  • Pen (primary purpose: writing)
  • Scissors (primary purpose: cutting)
  • Empty pot (primary purpose: cooking)
  • Hammer (primary purpose: building)

Important Notes:

  • Muktzeh is subjective - depends on individual use
  • If someone uses a pot primarily for storage (not cooking), it's not muktzeh for them
  • This is the most lenient category of muktzeh

Special Permissions for כלי שמלאכתו לאיסור:

  1. לצורך מקומו (For the sake of its place)
  • Can move it if you need the space where it's located
  • Example: Pen on your bed - can move it to sleep
  1. לצורך גופו (For the sake of the object itself/secondary use)
  • Can use it for a permissible secondary purpose
  • Example: Using hammer to crack nuts
  • Must be an actual secondary use, not the primary prohibited use

2. מוקצה מחמת חסרון כיס (Set Aside Due to Monetary Value)

Definition: Expensive items that are set aside due to their value

Examples:

  • Shochet's knife
  • Mohel's knife
  • Expensive camera (used occasionally)
  • Potentially: Passport (when traveling)

Characteristics:

  • More severe level of muktzeh than category 1
  • Items are typically used infrequently and carefully stored
  • Cannot be moved even לצורך מקומו or לצורך גופו (unlike category 1)

Modern Applications - Debate:

  • Smartphones: Expensive but used constantly
  • Rav Moshe Feinstein: Considered muktzeh
  • Counter-argument: So frequently used that not truly "set aside"
  • Different from expensive camera which is stored between uses

3. מוקצה מחמת גופו (Inherently Muktzeh)

Definition: Items that have no כלי (utensil) status and weren't designated for use before Shabbat

Examples:

  • Stones (unless designated as doorstop before Shabbat)
  • Pebbles
  • Money (even though it has purpose, lacks כלי status)

Characteristics:

  • Very severe level of muktzeh
  • No inherent purpose or use
  • Cannot be moved even לצורך מקומו or לצורך גופו

Methods of Moving Muktzeh

1. שינוי/כלאחר יד (In an Unusual Manner)

  • Status: Always permitted for ALL categories of muktzeh
  • Examples: Pushing with elbow, kicking with foot (for items not normally kicked)
  • Key: Must be genuinely unusual way of moving

2. טלטול מן הצד (Indirect Movement)

  • More Complex Rules: Sometimes permitted, sometimes prohibited

Two Types:

  1. For Non-Muktzeh Purpose: Generally permitted
  • Example: Taking ice cream from freezer, causing raw meat to fall
  • Moving the permitted item (ice cream), muktzeh moves incidentally
  1. For Muktzeh Purpose: Generally prohibited
  • Example: Using stick to move phone from bed to another location
  • Moving indirectly but for the sake of the muktzeh item

Important Distinction: Don't confuse שינוי (unusual manner) with טלטול מן הצד (indirect movement)

Practical Applications and Questions

Contemporary Issues

  1. iPhone on bed scenario:
  • Classification depends on whether considered חסרון כיס or כלי שמלאכתו לאיסור
  • Modern debate about smartphone status due to frequent use
  1. Expensive items:
  • Beautiful glass vase (expensive but usable) - not muktzeh
  • Passport - potentially מחמת חסרון כיס depending on context
  1. Raw meat:
  • Traditionally muktzeh in Talmudic times
  • Modern debate due to contemporary eating of raw foods (tartare, carpaccio)

Key Principles for Future Learning

  1. Subjectivity: Muktzeh often depends on individual use patterns
  2. Severity Levels: Categories 2 & 3 more severe than category 1
  3. Doubt Principles: Lenient for הלכה doubts, strict for מציאות doubts
  4. Movement Methods: שינוי always allowed, טלטול מן הצד has complex rules
  5. Contemporary Applications: Many modern items require case-by-case analysis
