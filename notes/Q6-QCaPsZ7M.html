Topic: Kamsa and Bar Kamsa, and seeing halacha as a whole system of values

Sources: The Gemara (Rabbi Yohanan on Rabbi Zekhariah ben Avkulas)

Daily Halacha: The Three Weeks, Friday 1 August 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The points

  • On Tisha BeAv we focus more on the Second Temple, destroyed by the Romans, as it is more recent and we have not yet been redeemed from that exile.
  • The Gemara's story: a wealthy man meant to invite Kamsa but Bar Kamsa came and was thrown out, deeply embarrassed. He told the Caesar the Jews were rebelling: send an offering and they will refuse it, though a non-Jew may bring one.
  • On the way Bar Kamsa made a tiny blemish on the animal's eyelid. The Kohanim wanted to offer it to avoid offending the Caesar.
  • Rabbi Zekhariah ben Avkulas objected that it would set a precedent for blemished offerings, and refused the suggestion to kill Bar Kamsa. The offering was refused, the Caesar sent his army, and Rabbi Yohanan declared Rabbi Zekhariah responsible for the destruction and the exile.
  • Yet he seemed to do the right thing. The lesson: one cannot take a halacha out of context or decide it in a vacuum; the halachot are part of a whole framework of values.
  • Hence the importance of apprenticeship: expertise differs from knowing how to apply it in life. The destruction came from an inability to zoom out.
  • So on Tisha BeAv, beyond the many questions of what is allowed, step back to see the whole picture: the exile of the Shekhinah, and our hope for the rebuilding of the Beit HaMikdash and Mashiah.
