Topic: Which blessing to say on thunder and on lightning, and when

Sources: The Gemara; the Rambam; Shulhan Arukh; the Taz (Rabbi David Segal)

Daily Halacha: Rainbows, Thunder & Lightning, Monday 31 October 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • There is no fixed blessing for lightning and another for thunder. The Gemara gives two blessings, either of which may be said on either: "Oseh Maaseh Bereshit" and "Shekoho UGevurato Malei Olam". The Rambam and Shulhan Arukh record this explicitly.
  • About 400 years ago the Taz recorded the custom to say Oseh Maaseh Bereshit on lightning and Shekoho UGevurato on thunder, since thunder is heard as a display of Hashem's might. That is our custom.
  • One who is unsure which goes with which, or knows only one, should not refrain: either blessing may be said on either.
  • If one sees and hears them separately, one says a blessing on each. If together, or lightning and immediately thunder, one blessing covers both, customarily Oseh Maaseh Bereshit.
  • The blessing must be said within Tokh Kedei Dibbur, about three or four seconds, so one stops at once and says it, without hunting for a Siddur. Once the time passes it cannot be said.
  • Unlike the rainbow, which stays in view, thunder and lightning come and go, hence the time limit.
