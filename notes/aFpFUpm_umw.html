Siman 314: Binyan U'Stirah B'Keilim

Seif Chet - Protective Casings

The Halacha:

חבילות של תמרים או גרוגרות - Cases for dates to dry

Two Permissions:

  1. Tying/Untying: If casing tied with string - may tie and untie (not kesher shel kayama)
  2. Cutting: וחותך אפילו בסכין - May even cut with knife, even actual body of casing

Reasoning: שכל זה כמו ששובר אגוזים או שקדים - Like breaking shells of almonds/pistachios

  • Casings are protective coverings
  • Removing protection is normal way
  • Not boneh or soter

Two Understandings:

Minority: It's a mustaki (temporary casing) - extension of Seif Aleph

Main/Simple Pshat: Anything around food whose primary purpose is protection is like a shell - obviously permitted to unshell on Shabbat

Two Reasons for Opening Packages

Reason 1 (Seif Alef): Binyan garua - temporary structure, mustaki

Reason 2 (Seif Chet): Protective casing for food (packets, cans of tuna/pickles)

Rav Moshe Feinstein: Though doubtful about Seif Alef, permits based on Seif Chet

Psak:

  • Sefardim: Clearly permissible (Chacham Ovadia, Ben Tzion)
  • Ashkenazim: Also allow based on protective casing

Extension to Non-Food

Magazines/Newspapers with Plastic Covering:

Arguments to Permit:

  1. Plastic coverings are protective shells
  2. Nature: made to be broken/removed
  3. Binyan garua - temporary
  4. At worst d'Rabbanan

Practical: Generally unpack before Shabbat; if forgot, grounds to allow (author's understanding, not in sources)

Main Point: Something made as protective casing - no binyan u'stirah, may break and tear open

Q&A: D'Rabbanan Stirah

Question (Robert): Where's the issue? Tearing packet isn't building anything.

Answer: From d'oraita nothing to discuss. Mi'd'Rabbanan, any destruction is assur. Question is whether issur d'Rabbanan exists here. Chachamim extended issur even where no subsequent binyan (like smashing eggshells) lest come to actual construction.

Seif Tet - Knots on Spits

מותר להפקיע ולחתוך קשרי השפוד - Permitted untie or cut knots of spit on meat

Reasoning: Binyan garua (mustaki) or protective covering

Modern Application: Fruit/clementines in nets - may untie or tear through

Exception - Connected to Ground

חותמת שעל בור קרקע - Seal on ground pit with rope

May Untie: Not kesher kayama, made to be untied

Cannot Break/Cut: אבל לא מפקיע וחותך משום סתירה - That's stira. Connected to ground = no leniency of ain binyan u'stirah b'keilim

Qualification: Only when made permanent; if not for permanence - permitted

Doors Application

Oven/Front Door: Why no issue opening/closing?

  • Every opening = soter, every closing = boneh

Answer: Nature is constant opening/closing. Anything made to be opened/closed constantly = permitted

Oven Specifics: Though attached to wall, nature is to open/close - no problem

Muktzeh: Oven door NOT muktzeh, may freely open/close if oven off

Siman 315: Hilchot Ohel - Introduction

Why Important

Often Unknown Topic:

  • People know boneh/soter
  • But entire ohel section often unknown
  • Many rabbinic extensions very relevant

Topics: Umbrellas, buggy roofs, many applications throughout Shabbat

Three Basic Categories

1. Ohel Kavua (Permanent Tent) - D'Oraita:

  • Permanent tent with tefach al tefach (8cm x 8cm), roof and walls
  • Tolada of boneh, chayav from Torah
  • מוסיף על אוהל קבוע - Even adding to permanent tent = chayav d'oraita

2. Ohel Arai (Temporary Tent) - D'Rabbanan:

  • Chachamim forbade making temporary tent (even very short time)
  • Also forbade taking down temporary tent
  • All d'Rabbanan

3. Mosif al Ohel Arai - PERMITTED:

  • Critical Distinction: Ohel kavua - even tiny addition chayav; Ohel arai - forbidden to make BUT permitted to add

Gemara - Making a Bed

Rebbe Yehuda: מלמעלה למטה שרי, מלמטה למעלה אסור

  • Top to bottom: permitted; Bottom to top: forbidden

Understanding with Bed:

Forbidden (milmata l'ma'ala): Put legs first, then board on top - makes ohel arai underneath (storage space)

Permitted (milma'ala l'mata): Hold board in air, put legs underneath - shows distinction so won't come to ohel keva

Rambam's Codification

העושה אהל קבוע חייב משום בונה - Making fixed tent = chayav boneh

לפיכך אין עושין אהל עראי לכתחילה - Don't make temporary tent (Chachamim)

ולא סותרין אהל עראי - Don't take down (decree lest make/destroy permanent)

Critical Permission: ומותר להוסיף על אהל עראי בשבת - PERMITTED add to temporary tent

Example: Talit partially spread on poles before Shabbat - if one tefach remains stretched, may stretch completely on Shabbat to make large tent

Application: Have poles and talit bit rolled out (one tefach) - may roll completely. But taking poles and spreading from scratch - can't (normally)

Tosfot and Rosh - Critical Qualification

Question: How set up tables? Take legs, put board on top - why not forbidden?

Tosfot: הואיל ואין לו מחיצות שרי - Since no walls, permitted. Gemara's restriction only when poles have walls (sheets reaching ground)

Conclusion: Permitted make table - board on four poles

Rashba: Disagreed - assur even pure poles (different reason for shulchan)

Shulchan Aruch (Seif Gimmel): Rules like Tosfot - may make bed/table normal way if poles purely poles without walls

Rashba's Additional Distinction

Even with Only Poles (No Walls):

Still Assur if:

  • A. Using space underneath, OR
  • B. Using as protection/shelter (wanting to be underneath)

Application - Chuppah:

  • Cannot set up chuppah on Shabbat (even just four poles)
  • Using space underneath (standing under it)
  • Assur lekulei alma

Tosfot's Leniency Only: When using ON TOP (bed, table - sitting/eating on top)

Summary of Principles

  1. Ohel kavua: Making or adding = chayav
  2. Ohel arai: Making = forbidden (d'Rabbanan)
  3. Mosif al ohel arai: MUTTAR - key distinction
  4. Gemara's method: Opposite way (board first, legs underneath)
  5. Tosfot/Shulchan Aruch: Only when poles have walls; pure poles normal way permitted
  6. Rashba's exception: Using space underneath OR protection - assur all circumstances

Seif Aleph - Mechitzot (Walls)

Basic Rules

אסור לעשות אוהל בשבת ויום טוב - Forbidden make ohel (even temporary)

Critical: v'davka gag - Specifically ROOF. Can't make tent = only poles WITH ROOF

אבל מחיצות מותר - But walls permitted (putting up temporary partition permitted)

Mechitza HaMateret - When Walls Forbidden

ואין מחיצה אסורה אלא אם כן נעשית להתיר - Wall only forbidden if mechitza hamateret (changes halachic status)

Example 1 - Sukkah:

  • Have two walls, need three
  • Cannot add third wall on Shabbat
  • Mechitza hamateret - creates new status, makes sukkah kosher
  • Assur (if permanent: d'oraita; if temporary: d'Rabbanan)

Example 2 - Carrying: Making enclosure to permit carrying - changing status - assur

Key: Anything changing halachic status = assur, even one wall without roof

Practical Cases

Sukkah Wall Falls:

  • Person cannot put up himself
  • May ask non-Jew - shevut d'shevut l'tzorech mitzvah

Rav Ovadia - Door as Third Wall:

  • Third wall acts as door (closed = sukkah; open = not sukkah)
  • In/out = making/breaking sukkah
  • Ruling: Permissible - nature to open/close, not mechitza hamateret

Mechitza for Tzniut - Permitted

אבל מחיצה הנעשית לצניעות בעלמא שרי - Wall purely for tzniut, doesn't change status - permitted

Source (Mordechai): Ancient practice - hear drasha with mechitza between men/women

Prayer Application:

  • Women pray in synagogue with mechitza
  • Only l'tzniut, doesn't prevent minyan participation
  • Not mechitza hamateret
  • Women behind curtain still part minyan
  • May open/close mechitza on Shabbat

Various Applications

Vilon (Curtain) Before Entrance:

  • ולכן מותר לתלות וילון לפני הפתח אף על פי שקבוע שם
  • Permitted hang curtain even if fixed
  • Nature: constant in/out, so flimsy
  • Note: Hazon Ish disagrees

Parochet, Pictures, Protection:

  • Parochet before Aron Kodesh - permitted
  • Pictures/decorative sheets on wall - permitted (Mishnah Berurah)
  • Protection from sun/cold/wind - permitted (not mechitza hamateret)

Sefarim and Tashmish HaMitah

Problem: אסור לעשות מחיצה בפני ספרים כדי לשמש - Forbidden make partition before sefarim for relations

Why: Forbidden have relations in room with sifrei kodesh. Partition = mechitza hamateret (changes status)

Exception: Unless already one tefach before Shabbat, then may add

Practical: Host should put up sheet before Shabbat

Magen Avraham's Chiddush:

Problem only with halachic mechitza (10 tefachim partition where still visible above)

But: Sheet all way to top completely covering = not mechitza but kisui (covering) - permitted

Critical Difference:

  • Partition (visible above) = mechitza hamateret (forbidden)
  • Complete covering = kisui (permitted)

Rav Ovadia: Better before Shabbat, but if stuck may rely on Magen Avraham - must cover entire length

Seif Bet - Tosefet Ohel

Ship Example

עצים שתוקעין ראשן - Wooden pegs on ship sides, want spread sheet for shade

When Permitted:

  • If wooden pieces have tefach (8cm) width, OR
  • Even without tefach width but 3 tefachim between them
  • חשיבי כאוהל - Considered ohel already
  • May spread sheet on Shabbat - adding to temporary ohel (permitted)

Understanding:

  • Cannot make ohel arai (forbidden)
  • BUT: Mosif al ohel arai mutar
  • If pieces one tefach = already beginning of ohel
  • May put entire sheet over ship (even using space underneath)
  • One tefach = beginning of ohel, can complete on Shabbat

Principle: Make ohel = assur; Tosefet ohel = mutar

Spreading Materials

ומיטעמא זה, מחצלת פרוסה כדי טפח - Mat spread to tefach, may spread rest on Shabbat

Chuppah Example: Bit rolled out (one tefach) - may finish rolling, even using space underneath

Critical Qualification: וטפח שאמרו חוץ מן הכריכה - Tefach aside from rolled portion

What Counts: What's extracted/stretched already = one tefach (rolled portion itself doesn't count)

Baby Buggies - Critical Application

The Problem

Buggy Roof: Protects child underneath (roof lehagen). Opening = breaking ohel; Closing = making ohel. Simple understanding: assur

Majority Position

Rav Ovadia, Ben Tzion, Most Poskim: Not allowed fully open/close buggy on Shabbat

Solution - Must Leave Tefach:

Before Shabbat: Leave one tefach (8cm) open

On Shabbat:

  • Closing: May close, but when opening leave tefach
  • Opening: Don't open completely to end, leave tefach showing

Why Works: Ohel already made (tefach there), mosif al ohel arai = mutar

Accepted: Sefardi poskim consensus

Chazon Ish - Lenient

Position: Nature of buggy roof constantly opened/closed (like door) - wants to say mutar

Practical: If mistake (opened/closed fully) - may rely on Chazon Ish

Mishnah Berurah

Baby's cot with hoops/sheet: Cannot put sheet on cot unless already spread bit before

Same law carriages: Made to protect underneath - ohel - unless already spread before Shabbat width of tefach

Seif Gimmel - Beds/Tables

מיטה כשמעמידין אותה - Setting up bed

Forbidden: Place legs first then boards

Permitted: Boards first in air, then legs underneath

Tosfot's Qualification (Psak):

  • והני מילי כשהרגליים הם דפים מחוברים - Only when legs are walls attached
  • אבל רגלים של מיטות שלנו וכן רגלי השולחן מותר - Our beds/tables permitted

Shulchan Aruch: Rules like Tosfot - pure poles permissible normal way (legs first, then board)

Additional Q&A

Cupboard Door (Robert): Sefarim in cupboard, door not closed before Shabbat - can close? Yes, nature of door: opened/closed

Synagogue Mechitza (Robert): Changing status? No, not changing status. Women pray with minyan - mechitza only tzniut b'alma, not mechitza matir'et. Theoretically permissible men/women same place (not facing). Only modesty, not preventing minyan. Why can open/close on Shabbat.
