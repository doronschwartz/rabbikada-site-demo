Topic: Rejoicing, meals, clothes and weddings on Hol HaMoed

Sources: The Torah; Rambam; the Mishnah in Avot; Rashi

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Wednesday 10 September 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The misvah of rejoicing on Pesah and Sukkot applies equally to Hol HaMoed, as Rambam writes explicitly.
  • For men there is a misvah (not an obligation) of wine if it makes them happy, of meat, and of a meal with bread. Anything that brings real happiness, such as dancing, is part of vesamahta behagekha. The ultimate simhah includes the less fortunate and those who need company.
  • One should not wear regular weekday clothes. The Mishnah says one who disgraces the moadim has no portion in the world to come. Rashi applies this to Hol HaMoed: one who eats and drinks as usual, with no noticeable difference.
  • So each day of Hol HaMoed should have a recognisably special meal, with wine for one who enjoys it, and Shabbat clothes.
  • It is forbidden to marry on Hol HaMoed. Sheva Berakhot for a couple married before the festival are fine, being an existing simhah; a new simhah would detract from the simhah of the festival.

Sources quoted

  • Rambam: the misvah of simhah applies to Hol HaMoed.
  • The Mishnah: "one who disgraces the moadim has no portion in the world to come".
  • Rashi: this refers to treating Hol HaMoed like any other day.

In one line

Conduct on Hol HaMoed should be almost like Yom Tov in food, drink and clothes, with no new weddings.
