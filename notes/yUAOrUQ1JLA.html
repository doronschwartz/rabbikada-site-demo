Topic: Which basic foods need a Hekhsher for Pesah, for Sepharadim

Sources: Shulhan Arukh; Hakham Ovadiah; the Rema

Daily Halacha: Pesah, Tuesday 9 March 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • For the Shulhan Arukh, the main stringency of Pesah is that Hames is not nullified on Pesah itself; all other leniencies of ordinary Kashrut still apply. The Rema adds further stringencies.
  • So for Sepharadim, salt, sugar, tea, coffee and oil do not need a Hekhsher, provided they are bought before Pesah.
  • It is highly unlikely Hames fell in, and even if it did it would be less than a sixtieth, nullified before Pesah, and Sepharadim hold it does not reawaken on Pesah.
  • By law all ingredients must be listed. Those under 2% need not be in order of quantity, but must still appear. If no Hames is listed, we do not presume any fell in.
  • Hakham Ovadiah rules that if bread fell before Pesah into wine set aside for the four cups, the strained wine may be used, since the remaining crumbs were nullified before Pesah.
  • He also rules that wheat that became Hames and was mixed before Pesah into wheat for Massot may be used if the other grain is sixty times more.
  • Conclusion for Sepharadim: natural foods whose ingredients are all listed may be assumed Kasher LePesah, if bought before Pesah. The Rema is stricter and would seemingly require a Hekhsher.

Sources quoted

In one line

For Sepharadim, simple natural foods such as salt, sugar, tea, coffee and oil bought before Pesah need no Hekhsher.
