Topic: How to dispose of the Arba Minim after Sukkot

Daily Halacha: Sukkot, Thursday 30 September 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • There are two kinds of sacred objects: tashmishei kedushah and tashmishei misvah.
  • Tashmishei kedushah, such as a Sefer Torah, mezuzah, siddur and tefillin, are intrinsically holy and must go to genizah.
  • Tashmishei misvah have no intrinsic holiness but were used for a misvah, such as a tallit, which is really a garment. They need no genizah but must not be discarded in a degrading way.
  • So strictly, a tallit may be thrown away if wrapped up, not put loose in a bin with food and sauce.
  • The Arba Minim are tashmishei misvah: not straight into the bin with other rubbish, but they may be put in a bag and thrown away, or put in a garden bin with grass cuttings, which is an honourable way.
  • Some keep them until Pesah and use them in the fire for baking Massot or burning the Hames, but strictly a bag in the bin or the garden bin is fine.

In one line

The Arba Minim need no genizah and may be bagged and binned or put in the garden waste, but not thrown loose among rubbish.
