Topic: Which forms of contraception are permitted where contraception is allowed

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer, Tuesday 10 February 2026

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Even where contraception is permitted, not every kind is. The best kind interferes neither with the natural act of cohabitation nor with the zera itself.
  • By all opinions, the birth control pill and the IUD are permitted, since neither interferes with the act or with the zera.
  • The pill gives hormones that prevent ovulation; it acts in the woman's body with no effect on the man. It can cause spotting, which raises separate questions of niddah, and the type should be chosen with medical advice. It needs no procedure.
  • The IUD is inserted by a doctor and prevents implantation in the lining of the uterus. It needs a procedure, but again does not interfere with the act or the zera.
  • Spermicide, inserted before cohabitation, stops the zera reaching the uterus. Many opinions permit it when there is a need, but the first two are the better forms.
  • In all of this, rabbinic guidance is most important, together with proper medical advice.

In one line

Where contraception is allowed, the pill and the IUD are permitted by all opinions, spermicide by many, and rabbinic and medical guidance is essential.
