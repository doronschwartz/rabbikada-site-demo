Topic: Whom a mamzer may marry, and the status of a mamzer's child with a Jew or a non-Jew

Sources: The Torah; the Gemara

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer, Monday 1 June 2026

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Torah forbids a mamzer or mamzeret to marry a regular Jew. Whichever side is the mamzer, the child is a full mamzer, as the Gemara sets out; this is agreed by all.
  • A non-Jewish man with a Jewish mamzeret: there is no marriage, so the child follows the mother and is a Jewish mamzer or mamzeret.
  • A Jewish male mamzer with a non-Jewish woman: forbidden, but if it happens the child follows the mother and is not Jewish.
  • The concept of mamzer applies only to a Jew, so that child is not a mamzer. If he or she later converts, they are a full ger or giyoret and may marry regular Jews.
  • So paradoxically: between two Jews, one a mamzer, the child is a mamzer; with a non-Jew, it depends on whether the mother is the Jewish mamzeret.

In one line

A mamzeret's child is always a mamzer, but a male mamzer's child with a non-Jewish woman is not Jewish and, after conversion, not a mamzer.
