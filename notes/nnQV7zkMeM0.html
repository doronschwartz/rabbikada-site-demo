Seif 1: Muktzeh Mechamat Mius - מוקצה מחמת מיאוס

Halacha

עץ שתולין בו דגים, אף על פי שהוא מאוס, מותר לטלטלו, ואין כאן במוקצה מחמת מיאוס כרבי שמעון דשרי

A rod used by fishmongers with unpleasant smell is permitted to move on Shabbat.

Core Principle

  • Gemara debate: Rabbi Shimon vs Rabbi Yehuda - does unpleasant (מאוס) become muktzeh?
  • Halacha follows Rabbi Shimon: muktzeh mechamat mius does NOT exist
  • Items that are merely unpleasant do NOT become muktzeh

Critical Distinction: Two Categories

1. Unpleasant (מאוס) - NOT Muktzeh

  • Bad smells, mildly repulsive items
  • Examples: Fishmonger's rod with fishy smell
  • May be moved freely

2. Disgusting (גרף של רעי) - IS Muktzeh

  • Examples: Excrement from child/animal
  • Muktzeh because has no purpose/use (not because of disgust itself)
  • Special heter: May move if room is needed and item is in your presence
  • If room NOT needed (locked/unused room) - remains muktzeh

Seif 2: Food Never Becomes Muktzeh - אוכל אינו נעשה מוקצה

Fundamental Halacha

אין שום אוכל תלוש הראוי לאכילה מוקצה לשבת

Any detached, edible food can NEVER become muktzeh on Shabbat.

Two Conditions

  1. Detached (תלוש) - Picked/harvested from source
  2. Edible (ראוי לאכילה) - Fit for eating in current state

Why Food is Different from Utensils

Utensils for Sale (from Siman 308):

  • כלים העומדים לסחורה - utensils set aside for commerce ARE muktzeh
  • Cannot take dishes from your shop to use on Shabbat
  • You've set your mind away from them

Food for Sale:

  • Even food designated for commerce is NOT muktzeh
  • May take fruit/vegetables from shop and eat on Shabbat
  • Reason: Humans never truly set aside food from their minds
  • Nature of humans: always eating, always need food tomorrow

Specific Applications

1. Dates, Almonds, Fruits for Commerce

  • התמרים והשקדים ושאר פירות העומדים לסחורה מותר לאכלן בשבת
  • May go to shop, take produce, bring home, eat on Shabbat
  • Despite being merchandise inventory

2. Wheat Planted in Ground

  • ואפילו חיטים שזרען בקרקע ועדיין לא השרישו
  • Even wheat planted but not yet rooted - NOT muktzeh
  • You did explicit action setting it aside, still permitted
  • If already rooted: Cannot remove (would be kotzer/harvesting - different issue)

3. Eggs Under Hen for Hatching

  • וביצים שתחת התרנגולת מותר לטלן
  • Eggs placed under chicken to hatch - permitted to move and eat
  • You clearly set them aside for specific purpose, still not muktzeh
  • Important: Eggs must be laid before Shabbat
  • If laid on Shabbat = nolad (newly created) - different muktzeh category

4. Dates Left to Ripen

  • תמרים הלקוטים קודם בישולם ומכניסים אותם בסלים ומתבשלים עליהם
  • Unripe dates in baskets ripening - permitted to eat before fully ripe
  • Gathering them to ripen = action showing you're setting aside
  • Still not muktzeh if currently edible

Special Topic: The Etrog

Question: Is etrog considered edible for muktzeh purposes?

The Debate:

  • Technically can be eaten but extremely bitter
  • Some hold: Is edible, therefore cannot be muktzeh
  • Others hold: So bitter people don't eat it - NOT considered edible, CAN be muktzeh

Practical Impact:

  • During Sukkot: May be muktzeh if designated for mitzvah
  • After Sukkot: Debate about using for jam
  • Shows nuance in defining "edible" (ראוי לאכילה)

Seif 3: Basis L'Davar Ha'Asur - בסיס לדבר האסור

The Concept

When muktzeh placed on non-muktzeh item, under certain conditions the base also becomes muktzeh because it "serves" the prohibited item.

Three Conditions - ALL Must Be Met

Condition 1: Intentional Placement (Da'at) - דעת

Requirement: Muktzeh must be placed intentionally and with awareness

Creates Da'at:

  • Consciously placing item there
  • Deliberate choice
  • Example: Intentionally placing iPhone on bed

Does NOT Create Da'at:

  • Fell there accidentally - no basis
  • Placed absent-mindedly - questionable
  • Forgot you placed it - questionable

Condition 2: From Before Shabbat (Mib'od Yom) - מבעוד יום

Requirement: Muktzeh must be placed before Shabbat begins (or during bein hashemashot)

Why:

  • Basis status determined when Shabbat enters
  • Status is fixed for duration
  • Cannot create new basis once Shabbat began
  • Actions during Shabbat don't change muktzeh status

Example:

  • Wallet placed on chair before Shabbat → chair becomes basis
  • Someone places muktzeh on chair during Shabbat → does NOT create basis

Condition 3: Significance (Chashivut HaBasis) - חשיבות הבסיס

Most complex condition with several factors:

A) Item Must Have Value/Importance

Items with Importance (create basis):

  • Money, coins (monetary value)
  • Electronics (phone, watch, tablet)
  • Jewelry
  • Important documents
  • Sefarim (if muktzeh)

Items WITHOUT Importance (don't create basis):

  • Worthless pebble
  • Trash
  • Broken items with no value
  • Even if placed intentionally on bed before Shabbat

B) Proper Ratio Between Base and Muktzeh

Must be reasonable proportion between size/importance of base and muktzeh item.

Examples:

  • Small coin on large bed → Entire bed does NOT become basis
  • Phone on small table → Table likely DOES become basis
  • Phone on large couch → Entire couch may NOT become basis
  • Money on small tray → Tray DOES become basis
  • Money on large cabinet → Cabinet likely does NOT become basis

Principle: Extremely large base doesn't become entirely forbidden due to very small muktzeh item.

C) Item Not Easily Removable

  • If can easily remove muktzeh (shake off) → should do so, then use base
  • If removal difficult or requires touching muktzeh → basis fully applies

Additional Critical Rules

1. Basis Lasts Entire Shabbat

  • If item becomes basis, remains muktzeh ALL of Shabbat
  • Even if muktzeh later removed/falls off
  • Status determined at beginning of Shabbat and stays fixed

Example:

  • Wallet placed on tray before Shabbat → tray is basis
  • Wallet falls off Shabbat morning → tray STILL muktzeh rest of Shabbat

2. Ownership Determines Basis (Ba'alim)

Personal Property:

  • Your personal bed: Others cannot create basis for you
  • Wife leaves phone on your bed → does NOT make bed assur for you
  • She lacks authority to create basis on your bed

Communal Property:

  • Family sofa: Anyone in family can create basis
  • One person places muktzeh → becomes basis for EVERYONE
  • All have equal rights to communal items

3. Children and Da'at

  • Young child without da'at: Cannot create basis
  • Can be lenient until bar/bat mitzvah age
  • Child over bar/bat mitzvah on communal furniture: CAN create basis (has da'at and equal rights)

Practical Application: Money in Pocket

The Scenario

  • Put money in pants pocket on Tuesday
  • Shabbat morning, put on pants and discover money there

Question 1: Do Pants Become Basis?

Rama's Ruling: NO - הכיס בטל אצלו

  • Pocket is small part of pants
  • Entire garment doesn't become basis to one pocket
  • Pocket is nullified relative to whole garment

Question 2: Can You Walk Around House?

From Muktzeh Perspective:

  • Would be PERMITTED (pants not basis, moving money indirectly - tiltul min hatzad)
  • No intention of moving money

The Actual Prohibition:

  • Should NOT walk around with money in pocket
  • Not muktzeh concern, but hotza'ah (carrying) concern
  • דחיישינן שמא יצא בו - concern lest you go outside
  • Might forget and walk into reshut harabim

What To Do

If Discovered at Home:

  1. Take off pants
  2. Shake out money (tiltul min hatzad - permitted)
  3. Don't walk around wearing them

If Discovered in Reshut HaRabim:

  1. Walk home WITHOUT STOPPING
  2. Go directly to reshut hayachid (any private domain)
  3. Don't pause (stopping creates akirah v'hanachah - completing melacha)
  4. Once home, shake out money

Text from Shulchan Aruch

וכן בכיס התפור בבגד, הואיל ועיקר הבגד עומד ללבוש, אם מוציא המעות משם, מותר ללבוש הבגד, דהכיס בטל אצלו

"Pocket sewn in garment - since main purpose is wearing, if remove money, permitted to wear garment, for pocket is nullified relative to it"

אבל אם שכח בו מעות מותר לטלטל הבגד, דלא אמרינן דכל הבגד נעשה בסיס למעות, אף על פי שהמעות על עיקר הבגד

"But if forgot money in it, permitted to move garment, we don't say entire garment becomes basis for money, even though money is on main part of garment"

אבל אין ללבוש בו בשבת, דחיישינן שמא יצא בו

"But shouldn't wear it on Shabbat, for we're concerned lest he go out with it"

Three Key Points Summary

  1. Pocket Status: Entire garment doesn't become basis (kis batel atzlo)
  2. Muktzeh Perspective: Walking with money is indirect movement (tiltul min hatzad) - technically permitted
  3. Carrying Concern: Don't do it - concern you'll forget and carry outside (chashash hotza'ah, not chashash muktzeh)

Complete Summary

Seif 1: Muktzeh Mechamat Mius

  • Unpleasant (מאוס) items NOT muktzeh - follow Rabbi Shimon
  • Different from graf shel rei (disgusting) which IS muktzeh
  • Graf shel rei movable only if room needed

Seif 2: Food Never Muktzeh

  • Any detached, edible food cannot be muktzeh
  • Applies to commerce food, planted seeds, eggs for hatching, fruit ripening
  • Reason: Humans never set aside food from minds
  • Exceptions: Food attached to ground, nolad (created on Shabbat)

Seif 3: Basis L'Davar Ha'Asur

Three Conditions (all must be met):

  1. Da'at - intentional placement
  2. Mib'od yom - before Shabbat
  3. Chashivut - item has value + proper ratio + not easily removable

Key Rules:

  • Basis lasts entire Shabbat even if muktzeh removed
  • Owner determines basis (ba'alim)
  • Communal items: anyone can create basis for all
  • Children without da'at cannot create basis
  • Pockets don't make whole garment basis

Practical:

  • Money in pocket: Don't walk around (chashash hotza'ah)
  • If at home: shake out
  • If outside: walk home without stopping
