Topic: How to understand increasing joy in Adar in practice, according to the Meiri

Sources: The Meiri; the Megillah

Daily Halacha: Purim, Tuesday 4 March 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The points

  • Yesterday: the Rambam and Shulhan Arukh omit "mishenikhnas Adar marbim besimhah", perhaps because the Rambam does not hold of it. Yet it is widely quoted, sung and accepted, and some communities hung a sign with it in the synagogue from Rosh Hodesh Adar.
  • The Meiri (13th century, south of France) says it is not literal: unlike Av, Adar does not bring practical laws. It means taking time to recognise HaKadosh Barukh Hu's presence in one's life.
  • Why Adar, and not Nisan, Tishrei or Kislev with their miracles? The Megillah never mentions HaKadosh Barukh Hu, and the Purim miracle unfolded in a roundabout, seemingly coincidental way.
  • Yet His hand is behind everything; He supervises all that happens, and nothing is random. It is easy to go through life not seeing the hand of Hashem.
  • One who lives knowing that what happens to him has a reason, and is not mere chance, is naturally filled with joy. Adar is a month to contemplate this.
  • So either the Rambam does not hold of the idea, or we hold of it not as physical acts but as a change of outlook that naturally brings joy.
  • Tomorrow: the mazal of Adar.
