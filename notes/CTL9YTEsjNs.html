Topic: The third solution: Rabbi Tarfon's ruling that a mamzer may marry a maidservant

Sources: Mishnah Kiddushin (Rabbi Tarfon); the Rambam; the Geonim; Tosafot; the Gemara

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer, Thursday 4 June 2026

The halacha

  • The Mishnah in Kiddushin: Rabbi Tarfon says mamzerim can have children who are not mamzerim by marrying a maidservant (shifhah).
  • This is not a Jewish maidservant, who is a regular Jewess and whose children would be mamzerim, but a non-Jewish woman who becomes the slave of a Jewish man.
  • She accepts the misvot of a woman and goes through a process, and in the Rambam's words is no longer a full non-Jew but not yet a Jew: an intermediate status. This existed in Talmudic times and in the time of the Geonim, who have many teshuvot on it.
  • It is agreed that a mamzer may marry a shifhah kenaanit, though a regular Jew may not. The Rambam's reason: her prohibition is only rabbinic, so it was allowed for a mamzer. Tosafot's: a mamzer is already tainted.
  • Their children are avadim, servant children. When he has finished having children, he frees her and the children, and they become fully Jewish.
  • The Gemara says this may be done in the first instance. So in the times of slavery a mamzer could buy and marry a non-Jewish slave, then free her and the children, who are full Jews and not mamzerim.
  • Whether this works today follows tomorrow.

In the times of slavery, a mamzer could marry his non-Jewish maidservant and free her and their children, who became full Jews.
