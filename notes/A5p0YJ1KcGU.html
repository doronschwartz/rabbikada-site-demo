Topic: Celebrating the festival of freedom while the war in Israel continues and hostages are held

Sources: The Torah; the Hakhamim; the Haggadah; the Rebbe (the fifth son)

Daily Halacha: Pesah, Friday 19 April 2024

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The points

  • The Torah calls the festival Hag HaPesah and Hag HaMassot; the Hakhamim added Zeman Herutenu, the time of our freedom. This year, with the war continuing and over 130 hostages still in captivity, the mood across the Jewish people is sombre.
  • The Haggadah is not only about victory: an essential part is recalling the difficult past. Pesah gave us the right to freedom over 3,300 years ago, but we must work for it, and we cannot hold the Seder without recognising how this year is different.
  • The four sons: the Rebbe spoke of a fifth son who does not even come to the table. This year we think of the fifth son who cannot come, the hostages and those fighting to protect our brothers and sisters.
  • At Yahas, the broken massah is the bread of poverty, and also the brokenness of our hearts and our nation.
  • Dipping the karpas into salt water recalls bitter tears: of our ancestors in Egypt and of the families of those murdered, the soldiers lost and those still in captivity, our brothers and sisters in Gaza and in Israel.
  • Each family should find ways to bring what is happening in Israel alive at the Seder.
  • Even in normal years our freedom is incomplete, nationally in exile and personally. Pesah celebrates the right to freedom and reminds us to work towards a world in which we, and everyone, are free.

In one line

This year's Seder must hold our incomplete freedom alongside its celebration, with the fifth son, the broken massah and the bitter tears speaking to the war and the hostages.
