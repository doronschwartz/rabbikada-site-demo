Topic: The two aspects of Kiddush on Seder night, starting early, and how much to drink

Daily Halacha: Pesah, Monday 8 April 2024

The halacha

  • Kiddush on Seder night is first the regular Kiddush, a Torah misvah on Shabbat and, according to many, on Yom Tov. For that alone, one person drinking would suffice.
  • It is also the first of the four cups, which the Hakhamim instituted for each person to drink to experience herut, freedom. Experience cannot be delegated, so everyone needs their own cup poured.
  • Four is a number of completeness, and marks each key part of the Seder: Kiddush, the Haggadah, Birkat HaMazon and Hallel. Other reasons: the four exiles, and the four expressions of redemption in Parashat Vaera.
  • This year nightfall is about 8:30 to 8:45 in England. The Shulhan Arukh is clear that the Seder begins after nightfall, and where possible one should wait.
  • If that is hard, for the elderly or young children, Kiddush may be made after sunset (about 8 o'clock), since Yom Tov can be brought in early. One may continue with Urhas, words of Torah from the children and Yahas, but Maggid, a Torah misvah, must be after nightfall.
  • One drinks a reviit, about 86 ml (roughly 90 ml), in the first instance; if necessary a cheekful (melo lugmav), about 44 ml.

Sources quoted

  • "Zakhor et Yom HaShabbat Lekadsho": Kiddush on Shabbat is from the Torah.
  • Parashat Vaera: the four expressions of redemption, one reason for four cups.
  • Shulhan Arukh: the Seder begins after nightfall.

In one line

Seder Kiddush is both Kiddush and one of the four cups, may begin after sunset when needed if Maggid waits for nightfall, and needs a reviit.
