Topic: The final meal on Shabbat before Tisha BeAv, and the hour between sunset and the end of Shabbat

Sources: The Gemara

Daily Halacha: The Three Weeks, Tuesday 13 July 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Normally the final meal on the afternoon before Tisha BeAv has many laws. This year Erev Tisha BeAv is Shabbat, so the final meal, Seudah Shelishit, has none of them: one may eat anything, with anyone.
  • The Gemara says one may even set a feast like the banquets of Shelomo HaMelekh, with meat and wine; though an hour later we mourn, until then Shabbat is a time of joy with food, wine and song.
  • The problem is twilight, doubtful day or night. In London Shabbat ends about 10:15 while sunset is about 9:10, so for over an hour it is still Shabbat yet the 9th of Av may have begun.
  • The consensus: refrain from anything passive, do nothing active. Stopping eating and drinking at sunset takes nothing from Shabbat, so one must stop then.
  • But actively removing shoes, changing into weekday clothes or sitting on the floor would detract from Shabbat, so one keeps shoes, Shabbat clothes and chair until Shabbat ends.
  • If one needs to wash hands in that hour, only up to the knuckles, as on Tisha BeAv.
  • From sunset one studies only the Torah permitted on Tisha BeAv. Reading Eikhah as study, not mournfully, is fine in that hour.

Sources quoted

  • The Gemara: on Shabbat before Tisha BeAv one may set a table like the banquets of Shelomo HaMelekh.

In one line

Seudah Shelishit before Tisha BeAv is a normal Shabbat meal, and from sunset one stops eating but changes nothing actively until Shabbat ends.
