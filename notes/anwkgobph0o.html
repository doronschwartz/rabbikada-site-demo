Topic: Kashering ovens, oven racks and trays, and dishwashers for Pesah

Sources: Hakham Ovadiah Yoseph; Rav Shlomo Zalman Auerbach

Daily Halacha: Pesah, Wednesday 2 April 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • An oven with a self-clean cycle reaching about 350 to 400 degrees achieves Libbun. Just run it: no 24-hour wait is needed, and strictly speaking not even cleaning, since Libbun eradicates any hames.
  • Without self-clean, the main concern is steam from hames cooking absorbed in the walls, which is kashered by running the oven at its highest temperature. Spills are covered by majority use for Sepharadim, and Pesah food does not touch the walls anyway.
  • Since the highest setting may not be true Libbun, one must clean the oven first, and Hakham Ovadiah says preferably to leave it unused 24 hours before.
  • Racks and baking trays touch hames directly and need Libbun. A self-clean at that temperature works if they survive it; otherwise get new ones for Pesah. A lenient view allows Libbun at the temperature of absorption, and one who relies on it has what to rely on, but ideally use new ones.
  • Some treat a dishwasher as a Keli Rishon on the fire, needing Hagalah, which is impossible, so they do not use it on Pesah.
  • Most poskim allow kashering it: only water heated in the machine itself is a Keli Rishon, while water sprayed in counts as pouring. Clean the filter and run it at the highest temperature. Many Ashkenazim agree, including Rav Shlomo Zalman Auerbach.

Sources quoted

  • Hakham Ovadiah Yoseph: clean and preferably wait 24 hours when the oven cannot reach Libbun.
  • Rav Shlomo Zalman Auerbach: a dishwasher can be kashered.

In one line

Self-clean kashers an oven outright, otherwise clean, wait and run it hot; racks need Libbun or replacing; a dishwasher is kashered by cleaning the filter and running it hot.
