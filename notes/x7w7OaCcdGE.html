Topic: When the restrictions end after Tisha BeAv, and meat and wine on the 10th of Av

Sources: Shulhan Arukh; the Rama; the Hakhamim

Daily Halacha: The Three Weeks, Thursday 27 July 2023

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Shulhan Arukh writes clearly that straight after the fast one may do laundry and have a haircut. For Sepharadim, tonight after the fast, haircuts, washing and laundry are all permitted.
  • The Shulhan Arukh records it as the correct custom not to eat meat or drink wine the whole of the 10th of Av, this year all of Friday.
  • The reason is not mourning, since a mourner may eat meat during shivah, but to remember the Temple, where meat offerings and wine libations were brought.
  • The Hakhamim tell us the Temple was set alight on the afternoon of the 9th and burned throughout the 10th. The fast was fixed on the 9th, as the beginning of the calamity is more fitting to mark, but on the 10th it was still ablaze.
  • The Rama writes the Ashkenazi custom is only until midday. Some Sepharadi communities did the same (Turkish and some Moroccan), and some, as recorded in Tunisia and Djerba, ate meat straight after the fast.
  • Without a custom, Sepharadim wait the whole 10th; Ashkenazim until midday.
  • Ashkenazim do not cut hair or do laundry on the night after Tisha BeAv. This year, with the 10th on Friday, all agree these are permitted from Friday morning to prepare for Shabbat.

Sources quoted

  • Shulhan Arukh: laundry and haircuts immediately after the fast; no meat or wine on the 10th.
  • The Rama: the Ashkenazi custom only until midday.
  • The Hakhamim: the Temple burned through the 10th of Av.

In one line

Sepharadim may wash, launder and cut hair right after Tisha BeAv but customarily avoid meat and wine all of the 10th, while Ashkenazim wait until midday.
