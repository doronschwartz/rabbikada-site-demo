Topic: The three levels of Hanukkah lighting and why Sepharadim light one Menorah per household

Sources: The Gemara ("Ner ish uveito"); Rambam; Tosafot; Shulhan Arukh; the Rema

Daily Halacha: Hanukkah

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Lighting the Menorah is a misvah of the Hakhamim. The Gemara gives three levels: one candle each night for the whole household ("ner ish uveito"); mehadrin, one candle each night for each person; mehadrin min hamehadrin, adding a candle each night.
  • The Rishonim debate whether the third level builds on the second or the first.
  • Rambam: each person lights an added candle each night, so a family of four has 32 candles on the eighth night.
  • Tosafot: the added candle shows which night it is; if several people each light, one cannot tell (two people on the third night light six). So only one person lights for the household.
  • Very unusually, the Shulhan Arukh follows Tosafot against the Rambam, so Sepharadim light one Menorah for the family, while the Rema follows the Rambam, and Ashkenazim each light their own.
  • One who has only one candle, even on the eighth night, fulfils the misvah completely, since the added candles are only beautification.
  • Sepharadim should not have different family members lighting; the Baal HaBayit lights an added candle each night for everyone.

In one line

Following Tosafot, Sepharadim have the head of the household light one Menorah for all, while Ashkenazim follow the Rambam and each light.
