Topic: Listening to music and saying Shehiyanu during the Omer

Sources: Shulhan Arukh; the Rambam; Gemara Gittin; the poskim

Daily Halacha: Sefirat HaOmer, Wednesday 14 April 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Shulhan Arukh mentions only weddings and haircuts in the Omer. Some wrongly conclude he permits music.
  • In fact, following the Rambam and the Gemara in Gittin, the Shulhan Arukh forbids playing or listening to music all year since the destruction of the Temple, so there was no need to mention it for the Omer. A more lenient view forbids it only in a merry setting with wine.
  • Most people do listen to music during the year; why that became common practice is for another time.
  • The poskim write that at least in the Omer, a time tinged with sadness, one should keep this halacha more and not listen to music until Lag BaOmer.
  • One who is very down may be lenient, since we are lenient all year.
  • Music for a misvah is permitted: a bar misvah, a berit milah, or a siyum masekhet, which is a seudat misvah.
  • Shehiyanu: Shulhan Arukh says it is good not to say it in the Three Weeks. Some extend this to the Omer, but that is incorrect, since the Omer is essentially happy. One may say it on new fruit and clothes even on weekdays; one who wishes to be strict can wear the new suit first on Shabbat and say it then.

Sources quoted

In one line

In the first instance avoid music in the Omer except for a misvah or to lift one's spirits, and Shehiyanu may be said.
