Topic: Artificial insemination for a single woman, and ranking the four cases

Sources: The Torah (Rachel to Yaakov)

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • A single woman with a Jewish donor: no mamzerut, as she is not married, but there is the concern of siblings marrying one another.
  • A single woman with a non-Jewish donor: no real halachic issue, but hashkafic and ethical ones: bringing non-Jewish lineage into the Jewish people, and raising a child without a known father.
  • Yet for a single woman who has not found her match it can be a very serious matter, as when Rachel, unable to have children, told Yaakov she needed children or she did not want to live.
  • Often the question is not whether to do it but what to be aware of. First, egg freezing, new in recent times, can take off the pressure, since age affects the eggs more than carrying, giving more time to marry and have children naturally.
  • Second, if she has decided and cannot be dissuaded, it is certainly better to use a non-Jewish donor than a Jewish one.
  • Of the four cases, a married woman with a Jewish donor is the most serious halachically, and a single woman with a non-Jewish donor the least.

In one line

For a single woman set on insemination, freezing eggs is one option, and a non-Jewish donor is halachically far better than a Jewish one.
