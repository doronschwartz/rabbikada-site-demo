Topic: What size the ten pieces of Hames should be

Sources: Hakham Ovadiah; the Mishnah Berurah

Daily Halacha: Pesah, Tuesday 2 April 2024

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • From the Torah, nullification suffices; the Hakhamim required disposal and a search.
  • The vast majority hold the search is for a Kezayit, about 27 grams, since only owning a Kezayit violates the Torah prohibition. Less is insignificant for the search, though eating any Hames on Pesah is forbidden.
  • Some required searching for less, but Hakham Ovadiah rules clearly it is a Kezayit, and the Mishnah Berurah seems to agree, even for Ashkenazim. So one searches for real pieces, relying on nullification for crumbs.
  • The ten pieces exist so that one finds something and the blessing is not in vain. Finding less than a Kezayit is not considered finding anything, so tiny sealed crumbs miss the point.
  • But a full Kezayit in one piece might not be found, leaving a Kezayit of Hames in one's possession.
  • The best way is to divide a single Kezayit among the ten pieces: finding all ten means a Kezayit was found, and if one is lost, it is under a Kezayit and one relies on nullification.
  • If larger pieces were put out and one is missing, it is a problem and must be properly searched for.

Sources quoted

In one line

Split one Kezayit of Hames across the ten pieces, so that together they make a real find and none alone is a problem if lost.
