Topic: The source of the Three Weeks and the two things the Shulhan Arukh records for them

Sources: The Gemara; Midrash Eikhah; the Rambam; Shulhan Arukh; Sefer Hasidim; the Vilna Gaon

Daily Halacha: The Three Weeks, Monday 5 July 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Gemara does not mention the Three Weeks; its restrictions begin only from Rosh Hodesh Av, the Nine Days. The Rambam, codifying the Gemara, mentions none.
  • The idea comes from Midrash Eikhah on "all her pursuers overtook her bein hamesarim": between the boundaries of the 17th of Tammuz and Tisha BeAv, a time of reflection and sadness.
  • For Sepharadim, the Shulhan Arukh records very little. First, based on another Midrash, "one must be careful" to avoid anything dangerous that one would think twice about the rest of the year; his examples include not hitting students or children and not going to dangerous places.
  • Swimming is not intrinsically wrong, especially for exercise, provided it is in a safe environment, for example with lifeguards; the concern is danger, not leisure.
  • Second, Sefer Hasidim argued that Shehiyanu, a blessing of happiness, is inappropriate now. The Shulhan Arukh writes only "it is good to be careful": preferably wait until after the Three Weeks, but for a misvah such as a Brit Milah, or a fruit not available afterwards, say it. The Vilna Gaon saw no problem at all.
  • One may buy a new suit or dress; it is good to postpone wearing it with Shehiyanu until after Tisha BeAv. On Shabbat, a happy time, Shehiyanu may be said according to halacha, though some are particular.

Sources quoted

  • Midrash Eikhah: "bein hamesarim" is the period between the 17th of Tammuz and Tisha BeAv.
  • Shulhan Arukh: "one must be careful" about danger; "it is good to be careful" about Shehiyanu.
  • Sefer Hasidim: not to say Shehiyanu in this period.
  • The Vilna Gaon: no source for avoiding Shehiyanu.

In one line

For Sepharadim the Shulhan Arukh asks only to avoid danger and, where possible, to postpone Shehiyanu during the Three Weeks.
