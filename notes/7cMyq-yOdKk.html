Topic: The children of a woman who remarried thinking her husband had died, and the rabbinic mamzer

Sources: The Mishnah

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • When the first husband returns, she needs a get from both men. From the Torah she was never married to man two, but the Hakhamim required a get because people thought she was married, and would see her marry a third man without one.
  • The Mishnah says a child from either man is a mamzer. Children with her husband before he left are of course regular Jewish children.
  • Children with man two are mamzerim from the Torah: her husband was alive all along, so the relationship was adultery, though unintended and based on a ruling of the Beit Din.
  • Now suppose she goes back to man one without receiving a get from man two. From the Torah she may do so, since she was never married to man two.
  • The Hakhamim required a get so it should not look as if she returned to her husband without one. Children born to man one after she returned without that get are mamzerim miderabbanan: regular Jews from the Torah, mamzerim rabbinically.
  • A rabbinic mamzer is in a difficult position: he cannot marry a regular Jew, being a rabbinic mamzer, nor a mamzeret, being a regular Jew from the Torah. He may marry only a rabbinic mamzeret or a convert, for reasons to be explained.
