Topic: Washing clothes on Hol HaMoed, and going to work

Sources: Hazon Ovadiah

Daily Halacha: Sukkot, Thursday 5 October 2023

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Washing clothes is maaseh hedyot and would seem permitted for festival use, but the Hakhamim forbade it lest people leave laundry to pile up until the festival.
  • Children's clothes may be washed, since children get dirty and cannot keep clothes clean. The age varies, roughly six to nine; each parent judges their child. A minority lets adults add their own clothes to the load, but the halacha does not follow this.
  • Items that regularly get dirty, like hand towels, may be washed; on this basis Hazon Ovadiah permits washing underwear, even for adults, if more is needed. The prohibition covers shirts, trousers, dresses and the like.
  • One with only one garment may wash it. The poskim say one who has only one Shabbat suit and needs it for the second Yom Tov may wash it, though today we rarely have just one.
  • Work: a self-employed person should do his best to take off. The heter is davar haaved, an actual loss, like grapes that will fall and spoil if not harvested now, not mere loss of profit.
  • A shop owner who can open after the festival without harm should; but if closing eight or nine days would really damage the shop's standing and future sales, there are grounds to open.
  • An employee with limited holiday should ask his Rav; he does not want to rule on this here.

Sources quoted

  • Hazon Ovadiah: underwear may be washed on Hol HaMoed, even for adults.

In one line

No laundry on Hol HaMoed except children's clothes and items that soil daily, and work is allowed only to prevent a real loss.
