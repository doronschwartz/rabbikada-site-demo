Topic: Shehiyanu and music during the Three Weeks

Sources: Sefer Hasidim; Shulhan Arukh; the Aharonim; the Magen Avraham; the Gemara

Daily Halacha: The Three Weeks, Friday 25 July 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The fourth custom in the Shulhan Arukh is Shehiyanu. Sefer Hasidim, about 600 to 700 years ago, wrote that thanking Hashem for reaching this time is inappropriate in a sad period.
  • The Shulhan Arukh does not call it forbidden but says "it is good to be careful". Some Aharonim disregarded it, but it has become the custom of Sepharadim and Ashkenazim.
  • Caveats: it concerns only saying Shehiyanu. Since today Shehiyanu is said on wearing new clothes, not buying them, one may buy clothes until Rosh Hodesh.
  • Where Shehiyanu cannot be said later, such as on seeing a beloved friend not seen or heard from for over 30 days, or on a new fruit, one says it according to everyone.
  • According to most poskim it does not apply on Shabbat.
  • Music is not in the Shulhan Arukh because he rules, based on the Gemara, against musical instruments all year round. How listening to live music, even not at a misvah, became so widespread needs discussion.
  • Even so, based on the Magen Avraham, the first to record it about 400 years ago, and the Aharonim, it is appropriate not to listen to live music in the Three Weeks.
  • Recorded music, such as the radio, for entertainment is certainly wrong from Rosh Hodesh. Before that there are poskim on both sides.

Sources quoted

  • Sefer Hasidim: Shehiyanu is inappropriate in this period.
  • Shulhan Arukh: "it is good to be careful" about Shehiyanu; music is a problem all year.
  • The Magen Avraham: not to listen to music or dance in the Three Weeks.

In one line

Avoid Shehiyanu in the Three Weeks unless the chance will pass or it is Shabbat, and avoid live music, and recorded music for entertainment from Rosh Hodesh.
