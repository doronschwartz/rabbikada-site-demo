Topic: The earliest (Plag HaMinhah) and latest (two minutes before sunset) times to accept Shabbat and light candles

Sources: Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Shabbat, Thursday 3 June 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Shulhan Arukh sets the earliest time to accept Shabbat at Plag HaMinhah, an hour and a quarter halachic hours before the end of the day. A halachic hour is a twelfth of the day, longer in summer and shorter in winter.
  • With sunset in London at 9:10, Plag HaMinhah is about 7:30, not 7:55. One may pray Minhah and perhaps Kabbalat Shabbat earlier, but Arvit, Kiddush and candle lighting must wait until Plag HaMinhah; in London it never gets later than about 7:37.
  • Candles lit before Plag HaMinhah are not a valid lighting or acceptance: extinguish them and light again, without another berakhah.
  • From sunset work is forbidden by the Torah. Most London calendars give Shabbat as 15 minutes before sunset, and one should try to accept by then.
  • In pressing circumstances one may work and light until two minutes before sunset, because of the Torah misvah of Tosefet Shabbat, whose minimum is two minutes.
  • At two minutes before sunset, stop everything and accept Shabbat without lighting; adding to Shabbat matters more than the candles.

In one line

Shabbat may be accepted from Plag HaMinhah and no later than two minutes before sunset, after which candles are not lit.
