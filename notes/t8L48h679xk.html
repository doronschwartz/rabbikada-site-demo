Topic: The latest age for marriage, and whether Beit Din could compel it

Sources: The Gemara; the Rambam; Shulhan Arukh; the Rosh; the Rema

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer, Monday 26 January 2026

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The proper time to marry is 18 (17 by the Rambam). The Shulhan Arukh says it is wrong to marry before 13, irrelevant today.
  • The Gemara sets 20 as the latest: by then a man should be married, otherwise he will struggle all his life with improper thoughts. The Rambam and Shulhan Arukh codify this, giving a window of 18 to 20.
  • The Rosh held that Beit Din, which then had far more authority, could compel a man of 20 to marry by imposing sanctions. The Shulhan Arukh codifies this.
  • Other Rishonim disagreed; the Rambam did not codify it, seemingly because one cannot force someone to marry against his will. The Rema's view follows in the next note.

In one line

A man should marry between 18 and 20; the Shulhan Arukh even lets Beit Din press a man of 20 to marry, though others disagree.
