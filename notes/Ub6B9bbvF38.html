Topic: Whether to pray Minhah before lighting the Menorah on Friday of Hanukkah

Sources: The Rishonim; Rabbi Shemuel Abuhav; the Hida; the Ben Ish Hai

Daily Halacha: Hanukkah

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • On Erev Shabbat Hanukkah the Menorah is lit before Shabbat. Many Rishonim record that in the synagogue they lit before Minhah, then prayed Minhah and accepted Shabbat, so that latecomers would not cause the Menorah to be lit after Shabbat began. Some Rishonim placed the lighting between Minhah and Kabbalat Shabbat.
  • Either way people lit at home and came to pray Minhah late in the afternoon.
  • The first to call for early Minhah was Rabbi Shemuel Abuhav (Italy, 17th century): the Friday lighting already belongs to Shabbat, Minhah belongs to the day before, and in the Mikdash the afternoon offering, to which Minhah corresponds, preceded lighting the Menorah. So pray Minhah early (around 12.30 or 1), go home to light, and return for Kabbalat Shabbat.
  • The Hida quotes this, and the Ben Ish Hai calls lighting before Minhah a minhag of the ignorant, though it was the Rishonim's common practice.
  • Some say it is better to pray Minhah alone than to light first and then pray with the sibbur. This is rejected.
  • Halachically: if one can pray an early Minhah with a minyan, then light, that is ideal. If not, light at home, then pray Minhah and Arvit and accept Shabbat; never give up a minyan for this.

In one line

Ideally pray an early Minhah with a minyan before lighting on Friday of Hanukkah, but never at the cost of the minyan.
