Topic: The parents' obligation to help their children marry

Sources: The Gemara; Yirmiyahu ("Take wives for your sons and give your daughters to husbands"); the Rambam

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer, Wednesday 4 February 2026

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Besides each person's own obligation, the Gemara lists among a father's duties to his son marrying him off, learnt from Yirmiyahu: "take wives for your sons, and give your daughters to husbands".
  • The obligation is mainly on the father, since a woman is not formally commanded in "Peru urevu". But as there is some misvah for women to marry and have children, it would seem to apply to mothers too.
  • Some Rishonim see it as Divrei Kabbalah (from the Prophets), between Torah and rabbinic law. The Rambam, as his commentators read him, treats it as rabbinic, with the verse only a support.
  • It is not enforced, but a father should be willing to give up to a tenth of his assets to marry off his daughter. The poskim say not more, so as not to create an expectation that grooms will receive everything.

In one line

Parents, mainly the father but seemingly the mother too, must help their children marry, even up to a tenth of their assets.
