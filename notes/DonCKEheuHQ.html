Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer, Friday 17 April 2026

The halacha

  • Though by the Gemara, Rambam and Shulhan Arukh a self-declared Kohen is not believed, the widespread minhag among Ashkenazim and Sepharadim is to call him up to the Torah first.
  • It extends to Birkat Kohanim, though that is more severe, as a non-Kohen may not do it. The Beit Shemuel explains that the Rosh's reason was financial gain, and there is none in Birkat Kohanim. This is difficult, since the Rosh also mentioned the prestige of a Kohen, but it is the halacha today.
  • Pidyon HaBen raises a question, since the father gives the Kohen money, a clear gain.
  • The Hatam Sofer answers: Pidyon HaBen is rare; it is usually given to a Kohen from the family; and in many cases the Kohen gives the money back. This must be done properly, not as an exchange, and it is still common practice.
  • Together these allow us to accept a self-declared Kohen for Pidyon HaBen too, unless it is clear someone is trying to profit.
  • The Rambam distinguishes a Kohen muhzak, presumed to be a Kohen, accepted for the aliyah, Birkat Kohanim and even Pidyon HaBen, from a Kohen meyuhas, of certain lineage with testimony, who may even eat Terumah from the Torah.
  • Most Kohanim today are Kohen muhzak, presumed without hard evidence.

In one line

Today one who says he is a Kohen is accepted as a Kohen muhzak for the aliyah, Birkat Kohanim and Pidyon HaBen, though not as a Kohen meyuhas.
