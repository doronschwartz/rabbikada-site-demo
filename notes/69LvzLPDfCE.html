Topic: Applying the distinction between professional and ordinary work to cleaning, furniture and gardening

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Monday 6 October 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The distinction between melekhet uman (professional) and melekhet hedyot (ordinary) runs through all the halachot of Hol HaMoed.
  • Sweeping and hoovering are everyday activities, melekhet hedyot, so permitted, provided they are needed for the moed. Cleaning the house on the last day of Hol HaMoed before going away for Yom Tov, so it is clean on return, would be wrong.
  • Occasional, professional jobs, such as cleaning sofa fabric or having the windows cleaned, are melekhet uman and not allowed.
  • A broken chair may be fixed temporarily, for example with sellotape, if it will be used on the moed; a permanent repair, by oneself or a professional, is melekhet uman and wrong.
  • Watering the garden, forbidden from the Torah on Shabbat, is no more than melekhet hedyot on Hol HaMoed, permitted when it is a regular weekly need for the moed. If it is not needed, leave it until after; if the plants would otherwise wither, it is permitted as davar haaved.

In one line

Ordinary cleaning, temporary repairs and routine watering for the moed are permitted on Hol HaMoed, but professional jobs are not.
