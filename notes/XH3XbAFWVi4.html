Topic: How the misvah of Hakhel was performed in Temple times

Sources: Parashat Vayelekh; the Gemara

Daily Halacha: Sukkot, Thursday 9 October 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Hakhel (Parashat Vayelekh): once every seven years, in the year after Shemitah, men, women and children gathered to hear the Torah read publicly. Unlike aliyah laregel, which is for males, everyone was obligated.
  • Despite "you shall read this Torah", the Gemara derives that only parts of Devarim were read: the beginning of the book, Shema, the blessings and curses, and more that is discussed.
  • It had to be in Leshon HaKodesh. Even those who could not understand had to come, since being present at the occasion is part of the misvah.
  • The Hakhamim said the king read it; without a king, another leader such as the Nasi or Kohen Gadol.
  • It should have been the first day, but the Gemara says a huge platform was built, which cannot be done on Yom Tov and beforehand would cause crowding and accidents. So it was read on the second day, straight after Yom Tov.
  • The Kohanim went around blowing trumpets to announce it, and special berakhot were said only then, such as "Hashokhen beSiyyon".
  • Its significance nowadays, which Hazon Ovadiah discusses, is for next time.
