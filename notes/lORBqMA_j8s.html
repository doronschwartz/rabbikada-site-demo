Topic: Introduction to the blessings for good and bad occasions: Shehiyanu, HaTov VeHaMetiv and Dayan HaEmet

Sources: The Bet Yoseph

Daily Halacha: Peculiar Berachot, Monday 24 February 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Hakhamim instituted berakhot for the good and the bad times in life, to express that everything comes from HaKadosh Barukh Hu. In their day this negated dualism, two powers for good and bad; for us too it affirms His unity.
  • For good occasions there are Shehiyanu and HaTov VeHaMetiv; for sad ones, Dayan HaEmet.
  • There are two kinds of Shehiyanu. The obligatory one is said on reaching milestones such as the festivals, or on doing a new misvah. That is not the subject here.
  • The second kind, according to most poskim and the Bet Yoseph, is a berakhah of reshut (voluntary), because it depends on the person's own joy. It is still a misvah to try to say it.
  • Shehiyanu is for joy that benefits only oneself. When the joy is shared by more than one person, the berakhah is HaTov VeHaMetiv, "who is good and bestows good on others".
  • Example: a single person who wins the lottery says Shehiyanu on hearing the news. If he is married and the money goes into a joint account shared equally, either spouse says HaTov VeHaMetiv on behalf of both.

In one line

Personal joy calls for Shehiyanu, shared joy for HaTov VeHaMetiv, and both proclaim that everything comes from Hashem.
