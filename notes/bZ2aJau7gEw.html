Topic: The hour between sunset and nightfall when Shabbat leads into Tisha BeAv

Sources: The Gemara (the meal of Shelomo HaMelekh)

Daily Halacha: The Three Weeks, Wednesday 3 August 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • This year Erev Tisha BeAv is Shabbat, so the usual restrictions of the final meal do not apply. On Shabbat there is no mourning, and Seudah Shelishit may be anything, even like the meal of Shelomo HaMelekh, with meat and friends.
  • Every day twilight (bein hashemashot) is doubtful day or doubtful night, so we are strict both ways, which is why Shabbat lasts 25 hours.
  • This Shabbat that creates a clash: in London sunset is about 8:40 and nightfall about 9:40. For that hour it may still be Shabbat, or already Tisha BeAv.
  • The consensus: avoid positive action, but keep to passive restraint. Stopping eating and drinking at sunset violates nothing of Shabbat, so one stops then; eating after sunset might directly violate Tisha BeAv.
  • Actively removing shoes, sitting on the floor or changing into weekday clothes would go against the spirit of Shabbat, so these wait until nightfall.
  • From sunset, washing hands is only up to the knuckles, as on Tisha BeAv.
  • One should not study the happy parts of Torah then. One may study what is allowed on Tisha BeAv, such as Eikhah or Iyov, but academically rather than mournfully, since it may still be Shabbat.

Sources quoted

  • The Gemara: on Shabbat before Tisha BeAv one may eat even like the meal of Shelomo HaMelekh.

In one line

Between sunset and the end of Shabbat before Tisha BeAv one stops eating but takes no active step against Shabbat until nightfall.
