Topic: When one must check that a person asking for sedakah is genuine

Sources: The Gemara (a dispute of the Amoraim)

Daily Halacha: Tsedakah & Maaser, Thursday 24 February 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Gemara says that even then there were rammaim, deceivers, who asked for sedakah without qualifying, and that the reward is reserved for those who give to the right people, aniyim hagunim.
  • One who means well but fails to check and gives to the wrong person is not rewarded: he has simply been conned.
  • The Amoraim disagree whether one checks requests for clothes or for food. The accepted halacha: one asking for food because he is hungry is given at once without checking, since the need is immediate.
  • Anything else (medical expenses, clothes, accommodation) requires a bedikah, a check that the need is genuine.
  • How to check is not specified. Communities were once small and people knew each other; today a stranger may come from another country and checking is very hard.
  • So many cities have a vaad sedakah that investigates collectors and issues certificates. A prominent rav told him he does not give in London to collectors without a certificate, relying on the organisation's due diligence.

In one line

Give food to the hungry without questions, but for anything else check first, and where a vaad sedakah exists give to those it has certified.
