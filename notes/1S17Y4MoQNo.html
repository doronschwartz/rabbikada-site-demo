Topic: Further details of laundry on Hol HaMoed: extra items, hotels, stains and one festive garment

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Tuesday 28 October 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • When doing a permitted wash (children's clothes or other items needed for the moed), may one add clothes not needed until after? Some poskim were lenient, but the general consensus is to wash only what is needed for the moed.
  • Hotels, for example in Israel, even religious ones, may wash towels and bedsheets for guests coming and going on the moed. They are not expected to stock extra linen; these get dirty regularly and are washed for each new guest.
  • The decree covers a full wash. Cleaning a stain or dust off a jacket or dress with water, forbidden on Shabbat and Yom Tov, is permitted on Hol HaMoed.
  • The poskim permit washing one's only garment if it gets dirty. Today this applies to one who has only one special suit or dress for the festival, even if he has weekday clothes.

In one line

Wash only what is needed for the moed, but hotels may wash linen, stains may be cleaned, and one's only festive outfit may be washed.
