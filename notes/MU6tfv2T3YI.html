Topic: Music and dancing during the Three Weeks for Sepharadim

Sources: Shulhan Arukh; the Rama; the later Sepharadi poskim

Daily Halacha: The Three Weeks, Tuesday 19 July 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Gemara and Rambam mention no laws for the Three Weeks; from the Midrashim the Shulhan Arukh records avoiding danger ("one must be careful") and refraining from Shehiyanu where it can wait until after Tisha BeAv ("it is commendable").
  • A third, recorded much later by Sepharadi poskim, is music and dancing. The Shulhan Arukh omits it because he rules music questionable all year round.
  • The Rama is more lenient, so Ashkenazi poskim write that at least in the Three Weeks one should refrain. The later poskim say Sepharadim, though lenient during the year, should at least follow this custom now.
  • A professional who teaches or performs music has what to rely on to continue, even until the week of Tisha BeAv if there is great need, though preferably stopping from Rosh Hodesh.
  • Music and dancing for a misvah, such as a Brit Milah, Bar Misvah or wedding, are fully permitted.
  • Otherwise, until Rosh Hodesh Av, Sepharadim may hold weddings and have haircuts; refraining became the Ashkenazi custom, never the Sepharadi. A wedding is certainly permitted, especially when delay would cause difficulties such as finding a venue.

Sources quoted

  • Shulhan Arukh: care about danger; Shehiyanu; music questionable all year.
  • The Rama: lenient on music during the year.
  • The later poskim: Sepharadim should refrain from music in the Three Weeks.

In one line

Sepharadim avoid music and dancing in the Three Weeks except for a misvah or livelihood, and may marry and cut hair until Rosh Hodesh Av.
