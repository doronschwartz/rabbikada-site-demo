Topic: The child of a betrothed woman (arusah), and of an engaged woman today

Sources: The Gemara

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer, Tuesday 30 June 2026

The halacha

  • In halacha, an arusah is betrothed with a ring, not what we call engaged today.
  • For mamzerut a betrothed woman has the status of a married woman: a child from another man is a mamzer. So most men around her are forbidden to her.
  • Nonetheless, the Gemara says clearly, agreed by all, that if she says the child is from her betrothed, she is believed and the child is kasher, provided he does not deny it. If he is silent, absent or has died, she is believed.
  • If he denies it, saying he was never with her, the child is a definite mamzer, as with a father's power to declare his child a mamzer.
  • If she does not know, having been with her betrothed and another man, or cannot be asked, the child is a shetuki, a safek mamzer, with all its laws.
  • Today betrothal and marriage are done together under the huppah, with the ring, so this case is rare.
  • More common is a woman engaged in today's sense who conceives after the engagement. There is a debate, but the main halacha is that she has the status of a single woman: she is believed to say the child is from her fiancé or another kasher man, and the child is kasher.
