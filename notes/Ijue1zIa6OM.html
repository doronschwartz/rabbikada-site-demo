Topic: Whether one who has not gone to synagogue must stop work when the community accepts Shabbat early

Sources: Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Shabbat, Monday 31 May 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • In summer Shabbat can begin as late as about 9pm in London, and many communities accept it earlier.
  • The Shulhan Arukh rules that once most of a community has accepted Shabbat, the minority must follow and may not do work, even without accepting it themselves.
  • The poskim limit this to a town with one or very few synagogues that all accept Shabbat at the same time, at Mizmor Shir LeYom HaShabbat and Barkhu. Then one at home is bound too.
  • Where there are many communities and some accept Shabbat later, one is not bound by one's own synagogue, even if one attends it regularly, and may work until one accepts Shabbat or all the communities have.
  • So in Golders Green or Hendon, where some communities accept Shabbat only at 9pm, one not at synagogue may work until about then.
  • Even where all the synagogues in one's own area accept early, as in Maida Vale, other London communities within walking distance accept later, so seemingly one may still work.
  • Only in a place with one community, such as a moshav in Israel, or a town like Sefat where the few synagogues all accept together, must everyone follow.

In one line

In a city with many communities like London, one who has not accepted Shabbat may work until the last communities accept it.
