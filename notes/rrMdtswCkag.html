Topic: Whether the Berakhah on Massah after Pesah is Mezonot or HaMosi

Sources: The Gemara (pat haba bekisnin); the Hida

Daily Halacha: Pesah, Monday 9 May 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • On Pesah all agree Massah is HaMosi with Birkat HaMazon, since it is the only bread we have.
  • During the year Sepharadim generally say Mezonot with Al HaMihyah, and Ashkenazim HaMosi with Birkat HaMazon.
  • The Gemara rules Mezonot on pat haba bekisnin. The halacha accepts all three interpretations: a filled pastry, a sweet one, or a crunchy one is Mezonot.
  • Thin, hard Massot are crunchy, so the Sepharadim say Mezonot outside Pesah.
  • The Ashkenazim hold that crunchy foods are Mezonot only because one does not normally make a meal of them, while Massah is normally eaten as the bread of a meal, as Pesah proves. So it is HaMosi all year.
  • The Hida concludes that because of the debate, a God-fearing person should eat Massah only within a meal where he has washed and made HaMosi on bread.
  • Sepharadim who do eat it alone say Mezonot and Al HaMihyah, including straight after Pesah, on Pesah Sheni and for leftover Massah.
  • This applies only to the thin, crunchy Massot. The soft traditional Sepharadi Massah is HaMosi all year round.

Sources quoted

  • The Gemara: pat haba bekisnin is Mezonot.
  • The Hida (Rabbi Hayim Yoseph David Azulai): best to eat Massah only within a bread meal.

In one line

For Sepharadim crunchy Massah is Mezonot after Pesah (soft Massah stays HaMosi), and the ideal is to eat it within a bread meal.
