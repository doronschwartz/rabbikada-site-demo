Topic: Who pays for what a parent needs, and whom to honour first when both ask

Sources: The Gemara; Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Honouring Parents, Monday 11 January 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Kavod includes giving parents food and drink, dressing them, taking them to appointments, anything that helps them.
  • Who pays? If the parent has the means, the child need not pay from his own pocket. If the parent cannot pay and the child can, the child must, taking into account his own family's needs. If neither can, the child need not collect charity for it.
  • If father and mother ask for something at the same moment (one on the phone, the other by text), the first to ask comes first.
  • If the parents are divorced and no longer live together, the child may choose whom to serve first: one of the cases where halacha leaves the choice to the person.
  • If they are married, the Gemara says the father comes first, since the mother also has an obligation to honour her husband. There are caveats, continued in the next note.

In one line

A child pays for a parent's needs only when the parent cannot and the child can; when both parents ask at once, divorced parents leave the child free to choose, while for married parents the Gemara puts the father first.
