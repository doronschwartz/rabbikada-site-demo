Topic: Rain on Sukkot, and the Sepharadi ruling for the first and second nights

Sources: The Torah (the 15th of the month, Pesah and Sukkot, by gezerah shavah); Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Sukkot, Sunday 13 October 2024

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • During Sukkot, rain enough to spoil even a basic dish, even a light drizzle that bothers a person, exempts him from the sukkah. He may eat indoors and need not return if it stops mid-meal.
  • Sitting in the sukkah in light rain is debatable, but the berakhah certainly cannot be said, since there is no obligation.
  • The first night is different: the Hakhamim learnt by gezerah shavah (the 15th of the month, Pesah and Sukkot) that just as one must eat a kezayit of Massah on Pesah night, one must eat a kezayit of bread in the sukkah on the first night of Sukkot. The rest of the festival, one need not eat bread at all.
  • Shulhan Arukh: the rain exemption applies on the first night too, and on the second night. It is ones, beyond our control.
  • But on these nights, if the meal was eaten indoors because of rain and it then stops, the obligation returns: even after Kiddush and the meal, one must go and eat in the sukkah, any time until about midnight.
  • If it rains all night there is nothing to be done.
  • Ashkenazim hold slightly differently; next time.

Sources quoted

  • Shulhan Arukh: rain exempts one from the sukkah even on the first night.

In one line

For Sepharadim, rain exempts even on the first night, but if it stops later that night one must still eat a kezayit of bread in the sukkah.
