Topic: Eating and drinking during the Haggadah, and when to eat the egg

Sources: Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Pesah

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • With Pesah coming in late and a busy Erev Pesah, people may be hungry during the long Haggadah. Before Kiddush one may not eat.
  • Besides the zeroa for the Korban Pesah, the egg recalls the Korban Hagigah offered every festival; an egg is round and represents mourning for the Beit HaMikdash.
  • The Shulhan Arukh permits drinking during the Seder, only not intoxicating drinks that remove focus. Tea, coffee or water after Kiddush is fine, with no berakhah, as the Kiddush wine exempts all drinks.
  • Strictly one may also eat, but not so much as to lose appetite for the massah.
  • Also, karpas is less than a kazayit so that no Borei Nefashot closes the Borei Peri HaAdamah meant to cover the maror. Eating a kazayit during the Haggadah would require Borei Nefashot and create a doubt about the maror.
  • So eat no more than a kazayit unless one cannot otherwise concentrate, such as a firstborn who fasted, who should eat. Then say Borei Nefashot and do not repeat Borei Peri HaAdamah on the maror.
  • Therefore the egg is better eaten during the meal, after the massah; eating a whole egg during the Haggadah raises the same problem.

Sources quoted

  • Shulhan Arukh: drinking is permitted during the Seder, except intoxicating drinks.

In one line

Drink freely after Kiddush, eat less than a kazayit before the massah unless needed to concentrate, and eat the egg during the meal.
