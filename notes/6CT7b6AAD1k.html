Topic: Professional and non-professional work (melekhet uman and melekhet hedyot), with torn clothing as the example

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Sunday 28 September 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • For food and for sorkhei haguf all melakhah is permitted, even melekhet uman. For everything else the distinction between professional and non-professional work applies.
  • A dress or suit torn on Yom Tov is neither food nor the body. It may not be repaired professionally on Hol HaMoed, whether by oneself or by taking it to someone, even to a non-Jew.
  • A temporary fix, such as patching a tear to get through until after Yom Tov, is permitted, by oneself or someone else, but without payment: according to most opinions paying makes it melekhet uman.
  • This applies only if the garment is needed on the moed; if one has another suit, wear that.
  • If the temporary fix is not comfortable to wear and one has nothing else, one may buy new clothing (buying on Hol HaMoed is a later topic).
  • An electrician, plumber or contractor is melekhet uman, permitted for the fridge, lighting or a broken window because they relate to food and the home, but not for other things, which allow only melekhet hedyot. Possible loss is an exception to be discussed.

In one line

Clothing may not be professionally repaired on Hol HaMoed, only patched temporarily without payment when needed for the moed.
