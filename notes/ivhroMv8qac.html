Topic: How mamzerim are created today without a get

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer, Tuesday 2 June 2026

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • A mamzer may marry a giyoret and a ger may marry a mamzeret; the marriage is halachically fine and a rabbi may officiate. But the child of either union remains a mamzer.
  • A mamzer is created by relationships that carry the penalty of karet, commonly adultery or incest, and this is more common than people think.
  • Example one: a couple not very religious marry under an Orthodox Beit Din with kosher witnesses. Years later, no longer part of any Jewish community, they divorce only civilly. She marries another man civilly and has children. With no get she is still married to the first husband, so the children are mamzerim, though there was no ill intent, only an oversight.
  • Example two, more tragic: a couple married under an Orthodox rabbi later join a Reform community, and a Reform rabbi conducts a get. For many reasons it is not a get halachically. She remarries in a Reform synagogue and has children; 20 years later, when they want to marry an Orthodox partner, an Orthodox body cannot allow it, since they are mamzerim.
  • He is aware that many Reform rabbis send couples who married in an Orthodox setting to an Orthodox Beit Din for the get, but this does not always happen, especially in America.
  • Such a child can marry only a fellow mamzer or a giyoret, and the children remain mamzerim, so the situation perpetuates.
  • There are three possible solutions, to be explained in the coming classes.

In one line

A civil divorce or a Reform get is not a get, so children of a second marriage are mamzerim even when no one intended wrong.
