The halacha

  • The Shulhan Arukh records only four things for the Three Weeks themselves, until Rosh Hodesh.
  • First, some fast every day from the 17th of Tammuz, a custom the Tur brings from the Rishonim. It is very hard and few do it; he is unsure it is recommended today.
  • Second, some (Ashkenazi Rishonim) avoid meat the whole Three Weeks, because the daily Tamid ceased on the 17th of Tammuz. By the law of the Gemara meat is forbidden only at the final meal after midday on Erev Tisha BeAv. Neither of these two is a communal custom; individuals may keep them.
  • Third, "one must be careful" to avoid danger, from Midrash Eikhah and the Rishonim: his examples are not walking alone at dangerous times and teachers not hitting students. One must always be careful, but extra vigilance is expected now.
  • This does not forbid a holiday. Cutting down on leisure begins only on Rosh Hodesh, so leisure is permitted until then, provided it is not dangerous.
  • Fourth, "it is good" not to say Shehiyanu, to be discussed tomorrow.
  • There is nothing in the Shulhan Arukh about music or dancing in the Three Weeks. All these are customs; for Sepharadim everything else is permitted, and for Ashkenazim too by the law of the Gemara, their other practices being custom.
