Topic: Children born after a get from one of the two men: the Rambam and Tosafot, and a question to Rav Moshe Feinstein

Sources: The Mishnah and Gemara; the Rambam; Tosafot; Shulhan Arukh; Rav Moshe Feinstein

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer, Wednesday 27 May 2026

The halacha

  • Accepted by all: if she returns to man one without a get from man two, the children are rabbinic mamzerim, since rabbinically she is still married to man two.
  • The dispute: she receives a get from man two and returns to man one (no new marriage is needed, as they were always married from the Torah), or man one gives her a get and she stays with man two. What of children born after that get?
  • The simple reading of the Rambam: in both cases the children are kasher. Children with man two before the get would of course be mamzerim from the Torah.
  • Tosafot: in both cases the children are mamzerim miderabbanan, as a penalty. Because we were so lenient in letting her remarry on flimsy evidence, she must bear the consequences, including that children with either man are penalised.
  • The Shulhan Arukh's ruling here is not entirely clear.
  • A true case put to Rav Moshe Feinstein in 1973, almost 30 years after the Holocaust: a woman whose husband was taken in the Holocaust said she received a heter from a rabbi to remarry, and married man two. Her first husband then turned out to have survived the camps and gave her a get. She stayed with man two and had children with him after the get. The answer follows next time.

Sources quoted

In one line

After a get from one man, the Rambam holds children with the other are kasher, while Tosafot penalises them as rabbinic mamzerim.
