Topic: Whether Sepharadi children studying away are covered by their parents' Hanukkah lighting

Sources: The Gemara ("Ner ish uveito"); Hakham Ovadiah Yoseph; Hakham Ben Sion Abba Shaul

Daily Halacha: Hanukkah

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • For Ashkenazim each person lights, so a child away lights his own Menorah. The question is for Sepharadim, where one Menorah covers the household.
  • A child who is still financially dependent on the parents, even somewhat (they pay the fees, he comes home regularly to eat or when unwell), is "samukh al shulhan aviv" and relies on the parents' berakhah and lighting.
  • He may light if he wishes, but without a berakhah, which would be in vain.
  • The consensus of the Sepharadi poskim is that intending not to be included in the household does not help, mainly because he cannot know whether his father has already lit.
  • Hakham Ovadiah holds this applies even across time zones, such as Israel two hours ahead, or a gap of five to seven hours with America; Hakham Ben Sion Abba Shaul was equivocal.
  • When the child is ahead in time and lights at the beginning of his night, before his parents' time has come, he has not yet been covered, so he may light with a berakhah if he wishes; otherwise he is covered by their later lighting.
  • Wherever he cannot make a berakhah, he may still light without one.

In one line

A dependent Sepharadi child away from home relies on his parents' lighting, but may light with a berakhah when he is ahead of them in time.
