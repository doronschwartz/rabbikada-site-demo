Topic: May one marry the Bene Israel, given the concern of mamzerut?

Sources: The Gemara on the Kutim; Rav Ovadiah Yoseph; Rav Herzog; Rav Nissim; Rav Rozovsky; the Beth Din HaGadol; the Sis Eliezer (Rav Eliezer Waldenberg)

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer, Thursday 12 March 2026

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Gemara forbids marrying a Kuti, the Samaritans, later the Karaites, who rejected the Oral Torah. Two key reasons given: they married but did not divorce properly, raising a concern of mamzerut; and invalid people such as servants became mixed into them.
  • If that is the reason, the Bene Israel, who also married without divorce, would seemingly be forbidden too.
  • But the main reason may be a gezerah, a fixed decree to keep a barrier from a group that rejected the Oral Torah. Then it applies only to such groups, not to the Bene Israel. This was the view of many poskim.
  • What of possible mamzerim among the Bene Israel themselves? The poskim apply the Gemara's principle that a family once mixed into Am Yisrael (mishpahah shenitmeah) is treated as kasher.
  • So most poskim allowed the Bene Israel to marry into Am Yisrael: Rav Ovadiah Yoseph, Rav Herzog, Rav Nissim, Rav Rozovsky (Rosh Yeshiva of Ponevezh, also a Dayan), and the Beth Din HaGadol.
  • The Sis Eliezer, Rav Eliezer Waldenberg, disagreed because of mamzerut, but the ruling of the Rabbanut in Israel is that the Bene Israel are Jewish and may marry in.

In one line

Most poskim and the Rabbanut rule that the Bene Israel are Jewish and may marry into Am Yisrael, though the Sis Eliezer disagreed.
