Topic: Where to light when staying in a hotel or with family during Hanukkah

Sources: The Gemara ("Ner ish uveito"); Hakham Ovadiah Yoseph

Daily Halacha: Hanukkah

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The misvah is "ner ish uveito", on the person and his house. The question is what counts as one's house when away. Ashkenazim and Sepharadim will differ, since for Ashkenazim each person lights and for Sepharadim it is one Menorah per household.
  • The general consensus of the poskim, though there is disagreement, is that one's house is where one sleeps that night.
  • A couple without children at home staying one night in a hotel light in the hotel (where allowed; where in a hotel to light is a separate question), even arriving at 7 pm, well after the time.
  • According to Hakham Ovadiah, a Sepharadi couple staying with parents or parents-in-law over Shabbat need not light: that is their home, and they fulfil the misvah with their hosts. They need not appoint a shaliah to light at their own home.
  • On Mosei Shabbat, even though the hosts may light after Havdalah, the couple light again in their own home, since they sleep there.
  • Ashkenazim staying with family light their own Menorah there with a berakhah on Friday, and at home on Mosei Shabbat.
  • This seems to be the mainstream halacha, though there are numerous opinions.

In one line

When moving over Hanukkah, one's home for lighting is where one sleeps that night.
