Topic: The two berakhot at Mosi Massah, which massot to hold, and how much to eat

Sources: The Rishonim

Daily Halacha: Pesah, Friday 31 March 2023

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • After the Haggadah comes the second cup. Sepharadim say no berakhah on it, relying on the first cup's berakhah; Ashkenazim say Borei Peri HaGefen on each of the four cups.
  • Then Rohsah, washing the hands, and Mosi Massah with two berakhot: Hamosi Lehem Min HaAres, since massah counts as bread, and "al akhilat massah".
  • For Hamosi, which begins the meal, one holds all three massot, since lehem mishneh needs two whole ones (the top and bottom; the middle one was broken at Yahas).
  • Then one puts down the bottom massah, kept for Korekh, and holds only the top and middle massot for "al akhilat massah", since those are eaten from now.
  • From the Torah, one kazayit of massah fulfils the misvah. The Rishonim debate whether it should come from the whole top massah or the broken middle one, the bread of poverty, so the halacha is to eat a kazayit from each.
  • This applies only to the Baal HaBayit, who has the three massot. Everyone else needs just one kazayit at this point, though eating more adds to the misvah.

In one line

Hold all three massot for Hamosi and the top two for "al akhilat massah"; the leader eats a kazayit from each of those, everyone else one kazayit.
