Topic: Whether sunbathing to get a tan raises issues of healing or colouring on Shabbat

Sources: Rav Weiss, head of the Manchester Beth Din

Daily Halacha: Shabbat, Thursday 17 June 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Refuah: there is a broad prohibition on certain medical acts on Shabbat, but whatever healthy people normally do is permitted, like a hot water bottle for a cramp. Sunbathing is normal, so even for health reasons it raises no issue.
  • Sovea (dyeing) is one of the 39 melakhot, which is why some makeup is forbidden. A tan acquired just by walking in the sun is unintended and indirect and no problem.
  • Sitting in the sun deliberately to tan: Rav Weiss, head of the Manchester Beth Din about 40 years ago, forbade it as colouring.
  • Many disagreed: one does no act but places oneself where the sun acts, which is indirect (gerama) and gradual, with no immediate effect. Also natural tanning may differ from the artificial colouring the Hakhamim forbade.
  • So on purely halachic grounds it is very hard to forbid sunbathing, even intending to tan.
  • But it is not in the spirit of Shabbat to spend its hours tanning; that is not what Shabbat was given for.

In one line

Sunbathing on Shabbat is probably not halachically forbidden, but deliberately spending Shabbat tanning is against its spirit.
