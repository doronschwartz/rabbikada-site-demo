Topic: The berakhot on good news and on bad news

Sources: The Gemara; the Mishnah; the Rambam; Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Peculiar Berachot, Thursday 27 February 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Gemara, codified by the Rambam and the Shulhan Arukh: on hearing exceptionally good news, one says Shehiyanu if it is good for oneself alone, and HaTov VeHaMetiv if it is good for more than one person.
  • A person whose business makes him an extraordinary sum says Shehiyanu; partners whose startup soars say HaTov VeHaMetiv, one saying it for the other, who answers Amen. Likewise a lottery win: single, Shehiyanu; a couple, HaTov VeHaMetiv.
  • It is rarely practised because good news is hard to define and subjective, but it should be said, a beautiful chance to thank HaKadosh Barukh Hu.
  • For bad news there is one berakhah, whether it affects one or many: Dayan HaEmet. It is known from the death of a relative, but applies to any bad tidings that leave a person very low, such as losing one's job and fearing for one's livelihood.
  • Its purpose is to restore faith that HaKadosh Barukh Hu is the true judge; there is a reason, perhaps good one will see, and even if not, as the Mishnah teaches, it is from Him.
  • On hearing news of the gravity of 7 October it would certainly have been appropriate to say Dayan HaEmet.

In one line

Good news brings Shehiyanu or HaTov VeHaMetiv, bad news Dayan HaEmet, recognising Hashem in every moment of life.
