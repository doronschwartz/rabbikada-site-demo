Topic: The second heter: melakhah for a misvah, and for a misvah that will pass

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Tuesday 11 November 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • For a misvah, melekhet hedyot is permitted on Hol HaMoed even if it is not for the needs of the festival.
  • Example: a baby born on the first days of Sukkot or Pesah whose berit milah falls on Isru Hag. Shopping on Hol HaMoed for use after the festival is normally forbidden, but since it is for a misvah, one may order the food and prepare everything else for the berit as melekhet hedyot.
  • A pasul Sefer Torah that is not needed during the festival: adding a missing letter is professional work for a sofer, melekhet uman, so not permitted. Erasing an extra letter is not professional, so it may be done for the misvah.
  • For a misvah that will pass if not done now (misvah overet), even melekhet uman is permitted: a Sefer Torah missing a letter that is the community's only one, or needed for Simhat Torah when there are not enough.
  • Similarly one who did not build his sukkah before the festival may build it on Hol HaMoed, though building is melekhet uman, since Sukkot lasts only a few days.
  • The rationale returns to davar haaved: the misvah would otherwise be lost.

In one line

Melakhah for a misvah is permitted on Hol HaMoed if it is non-professional, and even professional work is allowed for a misvah that would otherwise pass.
