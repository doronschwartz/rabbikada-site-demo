The points

  • The series resumes in Heshvan 5787 (end of 2026). Even HaEzer, one of the four sections of the Shulhan Arukh, deals with marriage, divorce and relationships.
  • Siman 1: the framework of marriage; the misvah of Peru Urevu and the secondary misvah to have children beyond a boy and a girl; the obligations of men and women; the age of marriage; contraception; and polygamy in Jewish history and practice to this day.
  • Siman 2: Jewish identity. Strictly a person seems to be believed to say he or she is Jewish, but in practice today proofs are needed. Also Jews from countries of doubtful status, such as India, Ethiopia, Spain and Portugal, and character traits to check before marriage.
  • Siman 3: whether a man is believed to say he is a Kohen, to be called up, say Birkat Kohanim or do Pidyon HaBen, and whether Kohanim today are fully fledged Kohanim, with contemporary Beit Din practice.
  • Siman 4: a Jewish woman with a non-Jewish man, and mainly mamzerut: the definition, the Torah, rabbinic, doubtful and half mamzer, and the remedies. Sadly these issues still arise today.
  • Also modern questions linked to mamzerut: artificial insemination, IVF, egg donation and surrogacy.
  • All classes are available for review, and after every chapter or two there will be a poll on what was learned.
