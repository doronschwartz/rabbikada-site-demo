Topic: A family that comes to Israel unsure whether it will stay: one day or two?

Sources: Hakham Ovadiah Yoseph; Rav Mordechai Eliyahu; Rav Shelomoh Zalman Auerbach; the Hakham Svi

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Tuesday 15 July 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Hakham Ovadiah's ruling that single students open to staying in Israel keep one day is shared by other Sepharadi Hakhamim, including Rav Mordechai Eliyahu, so there is plenty to rely on for yeshivah students.
  • A family or couple that comes to Israel without yet making aliyah, to see whether staying is feasible, is different. Hakham Ovadiah holds they keep two days until they decide, since they still have their main home abroad and retain the status of the place they came from.
  • A single student living with family has no home of his own abroad, so there is more room for flexibility.
  • It follows that a single person with his own flat abroad who goes to study for a few months does not keep one day merely by being open to staying: he too retains his former status until he decides firmly.
  • Rav Shelomoh Zalman Auerbach and others hold that even a family with a home abroad, unsure but open to staying, may keep one day, relying on the view of the Hakham Svi together with the uncertainty.
  • These are modern questions arising from frequent travel, which reached the poskim in the last couple of hundred years. It is a grey area, hard to rule on bindingly from the Talmud, Rambam and Shulhan Arukh.

Sources quoted

  • Hakham Ovadiah Yoseph: a family unsure of staying keeps two days; a single student open to staying keeps one.
  • Rav Mordechai Eliyahu and other Sepharadi Hakhamim: agree regarding students.
  • Rav Shelomoh Zalman Auerbach: even an unsure family may keep one day.

In one line

A single student open to staying has plenty to rely on to keep one day, while for an undecided family Hakham Ovadiah requires two and others allow one.
