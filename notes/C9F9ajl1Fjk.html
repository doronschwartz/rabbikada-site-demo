Topic: Closing laws of Siman 2: age gaps, marrying with intent to divorce, wives in two countries

Sources: The Gemara; Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Even HaEzer, Tuesday 31 March 2026

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • The Gemara and Shulhan Arukh say there should be no unreasonable age gap in marriage: a young man should not marry an elderly woman, nor an old man a very young girl, since they will not satisfy one another and it will lead to promiscuity.
  • It is forbidden to marry a woman intending to divorce her after a short time while she expects a lasting marriage. The Gemara cites the verse warning not to devise harm against one's fellow while he dwells trustingly with you.
  • It is permitted if he tells her at the outset that it is for a week or a month, since both consent.
  • For example, marrying to obtain a visa or residence in England, intending to divorce afterwards without telling her and without her consent, would be forbidden.
  • Not relevant today: one should not marry one wife in one country and another elsewhere, lest their children unknowingly marry each other.
  • After Pesah, Siman 3 deals with the Kohen: who is a Kohen and whether one who claims to be one is believed.

In one line

Avoid large age gaps, never marry intending a hidden divorce, and keep marriages where children will know each other.
