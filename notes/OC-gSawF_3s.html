Topic: Haircuts on Rosh Hodesh Iyar after lockdown, and whether the Omer haircut restriction applies to women and children

Sources: Shulhan Arukh; the Gemara; the laws of mourning; the poskim

Daily Halacha: Sefirat HaOmer, Tuesday 13 April 2021

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Recap: the Geonic custom forbids weddings and haircuts for about 34 days; the Shulhan Arukh rejects haircuts on Rosh Hodesh Iyar. The Gemara permits one released from prison to have a haircut even on Hol HaMoed, which is more severe.
  • Similarly, a mourner unable to cut his hair before Pesah because of the thirty days may cut it in the Omer, since the restriction is for one who could have done so before Pesah.
  • So this year: a man particular to have haircuts only at a barber, who could not before Pesah because barbers were closed, may have one on Rosh Hodesh (Monday or Tuesday). There is a double doubt (sefek sefekah): some permit Rosh Hodesh every year, and he could not cut before through no fault of his own; and it is only a custom.
  • One who sometimes cuts his own hair had the opportunity and cannot rely on this.
  • Women: after shivah a woman may cut her hair during shloshim, when men may not; the Omer cannot be stricter than shloshim, so women may cut their hair.
  • Children: we do not train children in mourning (avelut), even real mourning, all the more so a custom, so children may have haircuts.

In one line

Men may not cut their hair in the Omer, even on Rosh Hodesh, except this year for those kept from a barber by lockdown; women and children may.
