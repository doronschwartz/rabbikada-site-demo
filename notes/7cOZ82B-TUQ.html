Topic: Whether papaya is Boreh Peri HaEs or Boreh Peri HaAdamah

Sources: The Gemara, the Rishonim and Shulhan Arukh; the Ben Ish Hai; the Radbaz

Daily Halacha: Berachot, Thursday 18 January 2024

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Papaya is a tropical fruit, so it was the Sepharadi poskim, above all the Ben Ish Hai, who first worked through its berakhah. Some say HaEs and some HaAdamah.
  • The halachic line between tree and vegetable is not the English one: a strawberry is a fruit, yet we say HaAdamah.
  • From the Gemara, Rishonim and Shulhan Arukh, a tree is one whose stem survives the year; if the plant dies back in winter and regrows from the roots it is a bush, HaAdamah. So bananas and pineapple are HaAdamah.
  • The Ben Ish Hai first argued papaya is HaEs, since its stem survives the winter, but concluded firmly it is HaAdamah because of a further condition: whatever produces fruit within a year of planting is a vegetable.
  • His proof is the aubergine. All aubergines come from the plant's first two or three years, raising a question of orlah, but the Radbaz, 500 years ago in Egypt, ruled it a vegetable, since it fruits within its first year, so orlah does not apply.
  • On the Radbaz's definition papaya, which fruits in its first year, is HaAdamah. This is the consensus of most poskim, especially the Sepharadi poskim who lived where it grows, and certainly the minhag of Sepharadim.

In one line

Because papaya fruits within a year of planting, Sepharadim say Boreh Peri HaAdamah on it.
