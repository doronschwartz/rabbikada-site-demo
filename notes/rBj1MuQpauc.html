Topic: Whether one may take pills before Yom Kippur to make the fast easier

Sources: The Gemara; Hakham Ovadiah

Daily Halacha: Yom Kippur, Monday 3 October 2022

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Whether such pills work is beyond the Rabbi's expertise; the question assumes they do.
  • The Torah commands affliction on Yom Kippur, but the Gemara says this does not mean every kind of suffering, such as sitting in the hot sun. The Hakhamim understood the affliction to be refraining from eating and drinking.
  • The Misvah to eat on Erev Yom Kippur shows Hashem's love: He wants us well fed so as to fast properly the next day. So preparing before the fast to ease its pain is a recognized concept.
  • On this basis some held there is no problem with the pills, no different from eating a lot beforehand. Hakham Ovadiah rules that anything done on Erev Yom Kippur to ease the fast is permitted.
  • He adds that it is not necessarily the right thing: one who can fast properly without them should preferably not take them.
  • One who is a very bad faster, or will not be able to concentrate and will suffer, may take them before Yom Kippur.

Sources quoted

  • The Gemara: the affliction of Yom Kippur is not, for example, sitting in the sun.
  • Hakham Ovadiah: anything done on Erev Yom Kippur to ease the fast is permitted, though better avoided if not needed.

In one line

Taking such pills before Yom Kippur is permitted, advisable for a poor faster, and better avoided by those who manage without.
