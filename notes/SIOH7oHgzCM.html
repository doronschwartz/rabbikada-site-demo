Topic: Showering on Yom Tov with cold water and with hot water heated before Yom Tov

Sources: The Rishonim; Tosafot; the Rambam; Shulhan Arukh

Daily Halacha: Moadim, Thursday 12 June 2025

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The halacha

  • Cold water on Yom Tov, with a manual shower and no electricity, has the same status as Shabbat: for Sepharadim it is outright permitted; Ashkenazim generally refrain, but may shower when uncomfortable (mistaer). Details of drying come next time.
  • One may not bathe or shower in water heated on Yom Tov. The Rishonim differ on why: Tosafot, because bathing the whole body was not a universal need in the time of the Hakhamim, unlike today; the Rambam, because of a gezerah over people who pretended, as on Shabbat.
  • The difference from Shabbat: according to the Rambam and Shulhan Arukh, one may shower at home on Yom Tov in hot water heated before Yom Tov, though not in a public bathhouse or spa.
  • Crucially, not with a combi boiler, which is forbidden according to all opinions, since opening even a manual tap ignites a new fire.
  • With a conventional boiler heated before Yom Tov, the boiler retains that heat and the water keeps its status of being heated beforehand, so it may be used.
  • One must make sure no new water enters, since it would be heated on Yom Tov: if the shower is to be used more than once, close the cold water inlet valve, so all the water used was heated before Yom Tov.
  • That is the Sepharadi practice following the Shulhan Arukh. Ashkenazim follow Tosafot that even water heated before Yom Tov may not be used for a hot shower.
