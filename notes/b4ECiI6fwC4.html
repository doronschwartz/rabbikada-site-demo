Topic: The meaning of "leshev besukkat oro shel livyatan"

Sources: The Gemara; the Rama; the Or HaHayyim; Bereshit ("kotnot or")

Daily Halacha: Sukkot, Monday 21 October 2024

These notes are based on an automatic transcript of the recording.

The points

  • Sepharadim add to Birkat HaMazon on Sukkot "Harahaman hu yizakenu leshev besukkat oro shel livyatan", to sit in the sukkah of the Leviathan's skin. The Gemara links the Leviathan, a huge fish, to the end of days and Olam HaBa.
  • Ashkenazim do not say it in Birkat HaMazon, but the Rama brings that on leaving the sukkah at the end they say "may it be Your will that as we sat in this sukkah, we sit in the sukkah of the Leviathan's skin".
  • The poskim ask: sekhakh must grow from the ground, so a sekhakh of skin is invalid. Saying it means only the walls is not the simple meaning; saying it is just protection loses the link to Sukkot.
  • He explains, based on the Or HaHayyim: all year we ask for a "sukkat shalom". Body and soul pull against each other, and balancing them is our struggle.
  • On Sukkot there is peace between them: eating, drinking and sleeping in the sukkah become service of HaShem, with a berakhah.
  • The fish is the most elevated creature: an animal needs two simanim cut and a bird one, but a fish needs no shehitah, and it never leaves the water it is born in. The Leviathan is the greatest fish.
  • "Or", skin, recalls the kotnot or given to Adam after the sin, representing our material side.
  • So the sukkah of the Leviathan's skin is not a physical sukkah but the joining of spiritual and physical, which we taste on Sukkot and pray to see fully in the World to Come.

In one line

The sukkah of the Leviathan's skin is the future unity of body and soul that the sukkah gives us a taste of now.
